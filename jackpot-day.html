What Is 7 Brew Jackpot Day?

Jackpot Day is 7 Brew's monthly promotion held on the 7th of every month — a nod to the "7" in the brand name. Each month features a different deal: past offers have included two medium drinks for $9, free merchandise like bag charms and branded accessories, and limited-edition flavors. The September 2026 Jackpot Day featured free 7 Brew Doh (branded play dough) with qualifying purchases.

Deals are typically available all day from open to close at participating stands, but popular offers can run out of promotional items early. The best strategy is to arrive in the morning and order through the 7 Brew app to speed up your visit. Jackpot Day purchases still earn rewards points, so you stack the monthly deal on top of your regular earnings.

How to Prepare for Jackpot Day

Follow 7 Brew's official social media accounts for early announcements — the deal for each month is usually revealed a few days before the 7th. Plan your order in advance using the full menu with prices and the calorie calculator. If the deal is a BOGO or multi-drink promotion, bring a friend or order for the next day — breve drinks keep well refrigerated for up to 24 hours.

Not sure what to order? Check the best drinks ranking for our top picks, or try a secret menu creation you have been curious about. Compare cup sizes to decide how much to order, and explore sugar-free syrup options if you want to keep it lighter. Find your nearest stand on the locations page.

When Is the Next Jackpot Day Date?

The next jackpot day date is October 7, 2026. Jackpot Day is a monthly 7th promotion — it falls on the 7th of every month, all year long. Mark your calendar and follow 7 Brew on Instagram or check the app for the specific deal each month. Past promotions have typically been announced 1–3 days before the 7th.

What Is the Deal Exactly? Free Drink Deal BOGO & More

Each Jackpot Day features a different 7 Brew specials offers — the deal changes every month. Past promotions have included a free drink deal BOGO (buy one get one), discounted combos like two mediums for , free branded merchandise, and limited-edition seasonal flavors. The exact offer varies, so there is no single "Jackpot Day deal." To find out how to get free drink offers or discounts, check the 7 Brew app or social media the week of the 7th.

How to Participate in Jackpot Day

Participation is simple — just visit any 7 Brew stand on the 7th of the month. For most offers, no app required participation or special code is needed; the deal is applied at the window automatically. However, some promotions may require you to show the app or mention the deal. Here is the step-by-step:

  1. Check the 7 Brew app or Instagram for that month's specific Jackpot Day offer
  2. Visit any participating 7 Brew stand on the 7th
  3. Order as normal — mention "Jackpot Day" if prompted or show the app deal
  4. The discount or freebie is applied at checkout

Is Jackpot Day at Every Location Nationwide?

Yes — Jackpot Day runs at every location nationwide across all 700+ 7 Brew stands. Whether you are visiting a location in Arkansas, Texas, Florida, or any other state, the monthly 7th promotion applies. Occasionally a brand-new stand in its first week may not participate, but this is rare. Use the location finder to confirm your nearest stand.
