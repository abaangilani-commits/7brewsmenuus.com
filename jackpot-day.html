What Is 7 Brew Jackpot Day?

Jackpot Day is 7 Brew's monthly promotion held on the 7th of every month — a nod to the "7" in the brand name. Each month features a different deal: past offers have included two medium drinks for $9, free merchandise like bag charms and branded accessories, and limited-edition flavors. The September 2026 Jackpot Day featured free 7 Brew Doh (branded play dough) with qualifying purchases.

Deals run from 7 a.m. to 7 p.m. at participating stands, but popular offers can run out of promotional items early. Drinks you buy on Jackpot Day still earn the usual rewards points, so the deal stacks on top of your regular earnings.

How to Participate in Jackpot Day

Participation is simple — just visit any 7 Brew stand on the 7th of the month. For most offers, no app required participation or special code is needed; the deal is applied at the window automatically. No official announcement so far has required the app, a code, or a rewards account. Here is the step-by-step:

  1. Check the 7 Brew app or Instagram for that month's specific Jackpot Day offer
  2. Visit any participating 7 Brew stand on the 7th
  3. Order as normal. The offer is applied at the window.
  4. The discount or freebie is applied at checkout

When is the next 7 Brew Jackpot Day?

What time does Jackpot Day start and end?
