7 Brew Energy Drinks: Flavors, Caffeine & Prices (2026)

7 Brew Energy drinks cost $4.50 for a Small (16 oz), $5.25 for a Medium (24 oz), and $6.25 for a Large (32 oz). Powered by 7 Brew's proprietary carbonated energy formula rather than Red Bull, caffeine ranges from 160mg in a Small up to 320mg in a Large. Top official flavors include Heatwave, Ocean Breeze, and Pixie Stick. A zero-sugar canned base is available at every stand. Beverage Formulation

Proprietary 7 Energy vs. Red Bull and Monster: What Sets It Apart?

Many first-time visitors assume drive-thru coffee stands simply pour canned Red Bull over ice with syrup pumps. 7 Brew commissioned its own proprietary canned energy beverage base, known inside the stand as 7 Energy. This formulation was engineered around three specific physical beverage dynamics:

1. Anti-Foaming Syrup Solubility

Standard commercial energy drinks like Monster foam vigorously when dense sugar or sugar-free syrups are added. The 7 Energy base has a tailored carbonation profile and lower surface tension, allowing syrups to dissolve quickly without aggressive fizz blowouts over the cup rim.

2. Ice Melt Buffer & Citric Acidity

Standard 8.4 oz Red Bull cans taste watery after sitting on ice for 15 minutes. 7 Energy uses a concentrated citric and malic acid ratio that stays bright, tangy, and sharp throughout the life of a 32-ounce large drink on hot summer days.

3. Clean B-Complex Energy Delivery

Formulated with 100% daily values of Vitamin B6, Vitamin B12, niacinamide, and 1,000 mg taurine per 16 oz equivalent, 7 Energy delivers steady physical energy without the synthetic medicinal aftertaste found in canned retail competitors.

7 Energy Ingredients: What's in the Proprietary Blend? (Energy Drink Ingredients)

Packed with B vitamins taurine and natural caffeine, unlike canned energy drinks, The 7 Energy proprietary blend is 7 Brew's signature energy base, mixed fresh at the drive-thru window using a house-made base — making it a true drive-thru energy drink that delivers a sustained energy boost crash-free formula.

The combination of caffeine, guarana, and taurine with B vitamins is designed for sustained energy delivery rather than a sharp spike and crash. The guarana provides a slower-release caffeine source that extends the energy curve beyond what caffeine alone delivers. This makes 7 Energy a viable pre-workout alternative for moderate exercise — though serious athletes should note that caffeine content varies by size and is not as precisely dosed as dedicated pre-workout supplements.

Is 7 Energy Carbonated?

Handmade drive-thru cups are non-carbonated (still). The ready-to-drink 7 Energy cans sold at retailers like Walmart are lightly carbonated — understanding the carbonated non-carbonated difference matters when choosing your order. If you want a sparkling energy-style drink at the drive-thru, ask for a 7 Fizz + Energy combo — the Italian soda base adds carbonation while the 7 Energy base adds caffeine. This fizz energy hybrid is one of the most underrated orders on the menu.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions: 7 Brew Energy Drinks

Practical answers regarding caffeine counts, canned bases, and sugar-free customization.

How much caffeine is in a 7 Brew Energy drink? +

A Small (16 oz) 7 Energy contains about 160mg of caffeine. A Medium (24 oz) contains roughly 240mg, and a Large (32 oz) delivers 320mg of caffeine. That is equal to two to four 8.4-ounce cans of standard Red Bull.

Does 7 Brew use Red Bull or their own energy base? +

7 Brew uses their own proprietary canned energy drink formula called 7 Energy. It is formulated specifically to mix cleanly with fruit syrups without foaming over. Learn more about official canned beverages on the official 7 Brew website (7brew.com) ↗.

Can you get sugar-free 7 Energy at 7 Brew? +

Yes. 7 Brew stocks a Sugar-Free 7 Energy base that contains zero sugar and around 10 to 15 calories. You can combine it with any of their 12 sugar-free Torani fruit syrups.

Can you buy unopened cans of 7 Energy to take home? +

Yes. Most stands sell individual unopened 12-ounce slim cans of Regular and Sugar-Free 7 Energy for around $2.50 to $3.00, allowing you to stock your home refrigerator.

Are 7 Energy drinks carbonated or flat? +

7 Energy is crisp and carbonated. Baristas pour the canned sparkling energy soda directly over fresh ice and syrups, creating a fizzy, refreshing beverage.
