About 7 Brew in Tampa, Florida

Tampa's 7 Brew drive-thru on West Hillsborough Avenue opened in 2025 as part of the chain's big Florida expansion push. Florida now hosts nearly 50 7 Brew locations, and the Tampa stand brings the brand's breve-style espresso drinks, energy drinks, and Italian sodas to the Hillsborough County market. Founded in Rogers, Arkansas in 2017, 7 Brew has grown to more than 777 stands across 35-plus states.

The Tampa stand serves the complete 7 Brew menu, built around espresso pulled over steamed half-and-half — the breve base that gives every drink a richer, creamier texture than standard lattes. In Tampa's subtropical climate, iced and blended drinks are the top sellers. Try the Blondie over ice, a frozen Smooth 7, or one of the 7 Energy drinks for a non-coffee caffeine boost with B-vitamins.

New to 7 Brew? Browse the best drinks guide for ranked recommendations, or ask the barista about the secret menu — dozens of custom combinations popularized on TikTok. Every order can be tailored with sugar-free syrups and alternative milks at no extra charge. Use the calorie calculator to compare sizes and customize your macros.

The Tampa location typically opens at 5:30 AM and closes at 10:00 PM daily, seven days a week. Compare cup sizes and caffeine content for the 16 oz, 24 oz, and 32 oz options, and download the 7 Brew app to earn rewards with every purchase.
