What Is 7 Brew? Drinks, Locations & Official Services

Get a quick introduction to the 7 Brew drive-thru brand and find the right destination for menus, locations, rewards and official help.

Understand the brand

7 Brew is a drive-thru beverage brand that began in Northwest Arkansas in 2017. Its menu includes coffee, Energy, Fizz, tea, matcha, lemonade, smoothies and shakes. Official brand background.

Where to start

Use the menu to browse drinks, the first-order guide to compare flavors, or the location directory to find a stand.

Company History Origin Story: How 7 Brew Started

7 Brew was founded Rogers Arkansas 2017 by Ron Jori Crume founders who wanted to reinvent the drive-thru coffee chain experience. What began as a single stand in Northwest Arkansas quickly became a regional sensation thanks to fast service drive-thru only convenience, colorful drinks, and an energetic brand identity culture that set it apart from traditional cafes.

The Crumes built 7 Brew around a simple idea: serve high-quality breve-style specialty drinks at drive-thru speed without sacrificing flavor or fun. Every drink is made to order with fresh ingredients, and the menu spans coffee, energy, fizz, lemonade, smoothies, shakes, and more — making 7 Brew a true coffee shop alternative for people who want variety beyond standard drip coffee.

Growth Timeline: From One Stand to 900 Locations Nationwide

7 Brew's franchise expansion growth has been one of the fastest in the drive-thru coffee industry. Here is how the brand scaled:

  • 2017: First stand opens in Rogers, Arkansas
  • 2019: Expands beyond Arkansas with 20+ locations
  • 2021: Reaches 100 stands across the South and Midwest
  • 2023: Crosses 300 locations, enters new states rapidly
  • 2024: Surpasses 900 stands with coast-to-coast presence
  • 2025–2026: Now operating 900 stands across 38 states (September 2026), with new stands opening weekly
  • August 2026: Launches canned coffees and energy drinks at Walmart — 8 flavors across 4,400+ stores nationwide

The franchise model allows local entrepreneurs to open and operate their own 7 Brew stands while maintaining the brand's signature speed, quality, and atmosphere. This rapid expansion has made 7 Brew one of the fastest-growing drive-thru coffee brands in the United States.

What Makes 7 Brew Different?

Several things set 7 Brew apart from competitors like Starbucks, Dutch Bros, and Scooter's Coffee. First, it is drive-thru only — there are no indoor seats, lobbies, or walk-up windows at most locations. This keeps the line moving and wait times short, typically under three minutes. Second, the menu is built around breve-style drinks made with half-and-half as the default milk, giving every coffee drink a rich, creamy texture. Third, the energy drink lineup (7 Energy) is a proprietary blend you cannot get anywhere else — a genuine coffee shop alternative for people who prefer energy drinks over espresso.

7 Brew has also gained mainstream visibility through its Dude Perfect partnership and social media presence, with colorful drink photos and creative seasonal offerings regularly going viral on TikTok and Instagram. The brand projects a youthful, high-energy brand identity culture that appeals to a broad audience from college students to busy parents.

What Is a Breve? 7 Brew's Signature Style Explained

Breve is the default milk style behind most 7 Brew drinks, and it's the detail that confuses a lot of first-timers. A traditional latte is made with steamed milk; a breve swaps that milk for steamed half-and-half, giving the drink a noticeably richer, creamier, slightly sweeter mouthfeel than a standard latte made with whole milk. When you order a named 7 Brew drink like the Blondie or Brunette without specifying a milk, half-and-half breve is what you'll get by default.

Is breve healthier than a latte? Generally, no — half-and-half is roughly half cream, so a breve carries more fat and more calories per ounce than the same drink made with whole milk, and considerably more than one made with a lower-fat option. If you want the flavor without the extra richness, ask for oat, almond or skim milk in place of half-and-half — every 7 Brew drink can be made with a standard latte-style milk instead of breve at no extra charge. See our milk and syrup customization guide for the full list of swaps, or the nutrition calculator to compare calories by milk choice.

How Did 7 Brew Get Its Name?

The "7" in 7 Brew represents the idea of being open and available seven days a week, embodying the brand's commitment to convenience and consistency. The name is short, memorable, and reflects the company's focus on serving every community, every day. Standard hours at most locations are 5:30 AM to 10:00 PM, seven days a week — true to the name.

Brand Partnerships: Dude Perfect & American Idol

7 Brew's marketing has leaned heavily into sports and entertainment tie-ins. In September 2025 the brand announced a partnership with the trick-shot group Dude Perfect, and the collaboration launched its own “Brewed Perfect” line of drinks in summer 2026, complete with branded cups that quickly became a collector's item on social media and secondary marketplaces like Etsy.

7 Brew was also the official Cup Sponsor of American Idol's live shows from March through May 2026, putting 7 Brew-branded cups in front of the show's national primetime audience — one of the brand's biggest single visibility moments to date alongside its Dude Perfect collaboration.

Who Owns 7 Brew? Ownership & Investment

7 Brew remains a privately held company — it is not publicly traded, so there is no 7 Brew stock ticker to buy on a public exchange despite periodic search interest in “7 Brew IPO” or “7 Brew stock.” The company is led by founders Ron and Jori Crume and has taken on a growth investment from investment firm Blackstone to help fund its rapid multi-state expansion.
