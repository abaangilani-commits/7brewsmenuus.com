Company History Origin Story: How 7 Brew Started

7 Brew was founded in Rogers, Arkansas, in 2017 by Ron Crume, an Oregon native who wanted to reinvent the drive-thru coffee experience. What began as a single stand in Northwest Arkansas became a regional sensation fast, thanks to drive-thru-only convenience, colorful drinks, and a loud, friendly culture that set it apart from traditional cafes.

Crume built 7 Brew around a simple idea: serve high-quality breve-style specialty drinks at drive-thru speed without sacrificing flavor or fun. Every drink is made to order with fresh ingredients, and the menu spans coffee, energy, fizz, lemonade, smoothies, shakes, and more — making 7 Brew a true coffee shop alternative for people who want variety beyond standard drip coffee.

Growth Timeline: From One Stand to 900+ Locations Nationwide

7 Brew's franchise expansion growth has been one of the fastest in the drive-thru coffee industry. Here is how the brand scaled:

  • 2017: First stand opens in Rogers, Arkansas, with seven signature drinks on the menu
  • 2020: The chain reaches 7 stands. In January, a group led by John Davidson buys the company and forms Brew Culture LLC
  • 2021: Jimmy John Liautaud and Jamie Coulter take a majority stake through Drink House Holdings
  • January 2023: 38 stands
  • 2024: Blackstone makes its growth investment in February and Liautaud sells out. The year ends at 321 stands
  • October 2025: The 500th stand opens in Toms River, New Jersey
  • 2026: The 777th stand opens in June, the mobile ordering app launches in August, and canned 7 Brew drinks reach Walmart shelves
  • October 2026: 900+ stands across 38 states by the company's own count, with new stands opening weekly

The franchise model allows local entrepreneurs to open and operate their own 7 Brew stands while maintaining the brand's signature speed, quality, and atmosphere. This rapid expansion has made 7 Brew one of the fastest-growing drive-thru coffee brands in the United States.

What Makes 7 Brew Different?

Several things set 7 Brew apart from competitors like Starbucks, Dutch Bros, and Scooter's Coffee. First, it is drive-thru only — there are no indoor seats, lobbies, or walk-up windows at most locations. This keeps the line moving and wait times short, typically under three minutes. Second, the menu is built around breve-style drinks made with half-and-half as the default milk, giving every coffee drink a rich, creamy texture. Third, the energy drink lineup (7 Energy) is a proprietary blend you cannot get anywhere else — a genuine coffee shop alternative for people who prefer energy drinks over espresso.

7 Brew has also gained mainstream visibility through its Dude Perfect partnership and social media presence, with colorful drink photos and creative seasonal offerings regularly going viral on TikTok and Instagram. The brand projects a youthful, high-energy brand identity culture that appeals to a broad audience from college students to busy parents.

What Is a Breve? 7 Brew's Signature Style Explained

Breve is the default milk style behind most 7 Brew drinks, and it's the detail that confuses a lot of first-timers. A traditional latte is made with steamed milk; a breve swaps that milk for steamed half-and-half, giving the drink a noticeably richer, creamier, slightly sweeter mouthfeel than a standard latte made with whole milk. When you order a named 7 Brew drink like the Blondie or Brunette without specifying a milk, half-and-half breve is what you'll get by default.

Is breve healthier than a latte? Generally, no — half-and-half is roughly half cream, so a breve carries more fat and more calories per ounce than the same drink made with whole milk, and considerably more than one made with a lower-fat option. If you want the flavor without the extra richness, ask for oat, almond or skim milk in place of half-and-half — every 7 Brew drink can be made with a standard latte-style milk instead of breve at no extra charge. How Did 7 Brew Get Its Name?

The "7" is not about days of the week. It comes from the seven signature coffee drinks on the menu when the first stand opened in 2017: the Blondie, Brunette, Smooth 7, Cinnamon Roll, White Chocolate Mocha, German Chocolate and Sweet & Salty. Those seven are still sold today as the 7 Originals. The menu has grown past 20,000 possible combinations since, but the name never changed.

Brand Partnerships: Dude Perfect

7 Brew's marketing has leaned heavily into sports and entertainment tie-ins. In September 2025 the brand announced a partnership with the trick-shot group Dude Perfect, and the collaboration launched its own “Brewed Perfect” line of drinks in spring 2026, complete with branded cups that quickly became a collector's item on social media and secondary marketplaces like Etsy.

Who Owns 7 Brew? Ownership & Investment

7 Brew remains a privately held company — it is not publicly traded, so there is no 7 Brew stock ticker to buy on a public exchange despite periodic search interest in “7 Brew IPO” or “7 Brew stock.” The parent company is Brew Culture, LLC. A group led by John Davidson bought 7 Brew in January 2020, and Davidson has run it as CEO since. Jimmy John Liautaud, the Jimmy John's founder, and Lone Star Steakhouse founder Jamie Coulter took a majority stake in 2021 through Drink House Holdings, then sold out in February 2024 as part of Blackstone's growth investment. Blackstone holds a minority stake today. Founder Ron Crume remains a shareholder but is not involved in day-to-day operations. Frequently asked questions

What is 7 Brew Coffee?+

7 Brew is a drive-thru-only coffee chain founded in Rogers, Arkansas. It serves espresso-based breve drinks, energy drinks, Italian sodas, and shakes from standalone stands with no indoor seating.

How many 7 Brew locations are there?+

7 Brew has 900+ drive-thru stands across 38 US states as of October 2026, by the company's own count, with new stands opening weekly.

What makes 7 Brew different from Starbucks?+

7 Brew is drive-thru only with no walk-in stores. Their signature drinks use a breve base (espresso with half-and-half) rather than standard milk, and they emphasize speed of service and colorful energy drink options.

What is a breve at 7 Brew?+

A breve is 7 Brew's default espresso drink style, made with steamed half-and-half instead of the milk used in a standard latte, giving it a richer, creamier texture.

Is a 7 Brew breve healthier than a latte?+

Generally no. Half-and-half has more fat and calories than whole or lower-fat milk, so a breve is typically richer in calories than the same drink made with a standard latte milk. You can ask for oat, almond or skim milk instead of breve at no extra charge.

Who owns 7 Brew?+

Brew Culture LLC owns 7 Brew. A group led by John Davidson bought the company in 2020 and Davidson is CEO. Blackstone took a minority stake in 2024. Founder Ron Crume remains a shareholder but does not run the company.

Is 7 Brew publicly traded?+

No. 7 Brew is a privately held company and does not have publicly traded stock, despite search interest in a possible 7 Brew IPO.

Where did 7 Brew start?+

7 Brew started in Rogers, Arkansas, in 2017, when founder Ron Crume opened a single drive-thru stand in Northwest Arkansas.

When was 7 Brew founded?+

2017. The first stand opened that year in Rogers, Arkansas, and the chain stayed Arkansas-only until expanding in 2019.

Why is it called 7 Brew?+

The name comes from the seven signature drinks the brand launched with. Those 7 Originals — Blondie, Brunette, Smooth 7, Cinnamon Roll, White Chocolate Mocha, German Chocolate and Sweet & Salty — are still their own section on the menu board today.

Where is 7 Brew&rsquo;s headquarters?+

7 Brew is headquartered in Springdale, Arkansas, in the same Northwest Arkansas corner where the first stand opened in Rogers.

Who founded 7 Brew?+

Ron Crume founded 7 Brew in Rogers, Arkansas, in 2017. He sold the company in January 2020 to an investor group led by John Davidson, which runs it today as Brew Culture LLC. Crume remains a shareholder but is not involved in day-to-day operations.

Does Jimmy John own 7 Brew?+

No. Jimmy John Liautaud, the Jimmy John's founder, and Lone Star Steakhouse founder Jamie Coulter took a majority stake in 7 Brew in 2021 through Drink House Holdings. Liautaud sold his entire stake in February 2024, when Blackstone made its growth investment.

Is 7 Brew a franchise?+

Yes. Nearly every stand is owned and run by a local franchise operator under the Brew Culture LLC parent company. Franchising is what took the chain from 38 stands in January 2023 to 900+ by late 2026.

Is 7 Brew just coffee?+

No. Coffee is one of seven categories. Stands also serve 7 Energy drinks, 7 Fizz sodas, teas and chais, matcha, lemonades, smoothies and shakes, and the syrups and sauces combine into more than 20,000 possible drinks.
