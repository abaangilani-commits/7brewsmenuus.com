Customizing your order

Do not order an Energy drink assuming it is the same as a Fizz. For caffeine or ingredient questions, use the exact base and recipe in the official guide.

Tell the stand the drink base, size, flavor combination and any requested adjustments. Ask about availability before assuming a flavor or topping can be substituted. Browse flavor and sweetness options.

Nutrition and prices

Match your drink to the official nutrition guide for the chosen serving and preparation. Ask the stand about recipes not covered there. Current prices depend on the selected location and order; confirm in the official app or at the stand.

How many 7 Fizz flavors are there? Three honest counts

Pages disagree on the count because they are counting different lists. Here are all three, kept separate. The official menu board shows 10 named cards: Blood Orange, Pink Mermaid, Peaches 'n Cream, Brew Lagoon, Cherry Blossom, Tiki Tango, Stargazer, Dragons Blood, Bahama Mama and Midnight Rain. Underneath sits a suggested-combos list of about 50 more builds, which is where names like Pixie Stick, Fruit Roll-Up and Sunrise-as-a-combo live. And the official nutrition guide publishes numbers for only 6 entries: the plain base plus Blood Orange, Brew Lagoon, Peaches 'n Cream, Pink Mermaid and Sunrise.

That last point matters. Cherry Blossom, Tiki Tango, Stargazer, Dragons Blood, Bahama Mama and Midnight Rain are real menu drinks, but 7 Brew publishes no calories or sugar for them. Any page quoting exact numbers for those six invented them. The five flavors with real numbers each have a full page on this site, linked above and below.

Which Fizz is best? The critic and the fans disagree

Tasting Table tasted 13 fizzes in July 2026 and ranked Pixie Stick first, with Fruit Roll-Up second and Blood Orange third. Pink Mermaid came in 11th of 13. Then you open the fan threads and Pink Mermaid is the drink regulars call their favorite, over and over. Both can be true. The critic scored whether each soda delivered its named flavors in one sitting. Fans are scoring the drink they reorder weekly, and the watermelon-coconut-strawberry build is an easy one to live with. Start with Pixie Stick if you want the verdict of a tasting panel. Start with Pink Mermaid if you want the verdict of the drive-thru line. The full base drink, flavors and system are on the 7 Fizz Soda page.

What Are 7 Brew Fizz Drinks?

7 Brew Fizz drinks are the chain's take on Italian soda sparkling drinks — a fruity sparkling beverage — carbonated water combined with Torani-style flavoring syrups to create carbonated fruit flavored drinks and, optionally, a splash of cream. Unlike the 7 Energy line, Fizz drinks are a caffeine-free refreshment, making them a popular kid-friendly drink choice for children, caffeine-sensitive customers, and anyone who wants a refreshing soda alternative to coffee. The menu includes flavors like Blood Orange, Brew Lagoon, Peaches N Cream, and Pink Mermaid, each available in all three cup sizes.

The key difference between Fizz and 7 Energy is the base: Fizz uses plain sparkling water, while Energy adds caffeine, taurine, and B-vitamins. If you want the same flavor with a caffeine kick, order that combo on the 7 Energy base instead. At that point it is a 7 Energy, not a Fizz, and the caffeine is 67 to 133 mg by size.

Best Fizz Flavor Combinations

The standard Fizz menu is a starting point — most regulars customize their orders with extra syrup shots or cream additions. Top-selling combinations include the Peaches N Cream Fizz with a vanilla cream topping float, the Pink Mermaid with coconut syrup, and the Blood Orange Fizz with a splash of raspberry. Only two flavors, Peaches 'n Cream and Sunrise, have a published sugar-free version in the official guide (0 calories at small and medium). For the rest, ask what the stand can swap, but know that 7 Brew publishes no sugar-free numbers for them. For calorie counts by size and flavor, use the nutrition calculator.

Fizz drinks are also the lightest option on the menu calorie-wise — a plain Fizz is 0 calories at any size, and the named flavors run 160 to 180 calories in a small. Compare that to a breve-based Blondie, which is 280 calories in a small and 480 in a medium. For a full comparison, see the best drinks guide or browse the complete menu.

Fizz as a Summer Iced Cold Drink

When temperatures climb, 7 Brew Fizz drinks are the perfect summer iced cold drink. Served over ice in 16 oz, 24 oz, or 32 oz cups, these bubbly refreshers are light, fruity, and endlessly customizable. Popular warm-weather picks include cherry lime blue raspberry combinations, tropical blends with coconut and mango syrups, and the classic Blood Orange Fizz. Since every Fizz is caffeine-free, they are also ideal for evening drive-thru runs or for anyone limiting their daily caffeine intake. Pair a Fizz with a snack at the window for a quick, refreshing treat. Frequently Asked Questions: 7 Brew Fizz

Straightforward answers about caffeine, sugar-free versions, flavors and what is actually in the cup.

What is a 7 Brew Fizz?+

Sparkling water and flavor syrups over ice. An Italian soda, in other words, made to order at the stand. It is not a canned product and it is not soda from a fountain gun. The official line is a handcrafted, caffeine-free soda with your choice of flavor, and that word choice is doing real work: with about 50 suggested combos on top of the 10 board cards, the flavor is the drink.

Does 7 Fizz have caffeine?+

No. Every fizz entry in the official guide reads 0 mg, at every size, including the plain base. That is the whole point of the category. The only way caffeine enters the picture is if you order the same flavor combo built on the 7 Energy base instead, and then what you are holding is a 7 Energy, not a Fizz.

What is the difference between 7 Fizz and 7 Energy?+

The base, nothing else. The same syrup combos run across both categories. Sparkling water underneath makes it a Fizz at 0 mg of caffeine. The house energy base underneath makes it a 7 Energy at 67, 105 or 133 mg by size. Fans use this deliberately: a favorite energy flavor, ordered as a Fizz, is the evening version of the same drink.

Which 7 Fizz flavors are sugar-free?+

Two have a published sugar-free version in the official guide: Peaches 'n Cream and Sunrise, both at 0 calories and 0 g of sugar in a small or medium, and 5 calories in a large. No other fizz flavor has sugar-free numbers published. A stand may swap a syrup for you, but an unpublished swap is not the same as a sugar-free drink, and the guide's own footnote warns that a drink built with sugar-free flavors may not be sugar-free overall. The full picture is on our sugar-free page.

How many calories are in a 7 Fizz?+

The plain Fizz is 0 calories and 0 g of sugar at any size. The named flavors with published numbers run 160 to 180 calories in a small and 240 to 270 in a medium: Pink Mermaid is the lightest medium at 240, Peaches 'n Cream the heaviest at 270, and a large Peaches 'n Cream at 360 is the biggest number in the category. Cream or cold foam on top adds more, and those additions are not in the base figures.

What sparkling water does 7 Brew use for Fizz?+

7 Brew does not say. The brand is published nowhere, not on the menu, not in the guide. The name you will see repeated online is LaCroix, and even the review that popularized it calls it a rumor. Treat it as one. What is certain is the format: unflavored sparkling water, syrup, ice.

Which 7 Fizz flavor should I try first?+

Depends whose verdict you trust. Tasting Table ranked 13 fizzes after a full tasting and put Pixie Stick first and Blood Orange third, with Pink Mermaid down at 11th. Fan threads reverse that last call: Pink Mermaid is the most-named favorite among regulars. If you want one safe first order, the fan favorite is the safer bet for a weekly drink, and the critic's pick is the more interesting one to taste once.

Is 7 Fizz a good choice for kids?+

It is the category parents land on most, because it is caffeine-free at every size and there is a kids cup. The honest caveat is sugar: a medium named flavor carries 61 to 69 g, which is soda territory, because it is soda. The plain base and the two published sugar-free versions are the 0 g routes.
