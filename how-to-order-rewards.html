How to Order at 7 Brew: Drink Names & Customizations

Use a clear ordering sequence for a named drink or custom flavor combination, with links to the official app and stand information.

A simple ordering sequence

Say the size and preparation, the drink name or base, and then your requested flavors, sweetness, milk and toppings. This is a practical ordering suggestion, not a required brand script.

Example orders

“A medium iced Blondie, half sweet, please.” For a custom fruit drink, specify Fizz or Energy before listing flavors. Repeat back any substitutions so both you and the stand agree on the order.

Before you travel

Check your stand’s address and hours. For ordering ahead, account access and the current points system, use our app and rewards guide.

Confirm the price

Ask for the total after milk changes or toppings are added. A general online estimate is not a quote for your selected stand.

First Time Ordering Guide: Drive-Thru Etiquette at 7 Brew

If this is your first visit, here are some beginner ordering tips to make the experience smooth. 7 Brew is a drive-thru only coffee chain, so knowing how to order at drive-thru stands helps you feel confident pulling up. Unlike a traditional cafe, you stay in your car — a friendly brewista (7 Brew's term for barista) takes your order at the speaker or window.

What to Say When You Pull Up

Follow this simple script: state your size (small, medium, or large), whether you want it hot, iced, or blended, the drink name, and any customizations. For example: "Medium iced Blondie, sugar-free vanilla, oat milk, no whip." Check the menu board drink names displayed at the stand if you need inspiration — every location posts the full lineup with seasonal specials highlighted.

How to Customize Your Drink

You can customize your drink in several ways: add or remove espresso shots, swap milks (half-and-half, oat, almond, or skim), choose sugar-free syrups, adjust sweetness level (half sweet, no sweet), and add or skip whipped cream or drizzle. Don't hesitate to ask the barista brewista for recommendations — they're trained to help you build the perfect drink. See our syrup and flavor guide for all available options.

Payment Methods Accepted at 7 Brew

7 Brew accepts all major payment methods accepted at the window: cash credit card Apple Pay, Google Pay, and Samsung Pay are all valid options. You can also pay through mobile app ordering using the official 7 Brew app, which lets you earn rewards points on every purchase. Most stands also accept gift cards purchased online or in-app. For the fastest checkout, link a card to your app and scan at the window.

Tipping at 7 Brew: What to Know

Tipping at 7 Brew is appreciated but never required. When paying by card or through the app, you will see a tip prompt on the screen. Most customers tip – per drink or 15–20%%. The brewistas remember regulars and often go above and beyond with drink customization, so a tip is a great way to show appreciation for excellent drive-thru etiquette and service.
