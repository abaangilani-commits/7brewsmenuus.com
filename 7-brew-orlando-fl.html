About 7 Brew in Orlando, Florida

Orlando's 7 Brew drive-thru on East Colonial Drive is part of the chain's expanding Florida footprint, which now includes nearly 50 locations statewide. The stand serves the East Orlando corridor near Union Park, giving theme-park visitors and local commuters fast access to 7 Brew's breve-style espresso drinks, 7 Energy drinks, Italian sodas, smoothies, and shakes.

7 Brew's espresso-breve base — shots pulled over steamed half-and-half instead of regular milk — produces a naturally richer, creamier drink that holds up well over ice, which matters in Orlando's year-round warm weather. Iced and blended drinks dominate orders here. The Blondie over ice, a frozen Smooth 7, and the Italian soda line are all popular warm-weather picks.

First-time visitors should browse the full 7 Brew menu with prices and check the best drinks guide for ranked recommendations. The secret menu features dozens of custom creations trending on TikTok — just show the barista the recipe or name. Every drink can be made with sugar-free syrups at no upcharge, and the calorie calculator helps you compare options.
