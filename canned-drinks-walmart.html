7 Brew Canned Drinks at Walmart: Coffee & Energy Cans (2026 Guide)

All 8 ready-to-drink flavors, prices, nutrition facts, taste reviews, and where to find them. Walmart exclusive — updated September 2026.

Quick Overview: 7 Brew at Walmart

In August 2026, 7 Brew expanded beyond the drive-thru with its first-ever retail product line — ready-to-drink canned coffees and energy beverages sold exclusively at Walmart. The launch includes three chilled espresso breve coffees and five energy drink flavors, marking 7 Brew's entry into the booming ready to drink coffee market (projected to reach $50 billion by 2033). With 4,200% growth since 2022, 7 Brew is bringing its sugar free, dairy free, and gluten free energy options alongside cream-based espresso cans to grocery store shelves for the first time.

7 Brew Canned Coffees — Ready-to-Drink Espresso Breves

7 Brew's three canned coffees bring the drive-thru breve experience home in a portable 11 oz can. Made with 100% Arabica coffee, real cream, and natural flavors, each chilled espresso breve delivers about one shot of espresso worth of caffeine (~60 mg). These are refrigerated products — look for them in the cold case, not the shelf-stable coffee aisle. Each can retails for $2.98 (about 27 cents per fluid ounce).

Blondie® Chilled Espresso Breve

Caramel + Vanilla 11 oz Can $2.98 ~60 mg Caffeine 7g Protein

7 Brew's signature flavor in a can. The Blondie combines rich vanilla-caramel flavoring with real cream and 100% Arabica espresso. Tasting Table described it as "smoother and creamier than saccharine-sweet and syrupy" with "buttery, dessert-like notes" — a solid translation of the drive-thru original. With 7 grams of protein per can (14% daily value), it doubles as a filling on-the-go coffee.

Walmart rating: 4.3 out of 5 stars (482+ ratings) — 60% five-star reviews

Brunette Brownie™ Chilled Espresso Mocha

Chocolate + Espresso 11 oz Can $2.98 ~60 mg Caffeine 8g Protein

A semisweet cocoa-espresso blend that delivers "a nice balance between coffee and cocoa" according to Tasting Table's review. The Brunette Brownie uses the same real cream base but swaps the caramel-vanilla for a chocolate brownie mocha profile. At 8 grams of protein per can, it has the highest protein content of the three coffees. Rich and sweet without being overly so — think mocha without the sugar overload.

Best for: Mocha lovers who want chocolate and espresso in balance

Banana Bread™ Chilled Espresso Breve

Banana + Hazelnut 11 oz Can $2.98 ~60 mg Caffeine

The most unique flavor in the RTD lineup — there's no permanent year-round drive-thru equivalent, making it a Walmart-exclusive experience inspired by the seasonal Banana Bread Blondie. Ripe banana and roasted hazelnut flavors create what Tasting Table called "this rich, almost dessert-like taste." This is the one flavor you genuinely can't get at a 7 Brew stand.

Walmart rating: 4.3 out of 5 stars (647+ ratings) — 62% five-star reviews. The most-reviewed flavor in the lineup.

7 Brew Energy Cans — Ready-to-Drink at Walmart

7 Brew's energy drink line-up brings five carbonated flavors to Walmart shelves, each packed with 114 mg of caffeine, taurine, B vitamins (B3, B5, B6, B12), guarana seed extract, panax ginseng extract, and L-carnitine. Four of the five flavors are sugar-free (5 calories, sweetened with sucralose and acesulfame potassium). Each 12 oz can retails for $2.48. Three of these flavors — Sugar-Free Ocean Breeze, Sugar-Free Nightshade, and Sugar-Free Pink Mermaid — are sold only at Walmart and cannot be ordered at 7 Brew drive-thru stands.

7 Brew Energy Original

Classic Energy 12 oz Can $2.48 114 mg Caffeine 39g Sugar

The only non-sugar-free option in the energy lineup. Tart and candy-like with a SweeTarts-style sweetness that Tasting Table compared to Red Bull. Contains taurine and B vitamins. At 114 mg of caffeine, it sits between a standard cup of coffee and most high-caffeine energy drinks (Monster has 160 mg per 16 oz), providing a moderate boost without the jitters. Also available at 7 Brew drive-thru stands.

7 Brew Energy Original Sugar-Free

Classic Sugar-Free 12 oz Can $2.48 114 mg Caffeine 5 Calories

Same energy profile as the Original but with zero sugar and just 5 calories per can. Sweetened with sucralose and acesulfame potassium (Ace-K). Tasting Table described it as "smoother and more mellowed out" than the sugared version with "slightly heightened carbonation." Compatible with keto, diabetic, and low-carb diets. Also available at 7 Brew drive-thru stands.

7 Brew Energy Ocean Breeze™ Sugar-Free

Blue Raspberry + Coconut 12 oz Can $2.48 114 mg Caffeine 5 Calories Walmart Exclusive

A tropical blue raspberry and coconut blend that's exclusively available at Walmart — you cannot order this flavor at any 7 Brew drive-thru. The blue raspberry is balanced by subtle coconut undertones, keeping it from being too sweet or artificial. Contains Blue 1 dye. Rated 4.9 out of 5 stars on Walmart.com with buyers calling it the "best energy drink around."

7 Brew Energy Nightshade™ Sugar-Free

Lavender + Pomegranate + Blue Raspberry 12 oz Can $2.48 114 mg Caffeine 5 Calories Walmart Exclusive

The most distinctive flavor in the energy lineup. A deep purple, slushie-like blend of lavender, pomegranate, and blue raspberry that Tasting Table said "tastes the most like a slushie" with blue raspberry dominating the profile. The lavender adds a floral complexity you won't find in mainstream energy drinks. Another Walmart-exclusive flavor that isn't sold at drive-thru stands.

7 Brew Energy Pink Mermaid™ Sugar-Free

Watermelon + Coconut + Strawberry 12 oz Can $2.48 114 mg Caffeine 5 Calories Walmart Exclusive

A tropical-fruity blend of strawberry, watermelon, and coconut. The coconut gives it a "richer undertone" and "thicker mouthfeel" compared to the other energy flavors, while the watermelon-strawberry combination keeps it "sweet and sour yet fresh." Another Walmart-only exclusive that's based on one of 7 Brew's most popular drive-thru energy flavors — the in-store version has been called the best translation of a drive-thru favorite to can format.

Energy Can Base Ingredients

All 7 Brew Energy cans share a common base: carbonated water, natural flavors, caffeine, taurine, B vitamins (niacin/B3, pantothenic acid/B5, pyridoxine/B6, cyanocobalamin/B12), citric acid, potassium sorbate, sodium benzoate, guarana seed extract, panax ginseng extract, and L-carnitine. Sugar-free varieties add sucralose and acesulfame potassium. The energy cans are dairy-free, gluten-free, and vegan-friendly.

Where to Find 7 Brew Cans at Walmart

Canned Coffees

Available at approximately 4,400 Walmart stores nationwide. Look for them in two locations: the grab and go coolers near the checkout area (sometimes labeled Grab & Go), or in the refrigerated ready-to-drink coffee aisle alongside Starbucks Frappuccino, Dunkin', and La Colombe bottles.

These are chilled products — if you don't find them near ambient coffees, check the cold case.

Energy Drinks

Available at over 1,100 Walmart stores and approximately 450 Walmart fuel stations. Located in the energy drink aisle near Monster, Red Bull, Celsius, and Ghost. Distribution is expanding — if your local Walmart doesn't stock them yet, check back or order online at Walmart.com.

Three flavors (Ocean Breeze, Nightshade, Pink Mermaid) are Walmart-only and not sold at 7 Brew stands.

Online Ordering

Both product lines are available on Walmart.com at select locations for delivery, pickup, or shipping. Multi-packs (4-packs) may be available for a slight per-can discount. Stock varies — the Banana Bread flavor has been the fastest seller, with some locations showing "Low stock" or selling 2,000+ units daily.

Not available at Target, Kroger, Amazon, or any other retailer.

Why Walmart only? According to industry analysts, the Walmart exclusive partnership serves a dual purpose: it "instantly puts [7 Brew's] brand in front of millions of shoppers in markets where they don't even have a drive-thru lane yet," while Walmart gains exclusive access to "viral drive-thru coffee" that keeps younger consumers from shopping at Target or convenience stores for their morning caffeine fix. 7 Brew operates 800+ drive-thru stands across 38 states, but Walmart's 4,400+ stores give the brand instant national reach far beyond its physical drive-thru footprint.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where can I buy 7 Brew canned drinks?

Exclusively at Walmart. Canned coffees are at ~4,400 stores; energy drinks at 1,100+ stores and ~450 Walmart fuel stations. Also available on Walmart.com at select locations for delivery or pickup. Not sold at Target, Kroger, Amazon, or any other retailer.

How much do 7 Brew cans cost at Walmart?

Canned coffees are $2.98 per 11 oz can (about 27¢/oz). Energy drinks are $2.48 per 12 oz can (about 21¢/oz). Multi-packs (4-packs) may offer a slight per-can discount at some locations.

How much caffeine is in a 7 Brew can?

Canned coffees: approximately 60 mg per 11 oz can (equivalent to about one espresso shot). Energy drinks: 114 mg per 12 oz can, plus taurine, guarana, ginseng, and B vitamins.

Are 7 Brew cans the same as the drive-thru drinks?

Not identical. The canned coffees use the same 100% Arabica espresso and real cream but have a smoother, more integrated texture than the layered drive-thru breves. The Banana Bread flavor is actually unique to the cans — there's no year-round drive-thru equivalent.

Are 7 Brew energy cans sugar-free?

Four of five are sugar-free (5 calories each, sweetened with sucralose and acesulfame potassium): Original Sugar-Free, Ocean Breeze, Nightshade, and Pink Mermaid. Only the Original flavor contains sugar (39g per can).

Can I find 7 Brew cans at Target, Kroger, or Amazon?

No. These are Walmart exclusives as of September 2026. Three energy flavors (Ocean Breeze, Nightshade, Pink Mermaid) are exclusively available at Walmart and can't even be ordered at 7 Brew drive-thru stands.

Which Walmart aisle are 7 Brew cans in?

Canned coffees: Grab & Go coolers near checkout or the refrigerated RTD coffee aisle. Energy drinks: the energy drink aisle near Monster and Red Bull. Some Walmart fuel stations also carry both lines.

Are 7 Brew cans dairy-free or vegan?

The three canned coffees contain real cream and are NOT dairy-free or vegan. All five energy drinks are dairy-free, gluten-free, and vegan-friendly.

Which 7 Brew can flavor is the best?

Based on reviews and sales data: The Banana Bread is the most popular coffee (647+ ratings, most-reviewed). For energy drinks, the Ocean Breeze Sugar-Free has the highest rating (4.9 stars). Tasting Table rated the Blondie as the smoothest coffee and the Nightshade as the most unique energy flavor.

Are 7 Brew cans keto-friendly?

The four sugar-free energy drinks (5 calories, 0g sugar each) are keto-compatible. The canned coffees contain real cream and sugar, so they are not recommended for strict keto diets. The sugared Original Energy has 39g of sugar and is not keto-friendly.

When did 7 Brew canned drinks launch?

The nationwide Walmart launch was on August 22, 2026. An initial Arkansas-only pilot ran from March 2026. The launch was supported by a press release from 7 Brew and endorsed by Walmart Vice President of Beverages Brian Salmon.
