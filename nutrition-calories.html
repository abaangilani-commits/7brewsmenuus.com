Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions: 7 Brew Nutrition & Calories

Straightforward answers about calorie counts, keto customizations, sugar-free syrups, and plant milk options.

How many calories are in a 7 Brew Blondie? +

A standard 7 Brew Blondie Breve made with half-and-half contains roughly 380 calories for a Small (16 oz), 540 calories for a Medium (24 oz), and 710 calories for a Large (32 oz). You can cut calories by 50% by requesting skim milk, almond milk, or sugar-free syrups.

What sugar-free syrups does 7 Brew offer? +

7 Brew carries 12 sugar-free Torani syrups: SF Vanilla, SF Caramel, SF Hazelnut, SF White Chocolate, SF Dark Chocolate, SF Irish Cream, SF Brown Sugar Cinnamon, SF Coconut, SF Strawberry, SF Raspberry, SF Peach, and SF Peppermint.

How do you order keto at 7 Brew? +

Order an Iced Cold Brew or Americano with a splash of heavy cream and your choice of sugar-free syrups. This keeps net carbohydrates under 2 grams and delivers zero added sugars.

Which plant-based milk has the lowest calories at 7 Brew? +

Unsweetened almond milk is the lowest calorie milk choice at 7 Brew with around 40 calories and 1g carb per cup. Oat milk delivers about 130 calories and 19g carbs, while coconut milk has 70 calories and 7g carbs.

Does 7 Brew charge extra for plant-based milks? +

No. 7 Brew provides Pacific Barista Oat Milk, unsweetened almond milk, and coconut milk as free dairy substitutions with zero extra surcharge on coffee and espresso drinks.
