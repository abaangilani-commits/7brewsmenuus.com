Milk & Base Options

Most 7 Brew Originals and Classics are built as a "breve" — espresso steamed with half-and-half instead of milk, which is what gives drinks like the Blondie and Brunette their signature richness. If you want a lighter option, reported substitutes include whole milk, oat milk and almond milk. Non-dairy swaps may carry a small surcharge and availability can vary by stand, so it is worth confirming before you order if it matters to you. See our full allergen and dietary guide for dairy-free specifics.

Sweetness & Sugar-Free Customization

Sweetness in most 7 Brew drinks comes from flavored syrup pumps, which can typically be adjusted per size:

  • Regular sweet — the default recipe for that drink and size.
  • Half sweet — ask for half the usual syrup pumps; a common request for people who find the standard recipe too sweet.
  • Extra sweet — add extra pumps of your chosen flavor.
  • Sugar-free — many flavor combinations have a sugar-free syrup version, and all four sugar-free 7 Energy flavors use sucralose and acesulfame potassium instead of sugar. Always specify "sugar-free" explicitly, since it typically is not the default.

For full nutrition and sugar counts by drink, see nutrition & calories.

Popular Flavor Combinations to Try

Because 7 Brew mixes and matches syrup flavors across bases, you are not limited to the printed menu. Popular starting points reported by customers include pairing caramel with vanilla (the Blondie base), chocolate with hazelnut (a Brunette-style mocha), and fruit combinations like blackberry with lemonade. If you like a flavor from one drink, ask whether it can be added to a different base — for example, adding a energy flavor's fruit syrup to an Italian soda base, or vice versa.

Exactly What to Say at the Window

A simple order template that works well at any 7 Brew stand:

"Can I get a [size] [base — e.g. Blondie / Energy / Fizz], [half sweet / sugar-free], with [oat milk / almond milk / extra shot], [iced with light ice / hot / blended]?"

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you customize any drink at 7 Brew?

Yes. Nearly every drink on the menu, including Originals, Classics, Energy, Fizz sodas and lemonades, can be adjusted for milk, sweetness, ice level and extra flavor shots. Shakes and smoothies have more limited customization since they are blended to a set recipe.

What milk options does 7 Brew offer?

Reported options include whole milk, oat milk, almond milk and the brand's signature half-and-half used in breve-style drinks. Availability and any non-dairy surcharge can vary by stand — confirm with your local location before ordering.

How do I order a 7 Brew drink half sweet?

Ask for your drink "half sweet" or specify a percentage ("half the usual sweetener") when you place your order. Baristas can typically adjust sweetener pumps up or down per size.

Are there sugar-free syrup options at 7 Brew?

Yes. Sugar-free syrup versions are available for many flavor combinations, and the 7 Energy sugar-free varieties use sucralose and acesulfame potassium instead of sugar. Ask specifically for "sugar-free" when ordering, since the default recipe may use regular syrup.

What does 'breve' mean at 7 Brew?

A breve is an espresso drink made with steamed half-and-half instead of milk, giving it a noticeably richer, creamier texture than a standard latte. It's the base of 7 Brew's signature Originals lineup like the Blondie and Brunette.

Can I order a 7 Brew drink extra ice or no ice?

Yes, ice level is one of the most common customizations. You can ask for light ice, extra ice or no ice on any iced drink, which will also change how much liquid fits in the cup.

Does 7 Brew have dairy-free options?

Oat milk and almond milk are commonly available as substitutes for milk or half-and-half, though not every drink translates perfectly to a non-dairy base. See our full allergen and dietary guide for more detail.

What is a 'dirty' or 'red eye' style order at 7 Brew?

Ordering conventions like adding an extra shot of espresso to a non-coffee drink (sometimes called "dirty") or adding coffee to an energy drink work at most drive-thru concepts including 7 Brew — just describe exactly what you want to the brewista, since these aren't official menu names.
