7 Brew Rewards: How Points Work, How to Check Your Balance & Redeem

7 Brew Rewards gives members 100 points for every qualifying drink, and 1,000 points unlocks a free drink — so roughly every 10th drink is on the house. Here is how points work after the August 2026 changes, how to check your points balance and how to redeem a reward.

7 Brew Rewards at a glance

RedemptionManual — choose a reward in the app's Rewards tab

How 7 Brew Rewards points work

You earn points per drink, not per dollar. Every qualifying small, medium or large drink adds 100 points to your account, whatever it costs. Order through the app or scan your rewards code at the drive-thru window so the purchase is tied to your account.

Since August 12, 2026, 7 Brew Energy cans and kids-size drinks no longer earn points. Points and any unused free drinks expire if your account has no purchase for 365 consecutive days, so one visit a year keeps them active.

How to check your 7 Brew points balance

  1. Open the 7 Brew app and sign in to your rewards account.
  2. Tap the Rewards tab at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Your total points balance and the reward options you can afford are shown there.

7 Brew points can only be checked in the official app or through 7 Brew support. This independent website can't see or change your balance. If a recent drink is missing, see rewards not working below.

How to redeem 7 Brew rewards

Rewards are no longer applied automatically when you reach 1,000 points. You decide when to spend them:

  1. Open the Rewards tab in the app.
  2. Choose the reward you want — a free drink of any size, or keep saving for a merch reward.
  3. Redeem it on an app order, or have your code scanned at the window before you pay.

Because every drink earns the same 100 points, get the most from a free-drink reward by using it on a large or on your most expensive order.

Ordering ahead in the 7 Brew app

7 Brew offers an app for iOS and Android. Browse without signing in; an account is needed to place an order. Select the correct stand before customizing your drink, then check in and pay at the drive-thru. Official app and rewards information.

Check the stand before submitting

Compare the street address, city and state in the app with the location you intend to visit. A nearby city may have multiple stands, and a familiar road name can occur in another state. Find official stand links.

Having trouble with your account?

Use 7 Brew support for account or order help. Describe the issue and the selected stand. Never send account passwords or verification codes to this independent website. We cannot view points, place orders or reset a brand account.

7 Brew Loyalty Program: How to Earn & Redeem Rewards Points

The 7 Brew loyalty program lets you earn and redeem rewards points on qualifying drinks bought through the app or scanned at the window. Each qualifying drink earns 100 points. So how many points does a free drink take? 1,000 points unlocks a free drink of any size — roughly one free drink for every 10 you buy.

There are no paid membership levels. Once you pass 1,000 points you can redeem a free drink or keep banking points for merch rewards, and 7 Brew occasionally runs bonus-point promotions that let you earn faster. Keep an eye on push notifications and in-app banners for double- or triple-point days.

Mobile App Download: Get the 7 Brew App

The mobile app download is available on both App Store Google Play platforms. Search "7 Brew" and look for the official app with the red logo. The app lets you browse the full menu, customize drinks, save favorites, track your points balance, and use mobile order ahead to skip the line at your local stand.

When you first download, you may receive a sign-up bonus offer — typically a free drink or bonus points on your first purchase. Link a credit or debit card for seamless payment in-app at the drive-thru window. The app also supports Apple Pay and Google Pay for contactless checkout.

Free Drink Birthday Reward

One of the most popular perks is the free drink birthday reward. Make sure your birthday is entered in your app profile so you are eligible for any birthday offer 7 Brew runs. When a birthday offer is available, redeem it like any other reward from the Rewards tab.

To claim it, add your birthdate to your app profile before your birthday — check the Rewards tab during your birthday month for any birthday offer. Birthday perks are not listed on the official Rewards page, so availability and terms can vary. Like other 7 Brew rewards, it’s tied to your account and can only be redeemed by scanning the app at the drive-thru window; it isn’t available to guests without the app. Terms can change, so confirm the current birthday reward details in the app before your visit.

Referral Code Promo & Bonus Offers

7 Brew occasionally offers a referral code promo that rewards both you and a friend with bonus points or a free drink when they sign up using your link. Check the app's "Refer a Friend" section to see if a referral program is currently active. Promo codes from social media campaigns or local events can also be entered in the app for bonus rewards.

7 Brew Rewards Not Working: Troubleshooting Common Issues

If your points or rewards are not showing up, start with these steps: first, make sure the app is updated to the latest version. Force-close and reopen it. Check that your account is logged in and your selected stand is correct. Points may take up to 24 hours to appear after a purchase. If points are missing after that window, contact 7 Brew support with your receipt and stand location. Common issues include using a different payment method than the one linked to your account, or purchasing items that no longer earn points (energy cans and kids-sized drinks were removed from the points program in August 2026).

Is the 7 Brew Rewards Program Worth It?

The 7 Brew app is free to download on iOS and Android. The rewards program works per drink — 100 points for each qualifying drink, with a free drink available at 1,000 points. There are no membership fees. The app tracks your balance in the Rewards tab, and you choose when to redeem.

Beyond points, the app gives you mobile ordering — you can build your drink, customize it with any syrup or milk option, and have it ready when you pull up to the window. This is especially useful at busy stands where the drive-thru line can stretch during morning rush. The app also sends push notifications for Jackpot Day deals on the 7th of each month.

Tips for Maximizing Your 7 Brew Rewards

Points are the same for every size, so save your free-drink reward for a large (32 oz) or a pricier custom order to get the most value from it. Stack your rewards by ordering on Jackpot Day for the monthly promotion plus your regular points. If you are new to the menu, browse the best drinks ranking or check the calorie calculator before placing your mobile order. You can also explore the secret menu — custom drinks order through the app just like standard menu items, as long as you add the individual syrups and modifications manually.
