7 Brew App & Rewards: Ordering Ahead and Points

Find the official app, understand manual reward redemption and check the stand before placing an order.

Ordering ahead

7 Brew offers an app for iOS and Android. Browse without signing in; an account is needed to place an order. Select the correct stand before customizing your drink, then check in and pay at the drive-thru. Official app and rewards information.

How points work

Qualifying drinks earn 100 points. At 1,000 points, a free-drink reward is an option; redemption is no longer automatic. Energy cans and kids-sized drinks stopped earning points on August 12, 2026. Points and free drinks expire after 365 consecutive days without a purchase. Check current terms before redeeming.

Check the stand before submitting

Compare the street address, city and state in the app with the location you intend to visit. A nearby city may have multiple stands, and a familiar road name can occur in another state. Find official stand links.

Having trouble with your account?

Use 7 Brew support for account or order help. Describe the issue and the selected stand. Never send account passwords or verification codes to this independent website. We cannot view points, place orders or reset a brand account.

7 Brew Loyalty Program: How to Earn & Redeem Rewards Points

The 7 Brew loyalty program lets you rewards points earn redeem on qualifying purchases made through the app or by scanning at the window. You earn 10 points per dollar spent on eligible drinks. Once you accumulate enough, you can redeem for a free drink. So how many points free drink does it take? Currently, 1,000 points unlocks one free standard-size drink of your choice — that is roughly 00 in purchases for one freebie.

The program does not currently advertise formal reward tiers levels like some competitors, but 7 Brew occasionally runs bonus-point promotions that let you earn faster. Keep an eye on push notifications and in-app banners for double- or triple-point days.

Mobile App Download: Get the 7 Brew App

The mobile app download is available on both App Store Google Play platforms. Search "7 Brew" and look for the official app with the red logo. The app lets you browse the full menu, customize drinks, save favorites, track your points balance, and use mobile order ahead to skip the line at your local stand.

When you first download, you may receive a sign-up bonus offer — typically a free drink or bonus points on your first purchase. Link a credit or debit card for seamless payment in-app at the drive-thru window. The app also supports Apple Pay and Google Pay for contactless checkout.

Free Drink Birthday Reward

One of the most popular perks is the free drink birthday reward. Make sure your birthday is entered in your app profile — 7 Brew sends a complimentary drink reward during your birthday month. The birthday drink is valid for any size and any menu item, making it one of the best birthday freebies among drive-thru coffee chains. Just scan your app at the window to redeem.

Referral Code Promo & Bonus Offers

7 Brew occasionally offers a referral code promo that rewards both you and a friend with bonus points or a free drink when they sign up using your link. Check the app's "Refer a Friend" section to see if a referral program is currently active. Promo codes from social media campaigns or local events can also be entered in the app for bonus rewards.

Rewards Not Working Fix: Troubleshooting Common Issues

If your rewards not working fix starts with these steps: first, make sure the app is updated to the latest version. Force-close and reopen it. Check that your account is logged in and your selected stand is correct. Points may take up to 24 hours to appear after a purchase. If points are missing after that window, contact 7 Brew support with your receipt and stand location. Common issues include using a different payment method than the one linked to your account, or purchasing items that no longer earn points (energy cans and kids-sized drinks were removed from the points program in August 2026).

Is the 7 Brew Rewards Program Worth It?

The 7 Brew app is free to download on iOS and Android. The rewards program works on a points-per-dollar system — you earn points on every purchase and redeem them for free drinks once you hit the threshold. There are no membership tiers or monthly fees. Points accumulate automatically when you pay through the app, and the app tracks your progress toward your next reward.

Beyond points, the app gives you mobile ordering — you can build your drink, customize it with any syrup or milk option, and have it ready when you pull up to the window. This is especially useful at busy stands where the drive-thru line can stretch during morning rush. The app also sends push notifications for Jackpot Day deals on the 7th of each month.

Tips for Maximizing Your 7 Brew Rewards

Order the large (32 oz) size to earn more points per visit — you pay slightly more but the points-per-dollar ratio stays the same, and the large is the best value per ounce. Stack your rewards by ordering on Jackpot Day for the monthly promotion plus your regular points. If you are new to the menu, browse the best drinks ranking or check the calorie calculator before placing your mobile order. You can also explore the secret menu — custom drinks order through the app just like standard menu items, as long as you add the individual syrups and modifications manually.
