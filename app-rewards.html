7 Brew Rewards: How Points Work, How to Check Your Balance & Redeem

7 Brew Rewards gives members 100 points for every qualifying drink, and 1,000 points unlocks a free drink — so roughly every 10th drink is on the house. Here is how points work after the August 2026 changes, how to check your points balance and how to redeem a reward.

RedemptionManual — choose a reward in the app's Rewards tab

How 7 Brew Rewards points work

You earn points per drink, not per dollar. Every qualifying small, medium or large drink adds 100 points to your account, whatever it costs. Order through the app or scan your rewards code at the drive-thru window so the purchase is tied to your account.

Individual 7 Energy cans and bakery items earn 50 points each. Kids sizes are not mentioned in the official qualifying list, so do not count on them earning. Points and any unused free drinks expire if your account has no purchase for 365 consecutive days, so one visit a year keeps them active.

How to check your 7 Brew points balance

  1. Open the 7 Brew app and sign in to your rewards account.
  2. Tap the Rewards tab at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Your total points balance and the reward options you can afford are shown there.

You can also check at 7brew.com/rewards by signing in with your phone number, or ask a brewista to pull up your account with your phone number at the stand. This independent website can't see or change your balance. If a recent drink is missing, see rewards not working below.

How to redeem 7 Brew rewards

Rewards are no longer applied automatically when you reach 1,000 points. You decide when to spend them:

  1. Open the Rewards tab in the app.
  2. Choose the reward you want — a free drink, or keep saving for a merch reward.
  3. Redeem it on an app order, or have your code scanned at the window before you pay.

Because every drink earns the same 100 points, get the most from a free-drink reward by using it on your most expensive order.

Ordering ahead in the 7 Brew app

7 Brew offers an app for iOS and Android. Browse without signing in; an account is needed to place an order. Select the correct stand before customizing your drink, then check in and pay at the drive-thru. Official app and rewards information.

Check the stand before submitting

Compare the street address, city and state in the app with the location you intend to visit. A nearby city may have multiple stands, and a familiar road name can occur in another state. Find official stand links.

Having trouble with your account?

Use 7 Brew support for account or order help. Describe the issue and the selected stand. Never send account passwords or verification codes to this independent website. We cannot view points, place orders or reset a brand account.

7 Brew Loyalty Program: How to Earn & Redeem Rewards Points

The 7 Brew loyalty program lets you earn and redeem rewards points on qualifying drinks bought through the app or scanned at the window. Each qualifying drink earns 100 points. So how many points does a free drink take? 1,000 points unlocks a free drink — roughly one free drink for every 10 you buy.

There are no paid membership levels. Once you pass 1,000 points you can redeem a free drink or keep banking points for merch rewards, and 7 Brew occasionally runs bonus-point promotions that let you earn faster. Keep an eye on push notifications and in-app banners for double- or triple-point days.

Mobile App Download: Get the 7 Brew App

The mobile app download is available on both App Store Google Play platforms. Search "7 Brew" and look for the official app with the red logo (on the App Store it is rated 5.0 stars from about 119,000 ratings). The app lets you browse the full menu, customize drinks, save favorites, track your points balance, and use mobile order ahead to skip the line at your local stand.

There is no sign-up bonus. No official 7 Brew page offers a free drink or bonus points for downloading the app, whatever other sites claim. You also do not pay in the app. You order ahead, check in with the brewista when you arrive, and pay at the drive-thru like everyone else. Orders are same-day only, and any order not picked up is cleared at the end of the day.

Free Drink Birthday Reward

The birthday reward is real, and the official rule is simple. You need a registered 7 Brew Rewards account with your birthdate on it. The free birthday drink loads into your account automatically, and it is available for one week after your birthday.

If the birthdate on your account is wrong, you cannot fix it in the app. Send the contact form at 7brew.com/support with your rewards phone number and the correct birthdate, and the team will update it. One warning, because other guides get this wrong: the reward is not valid for your whole birthday month. It is one week after the day.

Referral Code Promo & Bonus Offers

Short answer: there is no official referral program to join. Nothing in the app, on the Rewards page or in 7 Brew's support answers mentions referral codes or a Refer a Friend scheme, checked October 2026. Sites advertising 7 Brew referral codes are not describing a real program. Real bonus points do exist, but they come from 7 Brew's own limited-time promotions, announced in the app and on its social channels.

7 Brew Rewards Not Working: Troubleshooting Common Issues

If your points or rewards are not showing up, start with these steps: first, make sure the app is updated to the latest version. Force-close and reopen it. Check that your account is logged in and your selected stand is correct. Points may take up to 24 hours to appear after a purchase. If points are missing after that window, contact 7 Brew support with your receipt and stand location. Common issues include using a different payment method than the one linked to your account, or buying items that earn fewer points (Energy cans and bakery items earn 50, not 100).

Is the 7 Brew Rewards Program Worth It?

The 7 Brew app is free to download on iOS and Android. The rewards program works per drink — 100 points for each qualifying drink, with a free drink available at 1,000 points. There are no membership fees. The app tracks your balance in the Rewards tab, and you choose when to redeem.

Beyond points, the app gives you mobile ordering — you can build your drink, customize it with any syrup or milk option, and have it ready when you pull up to the window. This is especially useful at busy stands where the drive-thru line can stretch during morning rush. The app also sends push notifications for Jackpot Day deals on the 7th of each month.

Tips for Maximizing Your 7 Brew Rewards

Points are the same for every size, so save your free-drink reward for a large (32 oz) or a pricier custom order to get the most value from it. Stack your rewards by ordering on Jackpot Day for the monthly promotion plus your regular points. November's Jackpot Day (Sat, Nov 7) is double points plus a 100,000 point sweepstakes: see the November drinks and deals post. If you are new to the menu, browse the best drinks ranking or check the calorie calculator before placing your mobile order. You can also explore the secret menu — custom drinks order through the app just like standard menu items, as long as you add the individual syrups and modifications manually.

Frequently asked questions

How do 7 Brew Rewards points work?+

Members earn 100 points for each qualifying small, medium or large drink. 7 Brew Energy cans and kids-size drinks do not earn points as of August 12, 2026.

How many points do you need for a free drink at 7 Brew?+

1,000 points unlocks a free drink of any size, which works out to roughly one free drink for every 10 qualifying drinks.

How do I check my 7 Brew points?+

Open the 7 Brew app, sign in and tap the Rewards tab at the bottom of the screen to see your total points balance.

How do I redeem 7 Brew rewards?+

Rewards are not applied automatically. Open the Rewards tab, choose a free drink or merch reward, and redeem it on an app order or at the drive-thru window.

Do 7 Brew points expire?+

Points and free drinks expire if there has been no purchase on your rewards account for 365 consecutive days.

Does 7 Brew have an app?+

Yes. The 7 Brew app is free on iPhone and Android, and it is where the rewards programme lives — points, your balance, redeeming a free drink and mobile ordering at participating stands.

How do I log in to 7 Brew Rewards?+

Rewards are part of the 7 Brew app rather than a separate website, so there is no browser login. Open the app, sign in with the phone number or email you registered, and your points are on the Rewards tab.

Do you get a free drink on your birthday at 7 Brew?+

You need a registered 7 Brew Rewards account with your birthdate on it. The free birthday drink loads into your account automatically and is available for one week after your birthday. If your birthdate is wrong, send the support form at 7brew.com with your rewards phone number and the correct date.

Do 7 Brew Energy cans earn rewards points?+

Yes, 50 points each. The official qualifying list gives individual 7 Energy cans and bakery items 50 points, while small, medium and large drinks earn 100 points each. Kids sizes are not mentioned in the official list.

How many points per dollar do you get at 7 Brew?+

None — 7 Brew counts drinks, not dollars. Every qualifying small, medium or large drink earns a flat 100 points whatever it costs, so a $4 drink and a $7 drink are worth the same. 1,000 points is a free drink, which works out to roughly every tenth drink.

Are 7 Brew Rewards points per drink or per order?+

Per drink. You earn 100 points for each standard drink, any size. Energy drinks and bakery items earn 50 points each. At 1,000 points you get a free drink, any size. The part that trips people up: give the phone number tied to your rewards account when you order, or the points have nowhere to land. If you visit daily and have never seen a reward, check the balance on that number first.

My points dropped from 800 to 0, then showed 200. Did someone use my account?+

Almost certainly not. That drop is the rollover. When your points reach 1,000, a free drink gets banked to your account and the counter starts over, and points past 1,000 stay as your new balance. So a balance that falls right after a purchase usually means a free drink is sitting in your account waiting. Check your rewards before you assume the worst, and the stand can confirm the banked drink is there.

Does my child need ID for the free birthday drink at 7 Brew?+

The birthday drink is a rewards perk, not a show-your-ID perk. Members with a birthdate on file in their rewards account get a free drink, any size, around their birthday, valid for about 7 days. That account route is the reliable way to claim it, for teens too. Whether a stand asks for ID on a walk-up request is up to that stand, which is why experiences in fan groups are all over the place. Set the birthdate on the account ahead of time and the drink is just there.
