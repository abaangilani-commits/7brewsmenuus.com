About 7 Brew in Columbus, Ohio

7 Brew Coffee has expanded its drive-thru-only footprint across the Columbus metro area with four stands serving the central Ohio market. Founded in Rogers, Arkansas in 2017, 7 Brew is known for its fast, friendly barista service and a menu built around espresso breve drinks — espresso poured over half-and-half instead of regular milk — along with energy drinks, Italian sodas, smoothies, and shakes.

The Columbus locations are spread across key corridors: the Sawmill Road stand near Polaris serves the northern suburbs, the North High Street stands cover the Clintonville and Worthington areas, and the Georgesville Road location anchors the west side. Most Columbus stands open between 5:30 and 6:00 AM and stay open until 11:00 PM seven days a week, making them one of the earliest coffee options in the city.

First-time visitors can explore the full 7 Brew menu with prices before pulling into the drive-thru lane. Popular starting orders include the Blondie (vanilla and caramel breve), the 7 Energy line for a non-coffee caffeine boost, and any drink from the secret menu. Every drink can be customized with sugar-free syrups or alternative milk options. Use the nutrition calculator to check calories before ordering, and download the 7 Brew app for rewards and mobile ordering.

Columbus coffee lovers comparing 7 Brew to other local drive-thru chains will notice the breve base gives drinks a richer, creamier texture than standard espresso drinks. Check our sizes and caffeine guide to compare the 16 oz small, 24 oz medium, and 32 oz large cups.
