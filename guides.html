Got Questions?

Guide Questions

How many 7 Brew guides are there?+

More than 70: one page per drink family and signature drink, plus the hubs for sizes and caffeine, sugar-free drinks, the secret menu and ordering. Every drink page carries the same structure: what it is, what it costs (estimated), the official calories, sugar and caffeine by size, allergens, a copycat recipe where one makes sense, and the questions people actually ask.

Are the prices and calories on these pages official?+

Split answer, and each page labels it. Calories, sugar and caffeine come from 7 Brew's official nutrition guide, quoted exactly. Prices are researched estimates, because 7 Brew does not publish national prices and every stand is independently owned. When a page gives you a dollar figure, treat it as a planning number, not a quote.

Which guide should I start with?+

If you are picking a drink, start with the best drinks ranking. If you are counting, start with the nutrition and calorie calculator page or the sizes and caffeine table. If you are avoiding sugar, the sugar-free guide is the honest one: it names the drinks where sugar-free has more calories than regular, and the ones where the sugar hides in the base.

How often are the guides updated?+

Whenever the official guide changes or a page's numbers are caught out. The current set was rebuilt against the October 2026 nutrition guide, including corrections most fan sites have not made: chai caffeine (it exists, 16 to 19 mg in a medium), matcha caffeine (17 mg, not 120) and the full smoothie and shake ranges.
