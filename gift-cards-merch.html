7 Brew Gift Cards: How to Buy, Redeem & Check Balance

How 7 Brew's gift card program works, where to redeem it, and where fans find 7 Brew tumblers and merch.

Quick Overview: Gift Cards at 7 Brew

7 Brew gift cards are physical cards that pay for drinks at participating U.S. stands. They are sold at stands and online, they never expire, and they cannot be used in the app. Here is every official rule, checked October 2026.

How to Buy a 7 Brew Gift Card

Three official routes. (1) Any stand: buy a physical card at the window. (2) Online at shop7brew.com: $10 and $25 physical cards, shipped by USPS Ground Advantage. Allow up to 7 business days for activation and mailing. Shipping is $6.99 on orders up to $50, $4.99 over $50, and free over $75. (3) The Kindness Pack at Sam's Club: 10 cards with $10 on each. 7 Brew also mentions select retailer partners without naming them, so for any other store, 7 Brew has not confirmed it. There are no digital eGift cards, and no $5 or any-amount cards, whatever third-party sites claim.

How to Redeem at the Drive-Thru Window

Hand the card over at the window like any other payment. Two limits matter: gift cards work in person at participating U.S. stands only, and they cannot be used as payment in the app. They also cannot be used to buy other gift cards. 7 Brew sometimes runs promotions tied to gift-card use (a free sticker offer ran one January), so it is worth asking what is on when you redeem. The current one runs Nov 27 to Dec 24: members earn a free drink reward for every $25 of gift cards bought in stand. Details in the November drinks and deals post.

Checking Your Gift Card Balance

Check your balance any time at rewards.7brew.com/giftcard, the official balance page. Any stand can also look it up for you. There is no official balance phone number, so treat any site publishing one with suspicion. Reloads happen at participating stands, and balances cannot be moved between cards. And the rule everyone asks about: no fees, and no expiration date.

7 Brew Merch & Tumblers

There are two kinds of 7 Brew merch, and they are easy to confuse. Retail merch is sold for money at the official shop, shop7brew.com: about 44 items, mostly apparel (T-shirts $25 to $30, crewnecks $50 to $60, hoodies up to $65, caps $28), plus the gift cards. Rewards merch is earned with points in the app instead: a Brew & Chill mini fridge, a 7 Originals letterman jacket, Founding Pour T-shirts, a baseball cap and a crewneck. 7 Brew does not publish the point prices, so check the app's Rewards tab. Resale listings on Etsy and similar sites are not official stock.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where can I buy a 7 Brew gift card?

At any stand, online at shop7brew.com ($10 and $25 physical cards, shipped by mail), and in the Kindness Pack of 10 cards at $10 each, sold at Sam's Club. There are no digital eGift cards.

How do I check my 7 Brew gift card balance?

At rewards.7brew.com/giftcard, the official balance page, or ask any stand to look it up for you. There is no official balance phone number.

Can I use a 7 Brew gift card to pay for a drive-thru order?

Yes, in person at participating U.S. stands. Just hand the card over at the window. Gift cards cannot be used in the app, and cannot be used to buy other gift cards.

Does 7 Brew sell digital eGift cards?

No. 7 Brew gift cards are physical only. Cards bought online are mailed to you. Third-party services offering to send a digital 7 Brew gift card are not selling an official 7 Brew card.

Where can I buy 7 Brew merch like tumblers and cups?

Retail merch is sold at the official shop, shop7brew.com (about 44 items, mostly apparel). A second set of merch, including a mini fridge and a letterman jacket, is earned with rewards points in the app. Etsy and resale listings are not official.

Can I reload or add money to an existing 7 Brew gift card?

Yes, at any participating stand. Reloads are not done in the app, and balances cannot be transferred between cards.

Do 7 Brew gift cards expire?

No. Officially, 7 Brew gift cards have no fees and no expiration date.

Is a 7 Brew gift card a good gift idea?

For anyone who already has a 7 Brew stand nearby, a gift card is a straightforward way to let them build their own custom order — pair it with our best drinks guide so they know what to try first.

Can I use a 7 Brew gift card in the app?

No. 7 Brew's official answer is that gift cards cannot be used as payment in the app at this time. App orders are paid at the drive-thru when the brewista checks you in.

Do I earn rewards points when I buy a gift card?

No. Points are earned when the card is redeemed, not when it is bought. Whoever spends the card should give their rewards phone number at the window so the points land on their account.

Can I buy 7 Brew gift cards at Walmart or Target?

7 Brew has not named Walmart or Target as sellers. Its official list is stands, shop7brew.com and select retailer partners, and the only retailer it names is Sam's Club, for the 10-card Kindness Pack.
