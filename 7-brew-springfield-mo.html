About 7 Brew in Springfield, Missouri

Springfield is one of 7 Brew's most saturated markets in the country, with eight or more drive-thru stands blanketing the city across Glenstone Avenue, Battlefield Road, Sunshine Street, Kansas Expressway, Western Avenue, and Buena Vista. That density means most Springfield residents are within a few minutes of a 7 Brew — a testament to how deeply the Arkansas-born chain has rooted itself in the Ozarks region since its 2017 founding in nearby Rogers, Arkansas.

Every Springfield stand serves the full 7 Brew menu: breve-style espresso drinks (espresso over half-and-half), 7 Energy drinks, Italian sodas, smoothies, hot chocolate, teas, and shakes. The breve base is what sets 7 Brew apart — it makes drinks naturally richer and creamier without extra flavor pumps. Check our sizes and caffeine guide for the 16 oz, 24 oz, and 32 oz breakdowns.

Springfield locals who have not tried 7 Brew yet should start with the Blondie or Brunette, or explore the secret menu for TikTok-trending custom drinks. Swap to sugar-free syrups for a keto-friendly version, and check the calorie calculator to compare options by size and milk choice.

Most Springfield stands open around 5:30 AM and close between 10:00 and 11:00 PM daily. Browse the best drinks ranking for our top picks, and grab a printable menu PDF to keep in your car. Download the 7 Brew app for rewards on every drive-thru visit.
