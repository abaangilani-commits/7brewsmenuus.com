7 Brew Smooth 7: White Chocolate & Irish Cream, Ingredients & Ordering

The Smooth 7 is 7 Brew's white chocolate and Irish cream breve, one of the 7 Originals. A medium iced runs 520 calories, 51 g of sugar and 120 mg of caffeine for about $5.25. It is one of the richest Originals, and the one fans ask about most, starting with "what is actually in it." Below: the official numbers by size, the sugar-free swap, and a copycat recipe.

What is in a Smooth 7?

Irish Cream + White Chocolate Breve. It is listed in 7 Brew’s 7 Originals collection. Official Smooth 7 listing.

A flavor name is not a full ingredient disclosure. Confirm the chosen milk, sauces and toppings when dietary restrictions matter.

What Does the Smooth 7 Taste Like?

The Smooth 7 lives up to its name. The first sip delivers soft, rounded white chocolate — creamy and sweet without the sharpness of candy. Then the Irish cream flavor comes in with a gentle, nutty warmth that adds vanilla-like depth to the drink. The espresso is there but never bitter, providing a grounding coffee presence underneath the sweetness. The finish leaves a light, creamy coat on your tongue, almost like melted ice cream with a subtle coffee aftertaste.

What makes the Smooth 7 stand out from drinks like the Blondie or Brunette is how understated it is — the flavors blend into one seamless, velvety profile rather than hitting you in distinct layers. The breve base of steamed half-and-half amplifies that silky texture. It is an excellent choice if you want something indulgent but not aggressively sweet. Order it iced on a warm day or hot when you want something cozy. For a lighter version, swap to oat milk and check the nutrition breakdown to find your ideal size.

How to order it

An example order is: “A medium iced Smooth 7, please.” Add your preferred sweetness, milk and topping choices before the order is finalized. The official menu lists hot, iced and frozen chiller preparations for the Originals. Ask the stand to confirm any substitution.

Quarter, half, regular and extra sweetness are menu options. “Half sweet” describes a flavor adjustment; it does not mean half the total calories.

Which coffee should you choose?

Consider Smooth 7 when you prefer Irish cream and white chocolate flavors in a creamy coffee.

Sweet & Salty combines salted caramel with white chocolate. Smooth 7 pairs white chocolate with Irish cream.

Is there a sugar-free Smooth 7 Breve?

Yes. Ask for sugar-free syrup and a medium drops 45 g of sugar and 120 calories, going by 7 Brew's published nutrition. The milk still carries some natural sugar, so it is low-sugar rather than zero. In full numbers: a sugar-free medium iced is 400 calories with 6 g of sugar, against 520 calories and 51 g of sugar in the regular.

Make a Smooth 7 at Home (Copycat Recipe)

Recipe searches are half the interest in this drink, and no two fan versions agree. This home copycat sticks to the stand's formula: white chocolate and Irish cream, espresso, and half-and-half instead of milk. The half-and-half is the "smooth" part. It is what makes the drink taste rounded instead of thin.

For one medium iced Smooth 7 (24 oz cup)

  • 2 shots of espresso (about 2 oz), hot
  • 1 oz white chocolate sauce
  • 1 oz Irish cream syrup (the flavoring syrup, non-alcoholic)
  • About 12 oz half-and-half
  • A full cup of ice
  1. Pour the white chocolate sauce and Irish cream syrup into the cup.
  2. Add the hot espresso and stir. The heat melts the sauce so nothing sinks to the bottom.
  3. Fill the cup with ice, then pour the half-and-half over the top.
  4. Stir once and drink.

Hot version: steam the half-and-half, skip the ice, same sauce, syrup and shots. Sugar-free version: swap in sugar-free white chocolate and Irish cream syrups. At the stand, the sugar-free medium runs 400 calories with 6 g of sugar, going by the official guide. No espresso machine? Use 2 oz of very strong coffee. The white chocolate and half-and-half carry the flavor.

Frequently Asked Questions About the Smooth 7

How many calories are in a 7 Brew Smooth 7?
A medium iced Smooth 7 has 520 calories. A small iced is 300 and a large iced is 600. Hot runs 400, 600 and 670 by size, and the frozen chiller is 370, 520 and 740. All figures are from 7 Brew's official nutrition guide.
What is in a 7 Brew Smooth 7?
White chocolate and Irish cream flavor, espresso, and half-and-half. It is a breve, so the base is half-and-half rather than milk, which is what makes it taste smooth and rounded. Fans sometimes call that base the smooth mix. It is just the breve build.
What does the Smooth 7 taste like?
The Smooth 7 tastes like white chocolate and Irish cream blended into a velvety espresso drink. It is one of 7 Brew's smoothest signature drinks — soft, creamy, and subtly sweet without strong individual flavor spikes.
How much caffeine is in a 7 Brew Smooth 7?
A small or medium Smooth 7 has 120 mg of caffeine (2 espresso shots). A large has 240 mg (4 shots). The frozen chiller runs higher, 165 to 329 mg by size.
How much does a 7 Brew Smooth 7 cost?
About $4.50 for a small, $5.25 for a medium and $6.25 for a large. Prices are estimates and vary by stand.
Can the Smooth 7 be sugar free?
Yes. Order it with sugar-free syrups and a medium iced drops from 51 g of sugar to 6 g, and from 520 calories to 400. It stays a rich drink, because the half-and-half base does not change.
Is there alcohol in a Smooth 7?
No. The Irish cream in a Smooth 7 is a flavored syrup, not Irish cream liqueur. There is no alcohol in the drink.
What is the difference between a Smooth 7 and a Blondie?
Flavor and numbers. The Blondie is caramel and vanilla: 480 calories and 38 g of sugar in a medium iced. The Smooth 7 is white chocolate and Irish cream: 520 calories and 51 g of sugar. Both are half-and-half breves with 120 mg of caffeine in a medium. Pick the Blondie for caramel, the Smooth 7 for the softer, creamier cup.
How do I make a Smooth 7 at home?
For a medium iced: 1 oz white chocolate sauce and 1 oz Irish cream syrup, 2 shots of espresso, and about 12 oz of half-and-half over ice. The full copycat recipe is in the recipe section on this page.
