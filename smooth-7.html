7 Brew Smooth 7: Ingredients, Ordering & Drink Comparisons

Explore the Smooth 7 flavor combination, how to order it, alternatives and where to check the correct calories and caffeine for your preparation.

What is in a Smooth 7?

Irish Cream + White Chocolate Breve. It is listed in 7 Brew’s 7 Originals collection. Official Smooth 7 listing.

A flavor name is not a full ingredient disclosure. Confirm the chosen milk, sauces and toppings when dietary restrictions matter.

What Does the Smooth 7 Taste Like?

The Smooth 7 lives up to its name. The first sip delivers soft, rounded white chocolate — creamy and sweet without the sharpness of candy. Then the Irish cream flavor comes in with a gentle, nutty warmth that adds vanilla-like depth to the drink. The espresso is there but never bitter, providing a grounding coffee presence underneath the sweetness. The finish leaves a light, creamy coat on your tongue, almost like melted ice cream with a subtle coffee aftertaste.

What makes the Smooth 7 stand out from drinks like the Blondie or Brunette is how understated it is — the flavors blend into one seamless, velvety profile rather than hitting you in distinct layers. The breve base of steamed half-and-half amplifies that silky texture. It is an excellent choice if you want something indulgent but not aggressively sweet. Order it iced on a warm day or hot when you want something cozy. For a lighter version, swap to oat milk and check the nutrition breakdown to find your ideal size.

How to order it

An example order is: “A medium iced Smooth 7, please.” Add your preferred sweetness, milk and topping choices before the order is finalized. The official menu lists hot, iced and frozen chiller preparations for the Originals. Ask the stand to confirm any substitution.

Quarter, half, regular and extra sweetness are menu options. “Half sweet” describes a flavor adjustment; it does not mean half the total calories.

Which coffee should you choose?

Consider Smooth 7 when you prefer Irish cream and white chocolate flavors in a creamy coffee.

Sweet & Salty combines salted caramel with white chocolate. Smooth 7 pairs white chocolate with Irish cream.

Frequently Asked Questions About the Smooth 7

How many calories are in a 7 Brew Smooth 7?
A medium (24 oz) Smooth 7 breve has approximately 430 calories. The small (16 oz) is around 285 calories and the large (32 oz) is roughly 575 calories. Ordering with oat milk or almond milk instead of half-and-half lowers the calorie count.
What does the Smooth 7 taste like?
The Smooth 7 tastes like white chocolate and Irish cream blended into a velvety espresso drink. It is one of 7 Brew's smoothest signature drinks — soft, creamy, and subtly sweet without strong individual flavor spikes.
What is the difference between a Smooth 7 and a Blondie?
The Blondie is caramel-vanilla forward and slightly sweeter, while the Smooth 7 uses white chocolate and Irish cream for a more understated, silky profile. Both are breve-style drinks, but the Smooth 7 feels more mellow and the Blondie more dessert-like.
