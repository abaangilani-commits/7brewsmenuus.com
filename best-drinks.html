Best Drinks by Who You Are

First Time at 7 Brew? Start Here

Not sure what to order first time? Recommended for beginners: if you have never been to 7 Brew, order the Blondie (iced, medium). It is the most approachable drink on the menu — vanilla and caramel over espresso — and almost everyone likes it. If you do not drink coffee, ask for an Ocean Breeze 7 Energy (blue raspberry and coconut, no espresso). Both are crowd-pleasers that show what 7 Brew does best.

Best for Coffee Lovers

Looking for the best coffee drink at 7 Brew? If you already drink espresso and want something bold, try the Brunette for a rich mocha-hazelnut experience, or the Smooth 7 for creamy white chocolate depth. Both use the breve base (espresso plus half-and-half) that gives 7 Brew drinks their signature richness. For a less sweet option, ask for a cold brew with one flavor shot — vanilla or hazelnut work well.

Best for Non-Coffee Drinkers

7 Brew has a full lineup of non-coffee alternatives that does not involve espresso — the best energy drink options and caffeine-free picks and non coffee alternatives are listed here. The 7 Energy drinks come in fruit flavors like Ocean Breeze, Nightshade (blue raspberry, pomegranate, lavender), and Sunrise. 7 Fizz Italian sodas are caffeine-free. Matcha lattes offer a gentler caffeine source. And the smoothies and shakes are completely caffeine-free dessert drinks.

Best Sugar-Free and Keto Orders

7 Brew offers sugar-free syrups in vanilla, caramel, hazelnut, Irish cream, coconut, peach, strawberry, raspberry, and brown sugar cinnamon — all sweetened with sucralose at no extra charge. For a keto-friendly order, ask for a sugar-free Blondie with heavy cream instead of breve (under 1g net carbs) or a sugar-free vanilla cold brew (roughly 25–50 calories, zero sugar). Avoid cold foam, caramel drizzle, and standard Chiller bases, which add hidden sugars.

Best for Kids

Order a strawberry smoothie or a 7 Fizz Italian soda — both are caffeine-free and fruit-flavored. For older kids who want something fancier, a vanilla steamer (steamed milk with vanilla syrup, no espresso) or a small Chiller in a dessert flavor works well. Always confirm caffeine content with the brewista before ordering for children.

Best 7 Brew drinks by caffeine level

Flavour is only half the decision. The other half is whether the drink will keep you up, and 7 Brew's range runs from nothing at all to more than a double espresso. These are medium sizes, from 7 Brew's published nutrition data — check any drink and size yourself in the caffeine calculator.

None at all — 0 mg

Sixteen drinks have no caffeine whatsoever: every lemonade, the 7 Fizz sodas, and the chai lattes. These are the ones to order after dinner, or for anyone who reacts badly to caffeine. The Fruit Roll-Up Lemonade and Key Lime Pie Lemonade are the most interesting of them.

Light — under 60 mg

The tea menu lives here. Earl Grey and Cinnamon Spice come in around 11 mg, the decaf teas at roughly 1 mg, and the Strawberry Matcha Latte at 17 mg. A matcha is the usual pick for someone who wants something without a coffee hit.

Standard — 60 to 139 mg

This is where most of the famous drinks sit. The Blondie, Brunette, Smooth 7, Sweet & Salty, Cinnamon Roll, German Chocolate, Cookie Butter and Banana Bread are all 120 mg in a medium — two shots of espresso. The 7 Energy flavours are slightly below that at 105 mg, which surprises people who assume an energy drink beats coffee. It does not.

Strong — 140 mg and up

The flavoured Americanos run 180 mg. The cold brews are the serious end: Blondie, Brunette and White Mac Cold Brew all land at 246 mg, and a plain Cold Brew at 268 mg — two-thirds of the 400 mg daily ceiling US health guidance suggests for healthy adults, in one medium cup.

So the short version: if you want the flavour without the jolt, order any of the signature breves as a small, or pick from the lemonade and tea side. If you are ordering coffee to stay awake, cold brew is roughly twice the caffeine of the drink next to it on the board.

This ranking combines three factors: ordering frequency data referenced across multiple independent 7 Brew fan communities and review sites, social media mentions and viral popularity (particularly TikTok and Reddit), and barista recommendations collected from drive-thru ordering guides. Ordering for someone else

Start by asking whether they want coffee, a caffeinated non-coffee drink or another drink category. Then ask about preferred flavors and milk. A colorful drink is not enough information to judge its caffeine, ingredients or suitability.

Want a less sweet order?

Ask about half or quarter sweetness, then check which parts of the recipe that changes. Choosing a different milk or skipping a topping can also change the drink, but the total nutrition needs a matching recipe. Read the nutrition guide.

Is 7 Brew Worth It? An Honest Take

The two most common criticisms of 7 Brew online are that it's “too sweet” and that it's pricier than a regular cup of drip coffee. Both are fair as defaults, and both are easy to fix. The sweetness comes from 7 Brew's default breve style — half-and-half plus flavored syrups — rather than any single drink being unusually sugary; asking for half sweet, sugar-free syrup, or a swap to oat or almond milk noticeably cuts the sweetness without changing the flavor profile. See our customization guide for the exact wording to use.

On price, a 7 Brew specialty drink generally lands in the same range as a comparable specialty drink at Dutch Bros or Starbucks — see our side-by-side price comparison — rather than being priced against plain drip coffee, which will always look cheaper by comparison. Whether it's “worth it” mostly comes down to what you're comparing it to: against a fast, customizable specialty drink from a drive-thru competitor, 7 Brew holds up well on price and speed; against a $2 gas-station coffee, it obviously costs more.

Best 7 Brew drinks FAQ

Is 7 Brew overpriced?

7 Brew's specialty drinks are generally priced in line with comparable drinks at Dutch Bros and Starbucks, not against plain drip coffee. Whether it feels overpriced depends on what you're comparing it to.

Why do some people say 7 Brew is too sweet?

7 Brew's default breve style uses half-and-half plus flavored syrups, which reads sweeter than a plain coffee. Asking for half sweet, sugar-free syrup, or a milk swap to oat or almond cuts the sweetness while keeping the flavor.

What should I order first at 7 Brew?

The Blondie, iced, in a medium. It is the most-ordered drink on the menu and the one the whole chain is built around: vanilla and caramel over espresso and half-and-half. If you do not drink coffee, start with an Ocean Breeze 7 Energy instead.

What is the best non-coffee drink at 7 Brew?

For caffeine without coffee, the Ocean Breeze 7 Energy (blue raspberry and coconut). With no caffeine at all, the Pink Mermaid 7 Fizz: watermelon, strawberry and coconut over sparkling soda.

What are the 7 Originals at 7 Brew?

The seven signature drinks 7 Brew launched with: the Blondie, Smooth 7, Brunette, Sweet & Salty, Cinnamon Roll, Banana Bread and Cookie Butter. Seven of the ten drinks ranked above are Originals.

What is the best 7 Brew drink that is not too sweet?

Order any breve or mocha half sweet, which is one tap on the order screen and cuts the syrup in half. The Brunette reads least sweet as-is because the dark mocha and hazelnut balance the sugar. Unsweetened teas and Americanos sit at the dry end of the menu.
