7 Brew Cup Sizes (oz) & Caffeine Guide (2026)

For iced drinks and chillers, 7 Brew cup sizes are Small (16 oz), Medium (24 oz), and Large (32 oz). Hot drink cups are sized at Small (12 oz), Medium (16 oz), and Large (20 oz). Standard espresso drinks get 2 shots in small and medium cups, and 4 shots in large cups. Caffeine totals range from 120 mg in a small latte up to 362 mg in a large Mocha Frozen Chiller. Scroll down to view visual cup comparison cards, espresso extraction mechanics, and our caffeine half-life metabolism timeline below.

One honesty note on ounces: 7 Brew does not publish cup volumes anywhere, not on its site and not in the nutrition guide. The 16/24/32 iced and 12/16/20 hot figures are the barista-reported volumes every stand works with, and every serious guide lands on the same numbers. The kids cup volume is not published at all. It is reported around 10 to 12 oz, so treat that one as approximate.

7 Brew small vs medium vs large: which size is actually worth it?

People usually ask this as "is the large worth it" — but the two jumps are not the same kind of upgrade, and knowing which is which makes the choice easy. Averaged across 116 iced drinks on the menu:

UpgradeExtra ouncesExtra caloriesExtra caffeineExtra sugar
Small → Medium+8 oz (16 → 24)+78+13 mg+10 g
Medium → Large+8 oz (24 → 32)+60+56 mg+10 g

Small vs medium

Going small to medium buys you mostly drink, not coffee. You gain 8 oz and about 78 calories, but only around 13 mg of extra caffeine, because most drinks pour the same two espresso shots into both cups. If you want a bigger drink that hits the same, the medium is the one.

Medium vs large

The large is the opposite. The same 8 oz step adds fewer calories than the first one — about 60 — but roughly 56 mg more caffeine, because this is usually where an extra shot goes in. A Blondie Breve is 120 mg in a medium and 240 mg in a large: double.

The practical rule. Order the medium if you want more to drink. Order the large if you want more caffeine — that is the jump where it actually arrives. And if you are watching sugar rather than caffeine, both steps cost you about the same 10 g, so the small is the only real saving.

Hot cups do not follow the same ounces: a hot large is 20 oz, not 32. Compare any specific drink across all four sizes in the size comparison tool above.

FDA Daily Caffeine Guidelines

The US Food and Drug Administration (FDA) recommends that healthy adults limit daily caffeine intake to 400 milligrams per day (roughly 4 to 5 standard cups of drip coffee). If you order a large Mocha Frozen Chiller (362 mg) or a large cold brew (357 mg), you are reaching your full daily threshold in a single beverage. If you are sensitive to caffeine or ordering in the late afternoon, consider decaf espresso or a caffeine-free 7 Fizz soda.

Extraction Science

Espresso Extraction Mechanics: Why 7 Brew Uses Double & Quad Pulls

The Double Shot Pull (Small & Medium)

Both Small (16 oz iced / 12 oz hot) and Medium (24 oz iced / 16 oz hot) beverages receive a full 2-shot double pull. This extracts approximately 2 fluid ounces of concentrated espresso with a dense tiger-stripe crema, providing 120 mg of caffeine and balanced dark chocolate notes.

The Quad Shot Pull (Large 32 oz)

Large drinks (32 oz iced / 20 oz hot) automatically receive a quad pull (4 full shots). This delivers 240 mg of caffeine. The extra shots ensure that as ice melts inside a massive 32-ounce cup, the rich coffee flavor never gets watered down or overwhelmed by sweet half-and-half breve cream.

⏱️ Caffeine Metabolism: Absorption & Half-Life Timeline

Clinical Pharmacokinetics
Timeline Phase Time Elapsed Physiological Impact Recommended Action
Initial Absorption 10 to 15 minutes Caffeine crosses into bloodstream through stomach lining and small intestine. Initial alertness boost; heart rate gently elevates.
Peak Plasma Concentration 30 to 45 minutes Maximum adenosine receptor blockade in the brain; peak energy, focus, and dopamine signaling. Optimal window for workouts, long road trips, or demanding cognitive work.
First Half-Life (50% Cleared) 4 to 5 hours Liver CYP1A2 enzyme metabolizes half the caffeine into paraxanthine, theobromine, and theophylline. Residual 60 mg from a Small or 120 mg from a Large remains active.
Second Half-Life (75% Cleared) 8 to 10 hours Around 25% of the original dose remains circulating in the central nervous system. Recommended cut-off window before bedtime to preserve deep REM sleep cycles.
Complete Clearance 12 to 14 hours Over 90% eliminated by the kidneys for average healthy adult metabolizers. Body resets baseline adenosine sensitivity for restorative overnight rest.

Which 7 Brew Size Is the Best Value?

The large (32 oz) is the best value price per ounce at roughly $0.20/oz versus $0.28/oz for the small. It also jumps from 2 espresso shots to 4, making it the only size with a true quad shot — double the caffeine for about $1.75 more than the small. However, the large also carries the most calories and sugar. Before you upsize upsell yourself on the large, consider that it also packs more sugar, so the medium (24 oz) is a better balance of value and moderation for daily drinkers.

Compared to Starbucks, 7 Brew runs roughly $1.00–$1.50 cheaper at equivalent sizes. The large has no true Starbucks equivalent — Starbucks Trenta (30 oz) is available only for cold drinks and does not include espresso, while the 7 Brew large packs 4 shots of espresso in 32 oz.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions: 7 Brew Cup Sizes & Caffeine

Straightforward answers about fluid ounces, espresso shot counts, and caffeine safety.

How many ounces are in a 7 Brew large iced drink? +

A Large iced drink at 7 Brew is 32 fluid ounces. In comparison, a Medium is 24 fluid ounces, and a Small is 16 fluid ounces. For hot drinks, sizes are 12 oz (Small), 16 oz (Medium), and 20 oz (Large).

How many shots of espresso are in 7 Brew sizes? +

A Small (12 oz hot or 16 oz iced) contains 2 shots. A Medium (16 oz hot or 24 oz iced) contains 2 shots. A Large (20 oz hot or 32 oz iced) contains 4 shots (a quad pull). The Triple 7 comes standard with 6 espresso shots. Explore cup dimensions and menu classifications on the official 7 Brew menu (7brew.com) ↗.

Which 7 Brew drink has the most caffeine? +

A large Mocha Frozen Chiller, at 362 mg from the official guide. A large cold brew is right behind it at 357 mg. The famous Triple 7 carries 6 espresso shots, which at the guide’s 60 mg per shot works out near 360 mg, so the chiller still edges it. On the energy menu, a large 7 Energy has 133 mg.

How does 7 Brew cup sizing compare to Starbucks? +

7 Brew offers larger iced volumes across the board. A 7 Brew Medium is 24 oz (larger than a Starbucks 16 oz Grande) and a 7 Brew Large is 32 oz (larger than a Starbucks 24 oz Venti iced).

Can I request extra espresso shots or decaf at 7 Brew? +

Yes. You can add extra espresso shots to any beverage for approximately $0.75 per double shot pull. You can also request any classic coffee or breve prepared decaf with zero added fee.

What sizes does 7 Brew have?+

Three: small, medium and large. Iced drinks are 16, 24 and 32 oz; hot drinks are 12, 16 and 20 oz. Kids sizes are also available on some drinks.

What is the largest size at 7 Brew?+

A large — 32 oz iced or 20 oz hot. A large espresso drink is pulled with four shots rather than the two in a small or medium.

How much caffeine is in a 7 Energy compared to Alani or Celsius?+

A drive-thru 7 Energy has 67 mg of caffeine in a small, 105 mg in a medium, and 133 mg in a large, from the official nutrition guide. A typical Alani or Celsius can has 200 mg. So even a large 7 Energy carries about a third less caffeine, and a small or medium is a much gentler option if strong energy drinks bother you. For comparison, a medium iced coffee at 7 Brew, built on two espresso shots, has 120 mg.

How much caffeine is in a large Hawaiian Punch at 7 Brew?+

133 mg. Hawaiian Punch is a flavor build on the 7 Energy base, so the caffeine comes from the base, not the flavor. It follows size: 67 mg in a small, 105 mg in a medium, 133 mg in a large. Every 7 Energy flavor at the same size has the same caffeine.

What can I order at 7 Brew that has caffeine but is not carbonated?+

Go to the coffee and tea side. Iced coffees, cold brew, and breves are not carbonated, and a medium iced coffee drink has about 120 mg of caffeine from two espresso shots. Teas and chai are not carbonated either and run much lower. Plain lemonade and shakes carry no caffeine on their own. One trick from regulars: an energy drink ordered as a frozen chiller loses most of its fizz in the blending, so it drinks smooth while keeping its caffeine.

Are 7 Brew's cup ounces official?+

No, and any page that presents them as official is skipping a step. 7 Brew does not publish cup volumes on its website or in its nutrition guide. The 16, 24 and 32 oz iced cups and 12, 16 and 20 oz hot cups are the volumes baristas report and every stand works with, which is why independent guides agree on them. The kids cup is the exception: its volume is published nowhere and is only reported around 10 to 12 oz.

Why does a medium coffee have the same caffeine as a small?+

Because both cups get the same 2 espresso shots. The extra 8 ounces in a medium is milk, breve cream, ice and syrup, not coffee. That is why the caffeine table above reads 120 mg, 120 mg, then 240 mg at the large, where the shot count finally doubles to 4. If caffeine is what you are paying for, a medium espresso drink is the worst value on the board. Size up to a large, switch to cold brew, where every step adds caffeine, or add a shot to the small.
