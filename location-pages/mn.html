7 Brew Minnesota FAQ

How many 7 Brew locations are in Minnesota?

Minnesota has 9 7 Brew stands in 9 cities, ranking #28 of the 38 states with 7 Brew (official locator, checked September 21, 2026).

Which city in Minnesota has the most 7 Brew stands?

Apple Valley, Bemidji, Duluth, Maplewood, Moorhead, St. Cloud, Vadnais Heights, Willmar and Winona are tied for the most, with 1 stand each.

What are 7 Brew hours in Minnesota?

Hours vary by stand in Minnesota. The most common schedule (8 of 9 stands) is Mon–Thu: 5:30 AM–10:00 PM; Fri–Sat: 5:30 AM–11:00 PM; Sun: 5:30 AM–10:00 PM. The earliest listed opening is 5:30 AM. Always check the hours for your stand before you go.
