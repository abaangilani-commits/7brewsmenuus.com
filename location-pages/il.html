7 Brew Illinois FAQ

How many 7 Brew locations are in Illinois?

Illinois has 37 7 Brew stands in 33 cities, ranking #10 of the 38 states with 7 Brew (official locator, checked September 21, 2026).

Which city in Illinois has the most 7 Brew stands?

Bloomingdale, Champaign, Decatur and Joliet are tied for the most, with 2 stands each.

What are 7 Brew hours in Illinois?

All 37 stands keep the same hours: Mon–Thu: 5:30 AM–10:00 PM; Fri–Sat: 5:30 AM–11:00 PM; Sun: 5:30 AM–10:00 PM. Always check the hours for your stand before you go.
