What the numbers mean

The calculator covers 24 of the most-ordered builds — the 7 Originals, classics, energy, fizz, tea and matcha drinks — at the size, milk and sweetness you choose. Two things move them most:

  • The base. 7 Brew's signature drinks are breves, made with half-and-half — that is where most of the calories live. Swapping to skim, almond or oat milk is the single biggest cut.
  • The syrup. Full-sugar syrups carry the sugar and carbs. Sugar-free versions are zero-calorie, so a sugar-free build on a light milk lands far lower than the standard recipe.

Prices are our researched estimates, not official figures. Every 7 Brew is independently owned, so your local price can differ. See the full menu with prices for the current estimates by category.

Calculator FAQ

How accurate is the 7 Brew calculator?

Calories, carbs, sugar, protein, fat and caffeine come from 7 Brew's own published nutrition guide, so they match the brand's figures for a standard build. Prices are researched estimates and vary by stand, and any custom syrup pumps a barista adds will shift the numbers.

Does it include sugar-free and milk swaps?

Yes. Switch the milk or drop to half sweetness and the calories, carbs and sugar update. Sugar-free syrups are zero-calorie, so they mostly change the sugar and carb lines.

Can I use it to count carbs for keto?

Yes — it shows net carbs alongside total carbs. An unsweetened cold brew or a sugar-free energy build lands in single-digit net carbs; anything on the breve base will not.

Does 7 Brew publish calories in store?

Not on the drive-thru board. 7 Brew publishes a nutrition guide PDF instead, which is where the numbers in this calculator come from.
