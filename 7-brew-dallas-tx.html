About 7 Brew in Dallas, Texas

7 Brew Coffee entered the Dallas–Fort Worth metroplex as part of its aggressive Texas expansion, with the Coit Road stand in far north Dallas serving as the city's first location. The Arkansas-born drive-thru chain — founded in Rogers in 2017 and now operating 777-plus stands nationwide — has announced plans for additional DFW locations, including converted former Salad and Go sites along Ross Avenue.

Dallas-area customers can expect 7 Brew's signature breve-style espresso drinks, where half-and-half replaces regular milk for a richer, creamier coffee. The full menu spans hot and iced espresso drinks, blended shakes, 7 Energy drinks, Italian sodas, and smoothies. Browse the complete 7 Brew menu with current prices to plan your order.

With Texas heat driving demand for cold drinks most of the year, the most popular Dallas orders tend to be iced breves and blended options. Try the Blondie over ice, a frozen Smooth 7, or any of the secret menu creations trending on TikTok. Every order can be adjusted with sugar-free syrups at no extra charge — check our calorie calculator to customize.

DFW commuters should note that most 7 Brew stands open as early as 5:30 AM, making them a convenient stop before hitting I-635 or the Dallas North Tollway. Compare cup sizes and caffeine content to pick the right size for your morning drive.
