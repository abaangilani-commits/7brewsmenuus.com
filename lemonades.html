The best 7 Brew lemonade flavors and combos

All five lemonades are caffeine-free, so this choice is purely about taste and sugar. Medium sizes, from 7 Brew's published nutrition data:

LemonadeCaloriesSugarBest for
Classic Lemonade20049 gthe lightest on the menu by a clear margin
Pink Paradise27065 gthe crowd-pleaser — strawberry and coconut
Fruit Roll-Up27066 gthe sweetest-tasting, and the one kids ask for
Key Lime Pie28066 gthe most unusual — tart rather than sweet
Cocoberry28068 gcoconut and berry, the richest of the five

If you only try one: Pink Paradise is the safest first order. Key Lime Pie is the one worth trying if you already know you dislike sweet drinks — it is the only genuinely tart option.

Combos worth asking for

Lemonades take the same flavour shots as everything else, which is where the combinations come from. Three that work:

  • Half classic, half Pink Paradise — keeps the strawberry but drops roughly 8 g of sugar against a full Pink Paradise.
  • Classic lemonade with peach — the closest thing on the board to a standard peach lemonade, and lighter than any of the pre-mixed flavours.
  • Cocoberry, ordered sugar-free — the only lemonade with a published sugar-free version. It still carries 45 g of sugar in a medium, because the sugar lives in the lemonade base itself. See the sugar-free checker before assuming a swap makes a lemonade sugar-free.

Worth knowing before you order: even the lightest lemonade here carries 49 g of sugar in a medium, which is more than a can of cola. Ordering a small is the single biggest reduction available.

Customizing your order

A sugar-free flavor substitution does not establish that the lemonade base contains no sugar. Check the complete drink rather than the syrup alone.

Tell the stand the drink base, size, flavor combination and any requested adjustments. Ask about availability before assuming a flavor or topping can be substituted. Browse flavor and sweetness options.

Nutrition and prices

Match your drink to the official nutrition guide for the chosen serving and preparation. Ask the stand about recipes not covered there. Current prices depend on the selected location and order; confirm in the official app or at the stand.

Is 7 Brew lemonade fresh-squeezed? What the documents say

You will find both answers online, sometimes from the brand itself. Its social posts have used the words "fresh squeezed." Its product documents never do. The official menu and nutrition guide make no fresh-squeezed claim, and two official builds, Key Lime Pie and the Heatwave combo, list lemon concentrate as an ingredient. The guide's base is lemon concentrate and water. That does not make the drink bad. Concentrate is how nearly every high-volume lemonade program keeps the tartness consistent from stand to stand. It does mean the "fresh-squeezed" label belongs to marketing posts, not to the published recipe, and this page goes with the documents.

The five lemonades with no official numbers

The guide publishes entries for exactly six lemonades: the plain base, Cocoberry, Fruit Roll-Up, Key Lime Pie, Pink Paradise and the sugar-free Cocoberry. The other five grid flavors, Tropic Thunder, Watermelon, Blue Typhoon, Heatwave and Blackberry Cobbler, have no published calories or sugar at all. Tropic Thunder shows what fills the gap: fan sites put a medium anywhere from about 180 to 280 calories, with sugar figures that do not agree either. At most one of those numbers can be right, and there is no way to know which. This hub lists those five flavors with their official builds and no invented numbers, and each of the six documented drinks has a full page with its real figures.

One more trap in the same category: the format changes the drink more than the flavor does. A plain medium is 200 calories over ice and 630 as a Frozen Chiller. The flavored drinks jump by roughly 2.3 times, and the Lemon Freeze format runs heavier again. Any lemonade number quoted without its format is only half a number.

About 7 Brew Lemonades

7 Brew's flavored lemonade lineup includes both signature recipes and customizable bases. The core flavors — Pink Paradise, Tropic Thunder, Blackberry Cobbler, and classic lemonade — are built on the same lemonade base, which the official guide treats as lemon concentrate and water. Tasting Table tasted the six May 2026 launch drinks on day one in Columbus, Ohio, counted five hits against one miss (the Blackberry Cobbler Lemon Freeze), and named the Tropic Thunder Frozen Chiller a favorite.

Lemonades are a top non-coffee drink option and caffeine-free refreshment by default, Every lemonade comes in all three cup sizes (16 oz, 24 oz, 32 oz) and can be blended into a frozen slush on request.

Customizing Your 7 Brew Lemonade

The real depth of the lemonade menu comes from customization. Add Torani syrups in strawberry mango peach, coconut, and other fruit flavors to build your own combination, or go sugar-free with any of the available SF syrup options. Popular custom orders include the Strawberry Mango Lemonade (strawberry + mango syrups over classic lemonade) and the Coconut Paradise (coconut syrup added to Pink Paradise).

Calorie-wise, a plain lemonade is 140 calories in a small and 200 in a medium — significantly lighter than espresso breve drinks like the Blondie. Use the nutrition calculator to compare sizes, and check the secret menu for more custom lemonade creations. You can also browse the full 7 Brew menu with prices to compare across categories.

7 Brew Lemonade as a Summer Cold Drink

When the temperature rises, a 7 Brew lemonade is the ultimate summer cold drink. Served over ice in all three cup sizes, these fruity sour sweet refreshers balance tart citrus with just the right amount of sweetness. There is no sugar-free plain lemonade: the base itself is sweetened, and the only published sugar-free lemonade is the Cocoberry. Frequently Asked Questions: 7 Brew Lemonades

The base, the sugar, the frozen formats and the flavors with no published numbers, answered straight.

What does 7 Brew use for its lemonade?+

Lemon concentrate and water, per the official menu and nutrition guide. Two official builds, Key Lime Pie and the Heatwave combo, list lemon concentrate as an ingredient outright. The brand's social posts have called the lemonades fresh squeezed, but no menu or guide document makes that claim, and this page goes with the documents.

Does 7 Brew lemonade have caffeine?+

No. Every lemonade entry in the guide reads 0 mg, iced or frozen. Lemonade is one of the two big caffeine-free categories at the stand, alongside 7 Fizz. If a drink with a lemonade flavor has caffeine, it was built on the energy or tea base instead, and that makes it a different drink.

How many lemonade flavors does 7 Brew have?+

Eleven names: the plain base plus 10 grid cards (Fruit Roll-Up, Pink Paradise, Cocoberry, Key Lime Pie, Tropic Thunder, Watermelon, Blue Typhoon, Heatwave, Blackberry Cobbler and Kokomo), with a long suggested-combos list beyond those. But only six have published nutrition: the plain base, Cocoberry, Fruit Roll-Up, Key Lime Pie, Pink Paradise and the sugar-free Cocoberry. Pages quoting exact calories for Tropic Thunder Lemonade are estimating, and their estimates disagree with each other by about 100 calories.

Is there a sugar-free 7 Brew lemonade?+

Only one published version: the Sugar-Free Cocoberry, and it is a trap worth knowing about. A medium iced still has 190 calories and 45 g of sugar, because the sugar lives in the lemonade base itself, not just the flavor syrups. There is no sugar-free plain lemonade in the guide. The full trap list is on our sugar-free page.

How much sugar is in a 7 Brew lemonade?+

In a medium over ice: 49 g in the plain, up to 68 g in the Cocoberry, with the other published flavors between 65 and 68 g. Frozen formats roughly double it and more: a medium chiller carries about 150 g, because the frozen base is a different, sweeter build. Full size-by-size figures are on the lemonade page.

What is the difference between iced lemonade, a chiller and a Lemon Freeze?+

Format, and a lot of sugar. Iced is the standard build over ice. The Frozen Chiller blends the drink with a frozen base: a plain medium jumps from 200 to 630 calories. The Lemon Freeze is the heaviest format, richer and closer to a shake in build. Same flavor name, three different drinks on paper, so always compare numbers within one format.

Which 7 Brew lemonade is the best?+

The launch-day evidence points two ways. Tasting Table tasted the six May 2026 drinks in Columbus and named the Tropic Thunder Frozen Chiller a favorite, with the Blackberry Cobbler Lemon Freeze as the one miss. Fan threads put Pink Paradise at the top instead, and of the flavors with published numbers it is also one of the lighter mediums at 270 calories. There is no official sales ranking.

Can I get an Arnold Palmer at 7 Brew?+

Yes, as a custom order: half lemonade, half tea. It is not a board drink. In May 2026 the stand featured a ready-made version, the Fruit Roll-Up Green Tea Lemonade, which is half green tea and half lemonade with the strawberry and lavender build. It is not in the nutrition guide, so it has no official calories. The standard Fruit Roll-Up Lemonade on the grid has no tea in it.
