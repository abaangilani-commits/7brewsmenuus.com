About 7 Brew Lemonades

7 Brew's flavored lemonade lineup includes both signature recipes and customizable bases. The core flavors — Pink Paradise, Tropic Thunder, Blackberry Cobbler, and classic lemonade — are built on a real lemon juice squeezed base rather than powdered mix, giving them a tart, balanced flavor that holds up well with added syrups. Tasting Table ranked the Pink Paradise and Tropic Thunder as standout flavors in their 2025 review of the full lemonade lineup.

Lemonades are a top non-coffee drink option and caffeine-free refreshment by default, but you can add an energy shot to turn any lemonade into a caffeinated refresher — a popular warm-weather hack. Every lemonade comes in all three cup sizes (16 oz, 24 oz, 32 oz) and can be blended into a frozen slush on request.

Customizing Your 7 Brew Lemonade

The real depth of the lemonade menu comes from customization. Add Torani syrups in strawberry mango peach, coconut, and other fruit flavors to build your own combination, or go sugar-free with any of the available SF syrup options. Popular custom orders include the Strawberry Mango Lemonade (strawberry + mango syrups over classic lemonade) and the Coconut Paradise (coconut syrup added to Pink Paradise).

Calorie-wise, a plain lemonade in a small runs around 130–160 calories depending on the flavor — significantly lighter than espresso breve drinks like the Blondie. Use the nutrition calculator to compare sizes, and check the secret menu for more custom lemonade creations. You can also browse the full 7 Brew menu with prices to compare across categories.

7 Brew Lemonade as a Summer Cold Drink

When the temperature rises, a 7 Brew lemonade is the ultimate summer cold drink. Served over ice in all three cup sizes, these fruity sour sweet refreshers balance tart citrus with just the right amount of sweetness. Ask for a sugar-free lemonade if you want the same bright flavor with fewer calories — any base can be paired with sugar-free syrups for a lighter version.
