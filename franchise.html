What it actually costs to open a 7 Brew stand, how the franchise-heavy growth model works, and where to start the application process.

Franchise Cost Breakdown

Total initial investment for a 7 Brew franchise per the Franchise Disclosure Document issued May 11, 2026, is $940,500 to $2,283,500 for the first store, under a minimum 10-store development agreement. You pay $125,000 at signing: the $35,000 franchise fee for store one plus $90,000 in development payments toward stores two through ten, and each later store carries a further $25,000 fee when it opens. The widely copied $350,000 to $775,000 range matches no recent FDD. This range typically covers the franchise fee, real estate or lease build-out, drive-thru equipment, signage, initial inventory and opening costs — the wide spread reflects how much real estate and construction costs vary by market. As with any franchise, the authoritative source for current fees and terms is the brand's official Franchise Disclosure Document (FDD), which any serious applicant should review with a franchise attorney or accountant before signing.

Revenue Potential: What a Stand Actually Sells

7 Brew reported an average unit volume (AUV) of about $2.6 million across a 320-location comparison base for the 2025 full year. AUV reflects typical gross revenue per store, not owner take-home profit — actual profitability depends on your specific lease terms, labor costs, local competition and financing structure. A Franchise-Heavy Growth Model

Unlike chains that grow primarily through corporate-owned stores, 7 Brew leans heavily on franchisees: as of late 2025 the brand operated 578 franchised locations against just 24 corporate-owned stands. That mix, combined with a Blackstone growth investment, has powered explosive expansion — 281 new stands opened in 2025 alone (about as many as the prior two years combined), with the 2026 FDD projecting 437 franchised and 10 company openings in 2026, and 117 franchised stands already opened early that year. The official locator counted 979 stands across 38 states in October 2026, up from 38 stands in January 2023.

How to Apply (Applications Are Closed)

First, the fact that changes everything: 7 Brew is not accepting franchise applications or expressions of interest at this time, per its own support page. There is no application form, no waiting list and no franchising contact. Growth right now comes from existing multi-unit operators building out their development agreements. If applications reopen, the process has historically run like this:

  1. Submit an initial inquiry through the brand's official franchise development channel.
  2. Receive and review the Franchise Disclosure Document (FDD) with a franchise attorney or accountant.
  3. Complete a qualification review, including available capital and relevant business experience.
  4. Discuss available territories and site selection.
  5. Sign the franchise agreement and begin build-out.

In the meantime, the only official source is the FDD, published annually and publicly available. 7 Brew says to watch its website and official announcements for any change. If you're more interested in working at a stand than owning one, see our careers and pay guide instead.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a 7 Brew franchise cost?

Per the Franchise Disclosure Document issued May 11, 2026, the total initial investment for the first store is $940,500 to $2,283,500, not counting land. You pay $125,000 at signing (a $35,000 franchise fee plus $90,000 in development payments), and the agreement requires a minimum of 10 stores. Older guides quoting $350,000 to $775,000 do not match any recent FDD.

Is 7 Brew a profitable franchise to own?

The 2026 FDD (Item 19) reports average gross sales of $2,646,063 for franchised stands open all of fiscal 2025, with a $2,550,624 median. That is revenue, not profit. Against it sit a tiered royalty of 4.5 to 7 percent, a 2 percent brand fund and a 0.25 percent technology fee, plus rent, labor and financing costs.

How many 7 Brew locations are franchised versus corporate?

At the end of 2025 the system had 602 traditional stores: 578 franchised and 24 company-owned, per Item 20 of the 2026 FDD. Almost all growth is franchised.

Who invested in 7 Brew?

Blackstone announced a growth investment in 7 Brew to help fund its rapid national expansion, joining the brand's existing leadership as it pursued an aggressive 2026 opening schedule.

How many new 7 Brew franchises are opening in 2026?

The 2026 FDD projects 437 franchised openings and 10 company openings in 2026, and 117 franchised stands had already opened early in the year. That follows 281 net new stands in 2025.

What states is 7 Brew expanding fastest in?

By stands open, Texas leads with 133, then Florida and Ohio at 60 each (official locator, October 2026). The biggest pipeline is Arizona: 3 stands open, but 63 former Salad and Go sites bought in October 2026 for conversion. Per-state 2026 opening counts are not published.

How do I apply to open a 7 Brew franchise?

You cannot apply right now. 7 Brew states on its support page that it is not accepting new franchise applications or expressions of interest, and there is no waiting list or sign-up. All official franchising information is in the annual Franchise Disclosure Document. Watch 7brew.com for announcements if that changes.

Does 7 Brew franchise cost more or less than Dutch Bros?

It is a historical comparison only. Dutch Bros stopped selling franchises in 2017 and opens company-owned shops now. Its older terms, about a $30,000 fee and 5 percent royalty in legacy reporting, were cheaper than 7 Brew's 2026 FDD terms. Neither chain is taking new franchisees today.
