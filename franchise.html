Franchise Cost Breakdown

Total initial investment for a 7 Brew franchise is reported to fall between approximately $350,000 and $775,000. This range typically covers the franchise fee, real estate or lease build-out, drive-thru equipment, signage, initial inventory and opening costs — the wide spread reflects how much real estate and construction costs vary by market. As with any franchise, the authoritative source for current fees and terms is the brand's official Franchise Disclosure Document (FDD), which any serious applicant should review with a franchise attorney or accountant before signing.

Revenue Potential: Average Unit Volume

7 Brew reported an average unit volume (AUV) of about $2.6 million across a 320-location comparison base for the 2025 full year. AUV reflects typical gross revenue per store, not owner take-home profit — actual profitability depends on your specific lease terms, labor costs, local competition and financing structure. For a wider industry comparison, see how 7 Brew's AUV and footprint stack up in our 7 Brew vs Dutch Bros vs Starbucks comparison.

A Franchise-Heavy Growth Model

Unlike chains that grow primarily through corporate-owned stores, 7 Brew leans heavily on franchisees: as of late 2025 the brand operated 578 franchised locations against just 24 corporate-owned stands. That mix, combined with a Blackstone growth investment, has powered explosive expansion — 281 new stands opened in 2025 alone (more than the prior two years combined), with roughly 437 more planned for 2026 and 62 new locations opened in August 2026 alone. Total U.S. locations passed 700 across 38 states by September 2026, representing roughly 4,200% growth since 2022, when the brand had just 14 stands.

How to Apply

Franchise applications are handled directly by 7 Brew, not by this independent site. The general process at most franchise brands — including 7 Brew — follows a similar pattern:

  1. Submit an initial inquiry through the brand's official franchise development channel.
  2. Receive and review the Franchise Disclosure Document (FDD) with a franchise attorney or accountant.
  3. Complete a qualification review, including available capital and relevant business experience.
  4. Discuss available territories and site selection.
  5. Sign the franchise agreement and begin build-out.

For current contact details and official terms, visit 7brew.com directly. If you're more interested in working at a stand than owning one, see our careers and pay guide instead.
