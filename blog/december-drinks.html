7 Brew December Drinks 2026: What's Expected, and When It Drops

Published October 29, 2026 · Status checked today · This page updates the day the lineup is announced

People are already searching for 7 Brew's December drinks, so here is the honest status: the lineup is not announced yet. No drinks, no names, no official photos. What we can give you is the pattern, and it is a strong one. November's lineup was announced on October 29 for a November 1 launch. Expect December's announcement around November 28 to 30, and expect this page to carry the full lineup the same day.

How to read this page: anything labeled "expected" is the pattern from the last two years, not a 7 Brew promise. Some sites are already publishing invented December lineups with made-up drinks. We would rather tell you what is not known yet, and fill it in the hour it becomes known.

What carries into December from November

Two November drinks are not going anywhere: the French Silk Mocha and the Tiramisu Cold Brew became permanent menu items the day they launched, so both roll straight into December. The rest of November's featured set rotates out on November 30. The bakery menu that launched on November 1 (five treats, $3 each) is also still new enough to count as a December story. The full November rundown is in the November drinks and bakery post.

December dates that matter

  • Around Nov 28-30: the expected announcement window for the December featured drinks.
  • Mon, Dec 7: Jackpot Day. November's deal was double points plus a sweepstakes entry; December's is announced closer to the day. The full promo picture is on our Jackpot Day guide.
  • Thu, Dec 24: the gift card deal ends. Members earn a free drink reward for every $25 of gift cards bought in stand, running from Nov 27. Details on the gift cards guide.
  • Christmas week: stand hours vary by location and there is no chain-wide holiday rule, so check your stand in the near-me finder before you drive.
  • Thu, Dec 31: the last two winters, the holiday run ended on New Year's Eve.

December drinks FAQ

When will 7 Brew announce the December 2026 drinks?

Going by the pattern, around November 28 to 30. November's lineup was announced on October 29 for a November 1 launch, about three days ahead. 7 Brew announces through a press release and its social channels, and this page updates the day the December set drops.

What drinks were in last year's December lineup?

The holiday regulars from the last two winters were the Candy Cane Mocha, Santa's Cookie Mocha, Creme Brulee Breve, Holiday Punch 7 Energy, Brew Frost 7 Fizz, Mistletoe Chai, gingerbread builds and a run of eggnog drinks. Expect most of the core to return, with the December featured set announced on top.

Can I order a holiday drink before December?

Most of them, yes. The regulars are built from standard syrups the stands carry all year, so a brewista can make a Candy Cane Mocha or a Creme Brulee Breve any month if you ask for the build. Our secret menu guide has the exact words to use.

What happens at 7 Brew on December 7?

December 7 is Jackpot Day, the chain's monthly promo day, and it lands on a Monday this year. The December deal is announced closer to the date. November's was double points plus a 100,000 point sweepstakes entry.

Do the November drinks stay for December?

The featured set rotates at month end, so most November drinks finish on November 30. Two are staying for good: the French Silk Mocha and the Tiramisu Cold Brew became permanent menu items when they launched. The bakery menu that launched November 1 also carries on.
