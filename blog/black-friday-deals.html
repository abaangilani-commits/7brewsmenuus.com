7 Brew Black Friday Deals 2026: BOGO Bakery Treats and a Free Drink With Gift Cards

Published November 19, 2026 · Terms checked against 7 Brew's official whats-new page

7 Brew Black Friday deals land on Friday, November 27, 2026, and this year there are two of them. The first is a buy-one-get-one-free deal on the new bakery treats, all day at the stand. The second is the holiday gift card promo, Give Joy, Get Joy, which starts the same day and runs to Christmas Eve. Here is how both work, and the fine print that decides what you actually get.

The Black Friday BOGO: buy a bakery treat, get one free

All day on Friday, November 27, every bakery item you buy earns a second one free. Buy one, get one. Buy two, get two. Buy three, get three. The free item has to be of equal or lesser value, and you can mix and match across the five treats, so it is the cheapest way to try the whole new lineup in one stop. The five treats are the Blondie Bar, Brunette Mocha Brownie Bar, Chocolate Chunk Cookie Bar, Banana Bread Mocha Chip Loaf and Blueberry Streusel Loaf, $3 each. Our bakery menu post has the full rundown on each one. One catch to know before you go: points land on the items you pay for only. The free ones earn nothing, so a buy-3-get-3 run earns points on three treats, not six.

The gift card deal: Give Joy, Get Joy

The gift card promo starts on Black Friday too, and it runs a lot longer, from November 27 through December 24. Buy $25 in gift cards at a stand and 7 Brew loads one free medium drink reward into your Rewards account. Every extra $25 earns another one, so $50 gets two and $75 gets three. The total is what counts, not the card size: five $5 gift cards in one transaction earn the same reward as a single $25 card. You have to be a Rewards member, or give your number at the till and finish registration by the end of the day, by replying Y to the registration text or signing up online or in the app. The reward is digital, there is no physical card, and it lands overnight, so it shows in your account the next day, not at the till. It is good for any medium drink, with no restrictions, and it stays valid until January 31, 2027. Our gift cards guide covers buying and redeeming gift cards all year round; this post is only about the 2026 holiday promo.

How to get the most out of November 27

The two deals run side by side on the same day, but they pay out on different clocks. The bakery BOGO is instant, at the till, while the gift card reward lands the next morning, so the free drink from your gift cards cannot pay for the same visit. Plan it the other way round: use the BOGO to taste the bakery menu on the day, and let the gift cards cover the drinks later. A $50 gift card run earns two free mediums, and with no restrictions on the reward, the smart pick is the priciest medium you like. And since the reward holds until January 31, 2027, there is no rush to spend it in the Christmas week crowds. Black Friday FAQ

How does the Black Friday BOGO for bakery items work?

For every bakery item you buy on Friday, November 27, 2026, you get one eligible item of equal or lesser value free. Buy 1 get 1, buy 2 get 2, buy 3 get 3, all day at participating stands.

Can I mix and match bakery items in the BOGO?

Yes. You can mix and match across the five $3 treats, so you can try a new one free with each one you buy.

Do I earn Rewards points on the free bakery items?

No. Points land on the bakery items you pay for only. Free items from the BOGO earn no points.

How many free drink rewards can I earn with gift cards?

One free medium drink reward for every $25 in gift cards bought in one transaction, from November 27 through December 24, 2026. $25 earns one, $50 earns two, $75 earns three, and five $5 cards in one trip count the same as one $25 card.

Do I need to be a Rewards member to get the free drink?

Yes. You must be a Rewards member and give the number tied to your account when you buy, or give your number and finish registration by the end of the day, by replying Y to the registration text or signing up online or in the 7 Brew app.

When will I get my free drink reward, and when does it expire?

The reward loads overnight and shows in your Rewards account the next day. It is fully digital, there is no physical card, and it expires on January 31, 2027.

Can I use the free drink reward on any drink?

Yes. It is good for one free medium drink with no restrictions, so you can customize it any way you like.
