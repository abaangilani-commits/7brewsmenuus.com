7 Brew Amaretto 7 Energy: the November Energy Everyone Is Rebuilding

Published November 2, 2026 · Build checked against 7 Brew's official November announcement

First, the question the name forces: no, there is no alcohol in it. Amaretto here is a flavor, the almond liqueur taste without the liqueur. The drink itself is a November 7 Energy build: blackberry, almond and cherry over 7 Brew's energy base. What makes it worth its own post is what people did next. They started ordering the flavor in everything.

Amaretto 7 Energy at a glanceThe short version
The buildBlackberry + almond + cherry in 7 Energy
AlcoholNone. Amaretto is the flavor, not an ingredient pour
CaffeineFlavored 7 Energy standard: 67 / 105 / 133 mg by size (small / medium / large)

What it tastes like

Cherry leads, almond rounds it, blackberry keeps it from going straight candy. The three together land where amaretto sours live, sweet-tart with that marzipan middle, which is exactly why the name works even with an energy base under it. Of the November set it is the brightest drink on the board, the one to pick when the dessert coffees all sound too heavy. It is in the full November lineup with its five shelf-mates.

The flavor, on every base

Search behavior gave this drink away: people are not only looking for the energy, they are looking for an amaretto shake, an amaretto latte, an amaretto coffee and an amaretto fizz. That works because the build is just three syrups, and stands build flavors across bases all day. Two worth ordering by name: the shake version, which drinks like a cherry-almond malt, and the sour-style fizz, which keeps the tartness and drops the caffeine. Both are custom orders built from the same three flavors, so ask for blackberry, almond and cherry on your base of choice and the stand will know what you mean.

Amaretto 7 Energy FAQ

What is in the 7 Brew Amaretto 7 Energy?

Blackberry, almond and cherry flavors in 7 Energy, 7 Brew's energy drink base. That is the official build from the November 2026 announcement.

Does the Amaretto 7 Energy contain alcohol?

No. Amaretto is the name of the flavor profile, the almond taste of the Italian liqueur, recreated with syrups. The drink is an energy drink: caffeine, not alcohol. Nothing at a 7 Brew stand contains alcohol.

How much caffeine is in the Amaretto 7 Energy?

7 Brew has not published a figure for this exact build. It sits on the flavored 7 Energy base, which the official guide lists at 67 mg for a small, 105 mg for a medium and 133 mg for a large, so expect it right in that range.

Is the Amaretto 7 Energy permanent?

It was not announced as permanent. French Silk and Tiramisu got the permanent label in November; Amaretto did not. Plan on it as a November featured drink, and if it follows the usual pattern it rotates out at month end.

Can I get the amaretto flavor in a coffee or shake?

Ask for it. The build is three syrups (blackberry, almond, cherry), and stands apply flavors across bases. An amaretto latte and an amaretto shake are both reasonable custom orders built from the same three flavors.

How much does the Amaretto 7 Energy cost?

7 Energy pricing: about $4.50 small, $5.25 medium and $6.25 large at most stands. Prices vary by stand.
