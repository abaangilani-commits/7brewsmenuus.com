7 Brew French Silk Mocha: Dark Chocolate, Vanilla, and Here to Stay

Published November 2, 2026 · Build checked against 7 Brew's official November announcement

The French Silk Mocha is the other half of November's permanent pair. Where the Tiramisu went to cold brew, French Silk went to the mocha: dark chocolate and vanilla, smoother and darker than the standard mocha, and it stays on the menu after November ends. If you order one new drink this month to keep in your rotation, this is the safer bet.

French Silk Mocha at a glanceThe short version
The buildDark chocolate + vanilla, mocha style
Finishing touchesWhipped cream and a dark chocolate drizzle, per 7 Brew's menu listing, with a sugar-free drizzle swap available
StatusPermanent. Stays after the November featured set rotates out
CaloriesNot published. The drink postdates the current official nutrition guide
PriceFeatured tier while featured: about $4.75 / $5.50 / $6.50 by size, varies by stand

What it tastes like

French silk pie is a chocolate pie with a whipped, mousse-like middle, and the drink chases that texture as much as the flavor. Dark chocolate keeps it from going candy-sweet, vanilla rounds the edges, and the whipped cream on top is not decoration, it is half the point. Against the regular mocha it reads darker and smoother. Against the Brunette, the chain's hazelnut and salted caramel mocha, it is the straighter chocolate drink: no nut, no salt, just chocolate done properly.

Why permanent matters

Featured drinks are a gamble for regulars: find a favorite in November, lose it in December. 7 Brew announced French Silk and Tiramisu as flavors that stay, available permanently across all stands. That changes the order decision. This is not a try-it-before-it-goes drink, it is a candidate for your usual, and it launched alongside the Tiramisu Cold Brew in the November lineup.

French Silk Mocha FAQ

What is in the 7 Brew French Silk Mocha?

Dark chocolate and vanilla, built as a mocha, topped with whipped cream and a dark chocolate drizzle per 7 Brew's menu listing. That is the official build from the November announcement.

Is the French Silk Mocha permanent?

Yes. 7 Brew announced French Silk and Tiramisu as new flavors that will be available permanently across all stands. The rest of the November featured drinks rotate out at month end. These two stay.

How many calories are in the French Silk Mocha?

Not published. It launched after the current official nutrition guide (October 1, 2026), so no official count exists yet. Any exact figure you see today is an estimate, including ours if we printed one, so we do not.

Does the French Silk Mocha come with whipped cream?

Yes, whipped cream and a dark chocolate drizzle are the standard finish per the chain's menu listing, and a sugar-free drizzle swap is available. You can always order it without.

French Silk Mocha or Brunette, which should I get?

Chocolate-first drinkers: French Silk. It is dark chocolate and vanilla, smooth and direct. If you like a nutty, salted edge in your mocha, the Brunette (hazelnut, salted caramel, chocolate) is the richer puzzle. Both are permanent, so this is a rotation question, not a deadline.

Can I get French Silk in other drinks?

It was announced as a permanent flavor, and stands build flavors across bases, so asking for it in a breve or a chiller is reasonable. The mocha is the official build; other bases are custom orders at the stand's discretion.
