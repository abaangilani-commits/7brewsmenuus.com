7 Brew Protein Boost: $2, 15 Grams of Collagen, and the Fine Print

Published November 2, 2026 · Details checked against 7 Brew's official protein page

For $2, 7 Brew will put 15 grams of protein in any drink on the menu. It is called the Protein Boost, it rolled out nationwide on October 1 after an August test around Dallas-Fort Worth, and it is the rare add-on where the fine print matters more than the headline. The protein is collagen. That one word decides who this is for and who it is not.

Protein Boost at a glanceThe short version
What it isA protein add-on for any drink, hot or iced
Protein15 g per boost
Source100% collagen, porcine (pork) source
Price$2 per drink
TasteUnflavored. It is built not to change the drink
Added caloriesAbout 90, with 0 g sugar, per the official nutrition for the add-on
Dietary flagsDairy-free and gluten-free. NOT vegetarian, vegan, halal or kosher

The fine print most posts bury

Collagen from a porcine source means this is not a neutral add-on for everyone. If you eat vegetarian or vegan, it is out. If you keep halal or kosher, it is out. 7 Brew states this on its own protein page, to its credit, but the launch coverage mostly skipped it, and it is the first thing a good chunk of customers need to know. For everyone else, the profile is friendly: dairy-free, gluten-free, unflavored, and it dissolves into hot and iced drinks without the chalky texture whey is known for.

Where it makes sense

Collagen is not a complete protein, so treat the boost as a top-up, not a meal replacement. It earns its $2 in the drinks people already use as a snack: smoothies and shakes first, then the big breve-style coffees. Adding it to a small black coffee works chemically and baffles everyone. Points note for regulars: the boost is an add-on, and the drink still earns its usual rewards points. Our nutrition page tracks where the boost lands in the wider menu numbers.

One wording conflict, flagged

7 Brew's own pages disagree slightly on availability: the protein page describes the nationwide rollout, while the whats-new page still says "select stands." The October 1 nationwide launch is corroborated by trade coverage (QSR Magazine and Parade both reported it), so most stands should have it. If yours does not, that is the select-stands language being out of date at some stands, not you misreading the menu.

Protein Boost FAQ

What is the 7 Brew Protein Boost?

An add-on that puts 15 grams of protein into any drink for $2. The protein is 100% collagen, unflavored, and it works in hot and iced drinks. It rolled out nationwide on October 1, 2026.

What kind of protein is in the Protein Boost?

Collagen, from a porcine (pork) source. That is the detail that matters: collagen is an incomplete protein, fine as a top-up, and the pork source means the boost is not vegetarian or vegan.

Is the 7 Brew Protein Boost vegan, halal or kosher?

No to all three. The collagen is porcine, so it does not fit vegan, vegetarian, halal or kosher diets. It is dairy-free and gluten-free, which it does state on 7 Brew's official protein page.

How many calories does the Protein Boost add?

About 90 calories and 0 grams of sugar for the 15 grams of protein, per the official nutrition for the add-on. Our nutrition page shows how that lands across the menu.

Does the Protein Boost change the taste of the drink?

It is unflavored and built not to. Collagen dissolves cleanly compared with whey, so in a flavored drink most people cannot pick it out. In a plain black coffee, you are on your own.

Which drinks can I add the Protein Boost to?

Any drink, per 7 Brew. It makes the most sense in smoothies, shakes and the big milk-based coffees, where 15 extra grams of protein turns a treat into something closer to a snack.
