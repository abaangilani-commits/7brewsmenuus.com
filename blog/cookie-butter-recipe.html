7 Brew Cookie Butter Recipe (Copycat): No Cookie Butter Needed

Published October 29, 2026 · A copycat recipe, checked against the official drink build

Here is the thing nobody tells you about the 7 Brew Cookie Butter: there is no cookie butter in it. Not a spoonful. The flavor comes from three syrups, and once you know which three, the drink takes five minutes at home. Most copycat recipes online reach for a jar of Biscoff. That makes a fine latte, but it is not this drink.

Copycat, stated plainly: 7 Brew does not publish its recipes. This one is built from the drink's official flavor build (toasted marshmallow, hazelnut and white chocolate, on a breve) and the amounts brewistas share. The stand's official numbers are on our Cookie Butter drink guide.

What is actually in the 7 Brew Cookie Butter?

Three flavors, equal parts, on a breve base. Toasted marshmallow brings the baked, campfire sweetness people read as "cookie." Hazelnut brings the nutty middle. White chocolate rounds it into dessert. Espresso and half-and-half do the rest. The full flavor list for every drink like this is on our 7 Brew syrups and flavors guide.

That is also why the Biscoff versions miss. Cookie butter spread is spiced, heavy and a little gritty in milk. The real drink is lighter and nuttier, more like a toasted cookie than a spread. Make both if you like. Just know which one you are making.

Ingredients for one medium iced Cookie Butter

  • 2/3 oz toasted marshmallow syrup
  • 2/3 oz hazelnut syrup
  • 2/3 oz white chocolate sauce
  • 2 shots of espresso, about 2 oz, pulled hot
  • About 8 oz half-and-half
  • A full cup of ice

Brewistas share the build as Torani bottles, and Torani sells all three flavors, so the home version is easy to stock. Any brand works if the flavors match. For a small (16 oz), cut the three syrups to 1/3 oz each and the half-and-half to about 6 oz. The espresso stays at 2 shots.

How to make it, step by step

  1. Syrups first. Pour the toasted marshmallow, hazelnut and white chocolate sauce into your cup.
  2. Hot espresso over the top. Add the shots and stir well. The heat melts the white chocolate sauce into the syrups so nothing sinks to the bottom.
  3. Ice to the top. Fill the cup all the way. A half-filled cup waters the drink down as you top it up.
  4. Half-and-half over the ice. Pour it in, stir once, and drink. One stir, not ten. You want the first sips rich and the rest balanced.

Make it your way

Hot. Steam the half-and-half, skip the ice, same syrup amounts. The official hot medium is 570 calories if you are counting.

Frozen. Blend everything with a full cup of ice until smooth. That copies the stand's chiller style (official frozen medium: 470 calories, and the caffeine climbs to 233 mg in the chiller build).

Sugar free. Swap all three bottles for sugar-free versions, same amounts. 7 Brew sells an official sugar-free Cookie Butter breve too, so the swap is proven at the stand, not a guess.

Cookie Butter Blondie. Add vanilla and caramel to the same cup and you have the popular mashup: the Blondie build wearing Cookie Butter's three syrups. Half an ounce of each extra flavor is plenty.

Cookie Butter recipe FAQ

Is there really no cookie butter in the 7 Brew Cookie Butter?

Right, none. Not at the stand and not in this recipe. The flavor is three syrups in equal parts: toasted marshmallow, hazelnut and white chocolate, built on a breve. Recipes that use Biscoff spread make a tasty drink, but it is a different drink, heavier and more spiced.

What syrups does 7 Brew use for Cookie Butter?

Brewistas share the build as Torani bottles: toasted marshmallow syrup, hazelnut syrup and white chocolate sauce, a third of an ounce each for a small and two thirds of an ounce each for a medium. Any brand with the same three flavors gets you close.

Can I make it without an espresso machine?

Yes. Use 2 ounces of very strong coffee or cold brew concentrate instead of the shots. Keep it hot when you mix it into the syrups, because the heat is what melts the white chocolate sauce smooth.

How do I make a sugar-free Cookie Butter at home?

Swap all three bottles for their sugar-free versions and keep the amounts the same. 7 Brew sells an official sugar-free Cookie Butter breve too, built the same way.

What milk is closest to the stand drink?

Half-and-half. A breve is espresso plus half-and-half instead of milk, and that richness is most of the Cookie Butter's dessert feel. Whole milk works and lands lighter. Oat milk is the best dairy-free swap.

Can I make the Cookie Butter hot or frozen?

Both. Hot: steam the half-and-half, skip the ice, same syrup amounts. Frozen: blend everything with a full cup of ice until smooth, which copies the stand's chiller style.
