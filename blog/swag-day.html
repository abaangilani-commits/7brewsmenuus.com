7 Brew Swag Day 2026: Free Shirt With a Large Drink on November 14

Published November 14, 2026 · Checked against 7 Brew's Swag Day announcement and grand opening flyers today

Today is 7 Brew Swag Day. Buy a large drink at one of the 18 stands 7 Brew named, and you get a free 7 Brew shirt with it. The deal runs today only, Saturday, November 14, 2026, while supplies last. Here is the full list of stands, what the shirts look like, and when the next Swag Days land.

Why this page exists: Swag Day is easy to miss. 7 Brew does not list it on its whats-new page. It goes out stand by stand, in the announcement and the grand opening flyers. We pulled all of it together here, so you can check your stand in one place instead of hunting through flyers.

How 7 Brew Swag Day works

The rule fits on one line: large drink, free shirt. Order any large drink at a participating stand and the shirt is yours. A small or a medium does not count, and there is no separate coupon or code to show.

Go early. The flyers say while supplies last, and no stand publishes how many shirts it has. When a stand runs out, it stops handing them out. Morning is the safe bet.

Designs vary. The announcement says so outright, and the flyers show different shirts from stand to stand. You may not get the design in the photo.

It is drive-thru only. 7 Brew stands have no indoor seating, so you pick the shirt up at the window with your drink.

Two stands to double-check before you go

Niles, IL and Romeoville, IL both list Swag Day on Saturday, November 14 on their grand opening flyers, but neither appears on the announcement we have seen. If either is your stand, check its social page or call before you make the trip. Tallahassee is a different case: its earlier flyer said November 14, but the current flyer moved Swag Day to Saturday, November 21, when the opening moved to November 16.

What the Swag Day shirts look like

The announcement shows three designs: a light blue shirt with an illustration of a 7 Brew drive-thru stand and "Seven" and "Drive Thru Coffee" lettering, a pink shirt with a script "Pink Mermaid" design, and a dark gray slogan shirt. Because designs may vary, the shirt you get may not match these exactly, and the flyers show different color sets from stand to stand. Sizes and quantities are not stated anywhere we have seen.

The next 7 Brew Swag Days

Swag Day is part of the standard grand opening week, so it follows the new stands. Twenty-eight stands open Monday, November 16, and ten of them already have a Swag Day date on their flyers: Saturday, November 21. Two more open November 23 and hold theirs Saturday, December 5.

Tips to get your free shirt

Order a large. The free shirt is tied to a large drink, so a smaller size will not qualify, whatever you order.

Go early. Shirts are limited at every stand, and supplies are not published.

Match the address. Several of these towns have more than one 7 Brew, or share a name with another town. Check the street number against the table above.

Check the stand's social page. Details can change fast in a stand's first week, and the stand's own page is the first place a change shows up.

Swag Day FAQ

When is 7 Brew Swag Day?

Saturday, November 14, 2026, at the 18 stands named on 7 Brew's Swag Day announcement. The next ones are Saturday, November 21 for stands that open November 16, and Saturday, December 5 for Bay City, MI and Sheboygan, WI.

What do you get on 7 Brew Swag Day?

A free 7 Brew shirt with a large drink purchase. The announcement says designs may vary, and the flyers say while supplies last.

Do I have to buy a large drink to get the free shirt?

Yes. The announcement reads "Large drink = free shirt," so the shirt comes with a large drink purchase. A small or medium does not qualify.

Which 7 Brew locations have Swag Day on November 14?

Burleson, TX; Charleston, WV; Clarksville, TN; Covington, LA; Franklin, VA; Harvey, LA; Helotes, TX; Kent, OH; Laramie, WY; Midlothian, TX; Roseville, MI; Sevierville, TN; Sioux Falls, SD; South Hill, VA; Suffolk, VA; Waco, TX; Warrenville, IL; and Wilmington, OH.

What time does Swag Day start on November 14?

The announcement lists no hours. The grand opening flyers we have seen list Swag Day from 5:30 AM to 11 PM. Confirm with your stand.

What do the Swag Day shirts look like?

The announcement shows a light blue drive-thru stand shirt, a pink "Pink Mermaid" shirt and a dark gray slogan shirt. Designs may vary, and the flyers show different colors from stand to stand. Sizes are not stated.

Is my stand taking part if it is not on the list?

Only the 18 named stands are confirmed for November 14. Niles, IL and Romeoville, IL list November 14 on their flyers but do not appear on the announcement, so check with the stand before you go. Any other stand is not part of this Swag Day.
