7 Brew Pecan Pie Breve: the November Comeback, Explained

Published November 2, 2026 · Build checked against 7 Brew's official November announcement

The Pecan Pie Breve is back. It returned November 1 as part of the November lineup, after first showing up on the 2025 holiday menu, and it is the drink readers ask us about most this week. Start with the surprise, because it changes what you expect from the cup: there is no pecan in it. No pecan syrup, no pecan anything. The name is the destination. The route is almond roca, Irish cream and salted caramel in a breve.

Why trust this build: it is the one from 7 Brew's own November announcement. At least one secret-menu site lists a different Pecan Pie build with cupcake syrup in it. That is a fan version you can order by name at some stands. It is not what the seasonal drink is made of.

What it tastes like

Read the build and you can taste the logic. Almond roca brings the buttery, toffee-nut note that stands in for the pie's filling. Irish cream softens everything and adds the baked, custardy middle. Salted caramel does the crust-and-top job, sweet with a salty edge so the cup does not go flat. The breve base is doing quiet work too: half-and-half makes it drink like a dessert instead of a flavored coffee.

Honest balance, because this drink splits people: in its 2025 run, at least one reviewer found it tasted like regular coffee with cream and sugar rather than a slice of pie. Both things can be true. If you want a pie in a cup, order it sweet. If you want a rich, nutty breve with a holiday lean, the standard build is the one.

How to order it well

It comes iced, hot or blended like the rest of the breve family, and the medium is the size the flavors were tuned around. Two upgrades worth knowing: an extra shot cuts the sweetness if you run it as a large, and it pairs properly with the new Chocolate Chunk Cookie Bar from the bakery menu, $3, which is the closest thing to eating the pie this drink is named after. The full November set it launched with is in our November drinks guide.

The at-home version

For a medium iced at home: pull 2 shots of espresso over ice, add 1 oz almond roca syrup, half an ounce each of Irish cream and salted caramel syrups, and top with about 8 oz of half-and-half. Stir before the first sip, the syrups sit low. It is an approximation of the stand build, tuned for a home fridge, and it lands close enough that the name makes sense.

Pecan Pie Breve FAQ

What is in the 7 Brew Pecan Pie Breve?

Three flavors in a breve: almond roca, Irish cream and salted caramel, over espresso and steamed half-and-half. That is the official build from 7 Brew's November announcement. A breve is the rich base here, espresso with half-and-half instead of milk.

Does the Pecan Pie Breve actually have pecan in it?

No pecan syrup at all. The name is the flavor the drink is aiming at, not an ingredient list. The nutty, buttery taste comes from the almond roca, and the pie feeling comes from the caramel and cream around it. If you have a nut allergy, ask your stand before ordering: 7 Brew has not published allergen details for this drink.

How many calories are in the 7 Brew Pecan Pie Breve?

Not published. The drink launched after 7 Brew's current nutrition guide (October 1, 2026), so it has no official calorie count yet, and any site quoting you an exact number is estimating. Our nutrition page covers the breve family numbers that do exist, and this post updates the day the official figure lands.

Can you get the Pecan Pie Breve sugar-free?

Partly, at most stands. Ask for the sugar-free versions of the syrups where the stand stocks them. Because the official nutrition for this drink is not out, treat the result as lower sugar rather than any specific number, and expect the taste to land a little less rich.

Is the Pecan Pie Breve permanent?

No. It is a returning seasonal drink, back for November 2026 after a run on the 2025 holiday menu. 7 Brew has not announced a 2026 end date. Last year the holiday set stayed through the season, but that is a pattern, not a promise.

How much does the Pecan Pie Breve cost?

It sits in the featured drinks tier: about $4.75 small, $5.50 medium and $6.50 large at most stands. Prices vary by stand, so treat those as the going rate, not a quote.
