7 Brew Muffin Tops Are Discontinued: What Happened, and What to Order Now

Published November 2, 2026 · Confirmed on 7 Brew's official site

It is official, and it is not a rumor anymore. 7 Brew discontinued its muffin tops on November 1, 2026, the day the new bakery menu launched. For years they were the chain's entire food menu: three flavors, no fanfare, sold from a small case most people walked past. This is the short record of what they were, why they ended, and what to order instead.

The official line: 7 Brew's bakery FAQ answers the muffin top question in one sentence: "As part of this update, Muffin Tops will no longer be available at 7 Brew stands." The update is the bakery launch. The five new items are the replacement, not an addition.
The three muffin topsOfficial nutrition (last guide, Oct 2026)
Blueberry470 cal · 34 g sugar · 4 g protein
Chocolate Chip560 cal · 45 g sugar · 7 g protein
Lemon Poppyseed480 cal · 35 g sugar · 6 g protein
AllergensAll three contain milk, soy, wheat and egg
Price while soldNo official price. Stands set their own, usually reported at $2.50 to $3.50

Why they ended

Simple math. A drive-thru stand has room for one small food case, and 7 Brew wanted a real bakery line: five items, a flat $3, bars and loaves built around its best-known drink flavors. Something had to give, and the muffin tops were the something. They were never printed on the menu board, never marketed, and most customers did not know they existed until they were gone.

Can you still find one?

Only as sell-through. Stands that stocked up before November 1 may still have a few in the case, and once they are gone, that stand is done. There is no announced return, no seasonal comeback on the calendar, and no frozen stock to order from. If a muffin top is the point of your trip, ring the stand first.

What to order instead

The replacements map closer than you would expect. Blueberry people: the Blueberry Streusel Loaf is your item, same fruit, better texture. Chocolate Chip people: the Chocolate Chunk Cookie Bar, and it is not close. Lemon Poppyseed is the orphan, nothing on the new menu matches it directly, and the Banana Bread Mocha Chip Loaf is the nearest loaf in spirit. The full new set is in our bakery menu guide, and the evergreen food picture lives on the food menu page.

Muffin Tops Discontinued FAQ

Are 7 Brew muffin tops really discontinued?

Yes. 7 Brew confirmed it on its official page: as part of the bakery update, Muffin Tops will no longer be available at 7 Brew stands. The change took effect with the bakery launch on November 1, 2026.

When did 7 Brew stop selling muffin tops?

November 1, 2026, the day the five-item bakery menu launched. Some stands may still sell through remaining stock for a short while, but no new stock is coming.

Why did 7 Brew discontinue muffin tops?

Space and focus. Stands have one small food case, and the chain replaced three quiet items with a five-item bakery line priced at a flat $3. 7 Brew has not given a longer explanation than the swap itself.

What replaced 7 Brew muffin tops?

The bakery menu: Blondie Bar, Brunette Mocha Brownie Bar, Chocolate Chunk Cookie Bar, Banana Bread Mocha Chip Loaf and Blueberry Streusel Loaf, $3 each, served chilled, 50 rewards points per item.

Will 7 Brew muffin tops come back?

Nothing announced. Discontinued items do return at chains when the noise is loud enough, and this post will update if 7 Brew says anything. For now, plan as if they are done.

How many calories were in a 7 Brew muffin top?

From the last official nutrition guide that listed them: Blueberry 470, Lemon Poppyseed 480 and Chocolate Chip 560 calories, with 34 to 45 grams of sugar. All three contained milk, soy, wheat and egg.
