7 Brew Tiramisu Cold Brew: What's In It, and Why It's Staying

Published November 2, 2026 · Build checked against 7 Brew's official November announcement

Most November drinks leave when the month does. This one is not leaving. The Tiramisu Cold Brew launched November 1 as one of two new permanent flavors, and it takes the chain's strongest base, the relaunched cold brew, and runs dessert flavors over it: salted caramel, almond and chocolate macadamia nut.

Tiramisu Cold Brew at a glanceThe short version
The buildSalted caramel + almond + chocolate macadamia nut, over cold brew
Finishing touchesCold foam and a dark chocolate drizzle, as listed on 7 Brew's menu pages
StatusPermanent. One of two flavors that stay after November
CaffeineThe drink's own figure is not published. The plain cold brew base runs 179 / 268 / 357 mg by size
CaloriesNot published. The drink postdates the current official nutrition guide
PriceFeatured tier while it is a featured drink: about $4.75 / $5.50 / $6.50 by size, varies by stand

Does it taste like tiramisu?

Like the idea of tiramisu, which is what a coffee drink can honestly do. There is no mascarpone and no soaked ladyfinger in a drive-thru cup. What the build borrows is the dessert's shape: coffee first, then cocoa from the chocolate macadamia nut, a nutty almond middle, and salted caramel standing in for the sweet, slightly dark top. Over cold brew instead of espresso shots, it drinks smoother and less bitter than a tiramisu latte would, which is the point of building it on this base.

The latte question

People are already searching for a tiramisu latte, and the permanent-flavor wording is why it can work: 7 Brew announced Tiramisu as a flavor that stays on the menu, not just a one-off drink. Stands build flavors across bases all day, so asking for the tiramisu flavors in a latte or a breve is a reasonable order. The cold brew version below is the official build. Anything else is a custom order, and your stand has the final say.

How it fits November

It launched with the November lineup next to its permanent sibling, the French Silk Mocha. Between the two, this is the one for cold coffee drinkers, and the one with the stronger caffeine base. If you are working through the month, the Pecan Pie Breve is the sweet one, this is the grown-up one.

Tiramisu Cold Brew FAQ

What is in the 7 Brew Tiramisu Cold Brew?

Salted caramel, almond and chocolate macadamia nut flavors over 7 Brew cold brew, finished with cold foam and a dark chocolate drizzle per the chain's menu listing. That is the official build from the November announcement.

Is the 7 Brew Tiramisu Cold Brew permanent?

Yes. 7 Brew announced Tiramisu and French Silk as new flavors that will be available permanently across all stands, unlike the rest of the November featured set, which rotates out at month end.

How much caffeine is in the Tiramisu Cold Brew?

7 Brew has not published a figure for this drink. Its base is the chain's cold brew, which the official guide lists at 179 mg for a small, 268 mg for a medium and 357 mg for a large, so expect it in that range rather than espresso-drink territory.

How many calories are in the 7 Brew Tiramisu Cold Brew?

Not published yet. The drink launched after the current official nutrition guide (October 1, 2026), so there is no official calorie count. Sites quoting an exact number are estimating.

Can I get the tiramisu flavor as a latte?

Ask. Tiramisu is a permanent flavor, and stands routinely build flavors on other bases. The official menu item is the cold brew version, so a latte build is a custom order at your stand's discretion.

Is there a sugar-free Tiramisu Cold Brew?

Not as an official menu build. You can ask for sugar-free swaps on the syrups the stand stocks, but with no published nutrition for the drink, treat it as a lower-sugar custom order rather than a counted one.
