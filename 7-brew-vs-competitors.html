7 Brew vs Dutch Bros vs Starbucks: Which Drive-Thru Wins in 2026?

We line up price, speed, menu size, rewards and real 2026 growth data — sourced from QSR Magazine — so you can decide where to pull in next.

Brand Overview: Three Different Drive-Thru Models

All three chains sell espresso-based drinks from a drive-thru, but the business model behind each is different. 7 Brew is a drive-thru-only, drinks-only concept built around a double-lane stand and a bright, customizable menu of breves, energy drinks and Italian sodas — it has no indoor seating and no food. Dutch Bros started as a single espresso cart in Grants Pass, Oregon in 1992 and has grown into a 1,100+ unit chain known for its "Broista" culture, added a food menu in 2025, and mixes drive-thru with some walk-up windows. Starbucks is the category creator: a globally recognized cafe brand with tens of thousands of locations, full food service, indoor seating at most stores, and a menu that spans well beyond espresso into food, merchandise and seasonal collectibles.

Menu Size & Drive-Thru Speed

7 Brew leans hardest into speed and customization: a compact drinks-only menu (7 Originals, Energy, Fizz sodas, lemonades, smoothies, shakes and teas) that a brewista can build to order in a typical 3–5 minute window, growing to around 15 minutes at peak hours. That focus is possible because there is no food line to run in parallel.

Dutch Bros has a broader menu after its 2025 food expansion, and according to QSR Magazine's May 2026 reporting, the brand "does not prioritize speed over friendliness" — the in-window conversation with your Brorista is part of the product. Starbucks has by far the largest menu of the three once food, merchandise and seasonal exclusives are included, but its drive-thru speed is frequently affected by mobile-order pileup during peak hours since online orders and drive-thru orders compete for the same queue.

2026 Growth & Locations

According to QSR Magazine's May 2026 comparison, Dutch Bros held roughly 1,117 locations (844 corporate, 333 franchised) across 25 states, with a Q1 2026 average unit volume of about $2.16 million. 7 Brew, by contrast, had 602 locations (24 corporate, 578 franchised) at that point, with a $2.6 million average unit volume across a 320-location comparison base — and has since crossed 700 locations across 38 states by September 2026, adding 62 new stands in August alone. 7 Brew is franchise-heavy by design, while Dutch Bros has leaned more corporate to protect density and average sales per store.

Starbucks dwarfs both: tens of thousands of locations across nearly every U.S. state and dozens of countries, making it the default option in areas neither challenger brand has reached yet.

Which One Should You Choose?

There is no universally "better" answer — it depends what you are optimizing for:

  • Fastest in-and-out: 7 Brew's model is built around drive-thru speed and a menu with no food line competing for register time.
  • Widest availability: Starbucks, by a wide margin, especially outside the South/Midwest where 7 Brew and Dutch Bros are still expanding.
  • Most personal service: Dutch Bros' "Brorista" culture is explicitly built around conversation over speed.
  • Most customizable drink menu relative to size: 7 Brew, with dozens of syrup and milk combinations across a compact drinks-only menu — see our full customization guide.
  • Food plus coffee in one stop: Starbucks (full menu) or Dutch Bros (added 2025).

Frequently Asked Questions

Is 7 Brew cheaper than Starbucks?

Menu prices overlap for most espresso drinks (roughly $4.50–$7.50 at both), but 7 Brew's drive-thru-only model skips the overhead of Starbucks' cafe seating and mobile-order kiosks, and 7 Brew has no separate food menu pushing up average ticket size. Exact pricing varies by stand and market.

Is 7 Brew faster than Dutch Bros?

Both brands are built around double drive-thru lanes, but they optimize for different things. 7 Brew markets speed as a core value (3–5 minutes typical, up to 15 at peak). QSR Magazine reported that Dutch Bros explicitly “does not prioritize speed over friendliness,” favoring the barista-customer “Brorista” interaction over pure throughput.

Does 7 Brew have more locations than Dutch Bros?

As of late 2025, Dutch Bros had more total locations (1,117 per QSR Magazine, May 2026) than 7 Brew (602 at the same point). But 7 Brew is growing faster in percentage terms — it opened 281 stands in 2025 alone and passed 700 locations across 38 states by September 2026, versus Dutch Bros' 25-state footprint.

Which has the better rewards program, 7 Brew or Starbucks?

Starbucks Rewards is the more mature program with a star-based system redeemable for food and merchandise as well as drinks. 7 Brew's program is simpler: qualifying drinks earn 100 points, and 1,000 points unlocks a free-drink option (redemption is manual, not automatic). Dutch Rewards sits in between, with points and periodic free-drink days.

Does 7 Brew have food like Dutch Bros or Starbucks?

No. 7 Brew is a drinks-only, drive-thru-only concept with no food menu and no indoor seating. Dutch Bros added food in 2025 and reported it reached a “low-teens” share of sales by Q1 2026. Starbucks has offered food nationwide for years, from pastries to sandwiches.

Which is growing faster, 7 Brew or Dutch Bros?

7 Brew is expanding at a much steeper percentage rate — roughly 4,200% growth since 2022 (from 14 to 900+ stands) — driven by an aggressive, franchise-heavy strategy (578 franchised locations versus 24 corporate as of late 2025). Dutch Bros has more total units and a higher average unit volume, but is growing at a comparatively steadier pace with a more corporate-heavy, market-dense strategy.

Is 7 Brew or Dutch Bros better for a stronger coffee fix?

Both use espresso as their base and offer similar caffeine ranges by size. 7 Brew's signature format is the “breve” — espresso built on steamed half-and-half rather than milk — which gives its classics a richer, creamier mouthfeel than a typical Dutch Bros or Starbucks latte. If you want the strongest jolt specifically, compare the largest size and ask for extra shots at either chain.

Which chain has more locations near me?

It depends entirely on your state. 7 Brew's fastest 2026 expansion has concentrated in Texas, Florida, Illinois and Georgia, while Dutch Bros remains strongest in the West and Pacific Northwest where it originated.
