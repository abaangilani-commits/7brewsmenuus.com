About 7 Brew in Rogers, Arkansas — Where It All Started

Rogers, Arkansas is where the 7 Brew story began. Founded in 2017 by Ron and Jori Crume, the very first 7 Brew drive-thru opened here in Northwest Arkansas, and the brand's headquarters remain in nearby Springdale. What started as a single stand has grown into a national chain with more than 777 locations across 35-plus states — but the Rogers stands are still the hometown originals.

The Rogers locations on South 52nd Street and West Walnut Street serve the Northwest Arkansas metro alongside stands in neighboring Bentonville, Fayetteville, and Springdale. The menu here is the same one served nationwide: breve-style espresso drinks made with half-and-half, 7 Energy drinks, Italian sodas, smoothies, teas, and shakes. Browse the complete 7 Brew menu with prices to see every category.

As the birthplace of 7 Brew, Rogers is where many of the signature drinks were first developed. The Blondie, Brunette, and Smooth 7 all originated from this market. First-time visitors should also explore the secret menu — locals here have been creating custom combinations since 2017. Customize any drink with sugar-free syrups, and use the calorie calculator to compare options.
