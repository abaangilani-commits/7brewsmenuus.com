7 Brew Brunette: Ingredients, Ordering & Drink Comparisons

Explore the Brunette flavor combination, how to order it, alternatives and where to check the correct calories and caffeine for your preparation.

What is in a Brunette?

Hazelnut + Caramel Mocha. It is listed in 7 Brew’s 7 Originals collection. Official Brunette listing.

Brunette cold brew and canned Brunette Brownie are separate preparations. Choose the matching entry in the official nutrition guide.

What Does the Brunette Taste Like?

The Brunette is a layered, indulgent drink that balances chocolate, hazelnut, and caramel in a way that feels rich without being overwhelming. The first sip opens with roasted hazelnut — warm and nutty rather than sugary — followed by smooth, buttery caramel that adds body and sweetness. The mocha finishes things off with a soft chocolate note that takes the edge off the espresso. Together, the three flavors create something that tastes layered instead of flat, like a grown-up candy bar in liquid form.

Built on the same breve base as the other 7 Brew originals, the Brunette gets its thick, creamy texture from steamed half-and-half. The espresso grounds the drink with a roasted coffee presence that keeps it from tipping into pure dessert territory. Try it iced for a lighter, more refreshing take, or as a frozen chiller when you want something closer to a milkshake. If you love Nutella or hazelnut chocolate spreads, the Brunette will feel immediately familiar. Check the calorie calculator to compare sizes before you order.

How to order it

An example order is: “A medium iced Brunette, please.” Add your preferred sweetness, milk and topping choices before the order is finalized. The official menu lists hot, iced and frozen chiller preparations for the Originals. Ask the stand to confirm any substitution.

Quarter, half, regular and extra sweetness are menu options. “Half sweet” describes a flavor adjustment; it does not mean half the total calories.

Which coffee should you choose?

A hazelnut-and-caramel option for someone who wants a chocolate-based coffee.

Blondie uses vanilla and caramel in a breve. Brunette uses hazelnut and caramel in a mocha.

Frequently Asked Questions About the Brunette

How many calories are in a 7 Brew Brunette?
A medium (24 oz) Brunette breve has approximately 440 calories. The small (16 oz) is around 290 calories and the large (32 oz) is roughly 590 calories. You can reduce calories by swapping the half-and-half for oat milk or almond milk.
What flavors are in the 7 Brew Brunette?
The Brunette combines hazelnut syrup, caramel syrup, and mocha sauce over espresso with steamed half-and-half. The result is a layered chocolate-hazelnut-caramel flavor profile similar to Nutella in coffee form.
Is the Brunette one of 7 Brew's signature drinks?
Yes, the Brunette is one of 7 Brew's original signature drinks — part of the core lineup alongside the Blondie, Smooth 7, and Cookie Butter. It is one of their most popular orders at the drive-thru.
