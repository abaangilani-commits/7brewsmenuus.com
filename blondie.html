7 Brew Blondie: Ingredients, Ordering & Drink Comparisons

The Blondie is the drink 7 Brew is famous for. Espresso under caramel and vanilla, drowned in half-and-half. A medium iced runs 480 calories and about $5.25. Here is exactly what is in it, how to order it, and how to make one at home.

Medium iced
$5.25 · 480 cal · 120 mg caffeine · 38 g sugar
In the cup
2 espresso shots, caramel + vanilla, half-and-half (a breve)
Sugar-free medium: 400 cal, 6 g sugar
Same order, sugar-free syrups. Sugar-free guide

What is in a Blondie?

Vanilla + Caramel Breve. It is listed in 7 Brew’s 7 Originals collection. Official Blondie listing.

A Blondie cold brew is a different preparation from the original breve; its milk balance and nutrition should not be assumed identical.

What Does the Blondie Taste Like?

The Blondie is sweet, smooth, and dessert-like without being overly sugary. The first sip delivers a wave of buttery caramel, followed by vanilla that rounds out the sweetness into something creamy and balanced. Because the drink is built on a breve base — espresso with steamed half-and-half — it has a naturally rich, velvety mouthfeel that makes the flavors linger. The espresso stays mellow underneath, giving you a coffee backbone without any bitterness. Many fans describe it as a caramel-vanilla coffee dessert you can drink through a straw.

If you prefer a lighter take, ask for oat milk or almond milk instead of the half-and-half. Want it bolder? Add an extra espresso shot or request caramel drizzle on top. The Blondie also works as an iced chiller — blended frozen — which brings out the vanilla and makes it taste almost like a melted milkshake. It is one of the most approachable drinks on the 7 Brew menu, and a go-to recommendation for first-timers who enjoy sweet coffee.

How to order it

An example order is: “A medium iced Blondie, please.” Add your preferred sweetness, milk and topping choices before the order is finalized. The official menu lists hot, iced and frozen chiller preparations for the Originals. Ask the stand to confirm any substitution.

Quarter, half, regular and extra sweetness are menu options. “Half sweet” describes a flavor adjustment; it does not mean half the total calories.

Which coffee should you choose?

A caramel-and-vanilla option for readers who want a creamy coffee without a chocolate flavor base.

Brunette adds hazelnut and uses a mocha base. Smooth 7 uses Irish cream and white chocolate.

Is there a sugar-free Blondie Breve?

Yes. Ask for sugar-free syrup and a medium drops 32 g of sugar and 80 calories, going by 7 Brew's published nutrition. The milk still carries some natural sugar, so it is low-sugar rather than zero. Our sugar-free menu and checker shows the same comparison for every drink that can be swapped.

Make a Blondie at Home (Copycat Recipe)

People search for this recipe more than almost anything else about the Blondie, so here it is. This is a home copycat built from the stand's formula: equal parts caramel and vanilla, espresso, and half-and-half instead of milk. That last part is the whole secret. A Blondie is a breve, and milk makes it taste thin.

For one medium iced Blondie (24 oz cup)

  • 2 shots of espresso (about 2 oz), hot
  • 1 oz caramel syrup (2 tablespoons)
  • 1 oz vanilla syrup (2 tablespoons)
  • About 12 oz half-and-half
  • A full cup of ice
  1. Pour the caramel and vanilla syrup into the cup.
  2. Add the hot espresso and stir. The heat melts the syrups into the shot so nothing sinks.
  3. Fill the cup with ice, then pour the half-and-half over the top.
  4. Stir once and drink. That is the whole drink.

Hot version: steam the half-and-half instead, skip the ice, same syrup and shots. Sugar-free version: swap both syrups for sugar-free ones and you land near the stand's sugar-free Blondie, about 400 calories with 6 g of sugar in a medium. No espresso machine? Use 2 oz of very strong coffee or a double shot from a pod machine. It will not be identical, but the caramel, vanilla and half-and-half carry the flavor.

Frequently Asked Questions About the Blondie

How many calories are in a 7 Brew Blondie?
A medium (24 oz) iced Blondie has 480 calories. The small iced is 280 and the large iced is 560. Hot runs higher (610 for a medium) and a frozen chiller medium is 490. These are the official nutrition guide figures.
What does a 7 Brew Blondie taste like?
The Blondie tastes like a creamy caramel-vanilla coffee dessert. The first sip is buttery caramel followed by smooth vanilla, all carried by a rich half-and-half breve base. The espresso stays mellow underneath, making it sweet but not overpowering.
Can you get a sugar-free Blondie at 7 Brew?
Yes. Ask for sugar-free vanilla and sugar-free caramel syrups. A medium iced sugar-free Blondie has 400 calories and 6 g of sugar, against 480 calories and 38 g of sugar in the regular. The remaining sugar comes from the half-and-half itself.
How many espresso shots are in a 7 Brew Blondie?
Two shots in a small and a medium, and four in a large. That is why the caffeine doubles at the large size: 120 mg in a small or medium iced, 240 mg in a large.
What is the difference between a Blondie and a latte?
The dairy and the flavor. A Blondie is a breve, built on half-and-half, so it is richer and creamier than a latte's whole milk. It also comes flavored by default, with equal parts caramel and vanilla syrup. A plain latte has neither unless you add it.
Can you get a hot Blondie at 7 Brew?
Yes. The hot Blondie is the same drink with steamed half-and-half and no ice. It runs higher in calories than the iced version, 610 for a medium, because the cup holds more of the breve base.
What milk is a 7 Brew Blondie made with?
Half-and-half by default. That is what breve means, and it is where the signature richness and most of the calories come from. You can swap in oat, almond or whole milk, and each one makes it lighter and a little less creamy.
What is the Starbucks equivalent of a 7 Brew Blondie?
Order an iced Blonde Vanilla Latte, swap the milk for half-and-half (breve), and add caramel syrup. That gets you the same build: vanilla, caramel, espresso and a cream base.
What is the difference between a Blondie and a Brunette at 7 Brew?
The flavor. A Blondie is caramel and vanilla. A Brunette swaps in hazelnut and caramel over a mocha base, so it tastes like chocolate-hazelnut where the Blondie tastes like caramel. The full comparison is on the Brunette guide.
What does breve mean, and why do macro posts say to order a Blondie 'no breve'?
Breve means the drink is made with half-and-half instead of milk. Most 7 Brew coffee drinks, including the Blondie, are breves by default. When a macro post tells you to order a small iced latte with almond milk and say no breve, it is just making the swap explicit: no half-and-half, almond milk instead. That is also why those posts show numbers near 60 calories. That is not a standard Blondie with a tweak. A standard medium iced Blondie is 480 calories. The macro version is a different, much lighter build that borrows the Blondie flavors.
How many calories are in a large Cookie Butter Blondie with extra sweet, cold foam, and caramel drizzle?
There is no official number for that exact cup, and anyone who gives you one is guessing. Start from the standard build: a large iced Blondie is 560 calories before extras. Extra sweet, cold foam, and caramel drizzle all add calories and sugar on top, and 7 Brew does not publish per-add-in nutrition to total them exactly. The guesses in fan groups run from about 800 to nearly 1,000 for this build. Treat them as guesses. If you want the flavor for fewer calories, the sugar-free pattern shows the lever: a medium Blondie drops from 480 to 400 calories when the syrups go sugar-free.
