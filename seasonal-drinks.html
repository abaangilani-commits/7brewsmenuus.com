7 Brew Seasonal Drinks & Fall Menu (2026 Guide)

7 Brew rolls out four quarterly seasonal menus each year, headlined by the autumn return of the Pumpkin Blondie (pumpkin pie sauce, caramel, and vanilla breve) and Apple Butter Chai. Seasonal drinks cost $4.75 for a Small, $5.50 for a Medium, and $6.50 for a Large. Fall items launch in late August and run through Thanksgiving, followed immediately by holiday peppermint and gingerbread drinks. You can also order several seasonal classics year-round using standard syrups. Scroll down to view official launch schedules, tasting notes, and step-by-step off-season ordering scripts below.

When Do Seasonal Drinks Launch & End?

7 Brew operates on a four-season rotating specialty drink schedule that covers fall, winter, spring, and summer. Each limited time offering LTO runs for roughly 8–12 weeks. The rotation covers fall winter spring summer with unique drinks each season. Here is when each new flavor launch typically happens:

  • Fall Menu — Mid-August to late November. Pumpkin spice, apple, and warm bakery flavors. The most anticipated seasonal window. Think pumpkin spice holiday favorites and cozy autumn blends.
  • Holiday / Winter Menu — Late November to late January. Holiday themed drinks including peppermint mocha gingerbread lattes, and sugar cookie breves.
  • Spring Menu — February to May. Lighter florals like lavender, berry-forward flavors, and refreshing citrus builds.
  • Summer Menu — June to mid-August. Tropical fruit energy combos, frozen chillers, and Italian soda specials.

When does seasonal end? Most seasonal syrups disappear once the next season's menu launches — so if you love a flavor, stock up before the window closes. Some items become returning favorite comeback drinks the following year based on customer demand. The current menu available now features the fall 2026 lineup listed above. That lineup — the Pumpkin Blondie Breve, Caramel Apple Pie Macchiato, Drizzled Apple 7 Energy Frozen Chiller, Apple Butter Chai Latte and Pumpkin Roll Shake — became available at participating stands starting September 1, 2026, per Tasting Table’s fall drinks review.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions: 7 Brew Seasonal Drinks

Clear answers to top customer questions about launch dates, recipes, and seasonal pricing.

When does 7 Brew release their fall pumpkin menu? +

7 Brew launches its official Fall Menu in mid-August every year (typically between August 15 and August 22) and runs it through Thanksgiving week in late November. Holiday winter drinks immediately follow in early December. Check national launch announcements on the official 7 Brew website (7brew.com) ↗.

What is in the 7 Brew Pumpkin Blondie? +

The Pumpkin Blondie takes 7 Brew's signature caramel and vanilla breve base and blends in real spiced pumpkin puree sauce, topped with cinnamon sprinkles and optional pumpkin cold foam. It contains double espresso in Medium and quad espresso in Large.

Can you order seasonal drinks out of season at 7 Brew? +

Drinks relying on permanent Torani syrups (like Brown Sugar Cinnamon, Vanilla, Salted Caramel, or Peppermint) can be made year-round. Limited pumpkin puree sauces and specialty seasonal cold foams are only stocked while seasonal supplies last.

Does 7 Brew offer sugar-free options for fall pumpkin drinks? +

While the actual pumpkin pie sauce contains real cane sugar, you can request sugar-free vanilla, sugar-free caramel, and sugar-free brown sugar cinnamon syrups. This cuts over 60% of the sugar content while keeping the authentic spiced autumn flavor.

How much do seasonal drinks cost compared to regular menu drinks? +

Seasonal drinks typically cost around $0.25 to $0.50 more than basic classics because they include specialty sauces like real pumpkin puree or artisan peppermint bark drizzle. A Medium seasonal drink runs between $5.25 and $5.75 depending on regional stand pricing.
