7 Brew Drive-Thru Hours & What to Expect

Most 7 Brew drive-thru coffee stands are open 7 days a week from 5:30 AM to 10:00 PM — those are the standard weekly operating hours across the chain, though store hours may vary by location. Every 7 Brew is drive-thru only with no indoor seating. Pull up to the window, tell the brewista your order, and you are typically on your way in under 3 minutes — the average wait time is one of the fastest in the drive-thru energy drink and coffee industry. Holiday hours

7 Brew does not publish chain-wide holiday hours, and every stand is independently owned, so holiday schedules are set locally. Most stands shorten hours around Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve and New Year's Eve, and most close on Christmas Day. See the 7 Brew hours guide for the standard schedule and how to check your own stand before a holiday trip.
