7 Brew Drive-Thru Hours & What to Expect

Most 7 Brew drive-thru coffee stands are open 7 days a week from 5:30 AM to 10:00 PM — those are the standard weekly operating hours across the chain, though store hours may vary by location. Every 7 Brew is drive-thru only with no indoor seating. Pull up to the window, tell the brewista your order, and you are typically on your way in under 3 minutes — the average wait time is one of the fastest in the drive-thru energy drink and coffee industry. 7 Brew Holiday Hours: Christmas, Thanksgiving & New Year's

Is 7 Brew open on Christmas? Most 7 Brew stands close for Christmas Day and Thanksgiving Day, the two holidays observed chain-wide. Hours are commonly shortened on Christmas Eve and New Year's Eve, and some stands open later or close earlier on New Year's Day. Because every 7 Brew is independently owned and operated, exact holiday hours are set stand by stand — always check the official stand listing or call ahead before a holiday visit. Sunday hours are not reduced: nearly every stand runs its full standard schedule seven days a week, including Sundays.

7 Brew Hours: Frequently Asked Questions

What time does 7 Brew open?

Most 7 Brew stands open at 5:30 AM, seven days a week. A handful of stands open earlier or later depending on local traffic patterns — confirm your exact stand's opening time in the directory below.

What time does 7 Brew close?

Most stands close at 10:00 PM Sunday through Thursday and 11:00 PM on Friday and Saturday, though hours vary by market. Check the weekly hours listed under each stand below.

Is 7 Brew open on Christmas?

Most 7 Brew stands are closed on Christmas Day, along with Thanksgiving Day. Some locations shorten hours on Christmas Eve. Because scheduling is set by each franchise owner, call your local stand to confirm before visiting on or near a holiday.

Is 7 Brew open on Sundays?

Yes. Almost every 7 Brew stand is open on Sundays with the same hours as the rest of the week — there is no separate reduced Sunday schedule at most locations.
