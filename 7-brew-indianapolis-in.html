About 7 Brew in Indianapolis, Indiana

Indianapolis has three 7 Brew drive-thru stands spanning the south and southeast side of the metro — on South East Street, East County Line Road, and South Emerson Avenue. Indiana is one of 7 Brew's strongest Midwest markets with 30 locations statewide, and the Indy stands give Circle City commuters a fast alternative to traditional coffee shops.

7 Brew's menu revolves around breve-style espresso drinks, where half-and-half replaces regular milk for a noticeably creamier cup. Beyond espresso, the full menu includes 7 Energy drinks, Italian sodas, smoothies, hot chocolate, and shakes — all available in three sizes. Check our sizes and caffeine guide to compare the 16 oz small, 24 oz medium, and 32 oz large.

Popular orders for Indianapolis first-timers include the Blondie (vanilla-caramel breve), the Brunette (hazelnut-mocha), and any secret menu drink you've seen on TikTok — just tell the barista the recipe. Every drink can be made with sugar-free syrups, and our calorie calculator lets you compare options before you arrive.

Indianapolis stands typically open at 5:30 AM and close around 10:00 PM seven days a week, including weekends. The drive-thru-only format means there is no indoor seating — pull up, order through the window, and you are on your way in minutes. Check the best drinks ranking for our top recommendations.
