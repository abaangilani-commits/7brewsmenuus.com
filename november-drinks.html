7 Brew November Drinks 2026: A Bakery Menu, Six Dessert Drinks and Three Deals

Your drive-thru run is about to get longer. From Nov 1, 7 Brew adds its first bakery lineup: five treats at $3 each, three of them inspired by the Blondie, Brunette and Banana Bread drinks. Six dessert-style drinks headline the November menu, and two of the new flavors, French Silk and Tiramisu, are staying for good. Here is everything landing, what it costs, and the three November dates worth circling.

✓ Bakery from Nov 1, $3 a Treat • 6 November Drinks • Double Points on Nov 7
Prices and nutrition: Bakery prices ($3 each) and every drink build below come from 7 Brew's October 29 announcement. 7 Brew has not published prices or calories for the six November drinks yet, and none of the new items are in the current official nutrition guide. We will add the official numbers when the new guide drops instead of guessing.

What is in the 7 Brew bakery lineup?

Five items, one price. Every treat in the lineup costs $3, and three of them are the chain's own drinks turned into snacks. The Blondie, Brunette and Banana Bread each get a matching bite, next to a classic cookie bar and a blueberry loaf.

Until now, the whole food menu was three muffin tops. They are not going anywhere, so the food count jumps from three items to eight overnight. One more detail worth knowing before you order: bakery items earn 50 rewards points each, half a drink's worth, under the normal rewards rules. Calories for the five new items are not published yet. The muffin tops run 470 to 560 calories in the official guide, and we will post the new items' numbers the same way once 7 Brew releases them.

Are French Silk and Tiramisu really staying?

Yes, and it is the most useful line in the whole announcement. The drinks rotate with the month, but the two new flavors stay at every stand for good. Fall in love with the French Silk Mocha in November and you can still build on that flavor in February. Same for Tiramisu in a cold brew or anything else you pair it with. Featured menus come and go; a permanent flavor quietly grows the menu forever. The standing lineup is always tracked on the seasonal drinks page.

What are the November dates to know?

  • Nov 1: the bakery lineup goes on sale, $3 per item, at participating stands nationwide.
  • Sat, Nov 7, Jackpot Day: rewards members earn double points from 7 AM to 7 PM, and every qualifying order earns one entry into a sweepstakes for 100,000 rewards points. 7 Brew says the prize covers over two years of free drinks and bakery items.
  • Wed, Nov 11, Veterans Day: veterans get 50% off any drink, all day.
  • Nov 27 to Dec 24, Give Joy, Get Joy: members earn a free drink reward for every $25 in gift cards bought in stand. That is the stocking-stuffer math sorted.

What happens to the October drinks?

October's Halloween lineup, the Pumpkin Blondie, Rocky Road Mocha, Nightshade and the rest, was built to end with the month, closing out with the Fill-A-Tray deal on Oct 31. November's menu takes their slot on the featured board. Individual stands sometimes keep a seasonal syrup a little longer, so it never hurts to ask, but do not count on it.

November drinks and bakery FAQ

When do the 7 Brew November drinks come out?

The bakery lineup has a hard launch date: November 1, at participating stands nationwide. The six drinks were announced as the November featured lineup, so expect them on the menu as November starts.

How much are the 7 Brew bakery items?

Every item in the new bakery lineup costs $3: the Blondie Bar, Brunette Mocha Brownie Bar, Chocolate Chunk Cookie Bar, Banana Bread Mocha Chip Loaf and Blueberry Streusel Loaf. The older muffin tops are priced separately by stand, usually reported between $2.50 and $3.50.

What food does 7 Brew have now?

Eight items. The five new bakery treats (three bars and two loaves) join the three muffin tops that were the whole food menu until now: Blueberry, Chocolate Chip and Lemon Poppyseed.

Are the French Silk and Tiramisu flavors permanent?

Yes. 7 Brew says the French Silk and Tiramisu flavors stay at all stands after November ends, so you can keep ordering drinks built on them even when the featured lineup rotates.

What is the Jackpot Day deal on November 7?

Rewards members earn double points from 7 AM to 7 PM at participating stands, and every qualifying order earns one entry into a sweepstakes for 100,000 rewards points. 7 Brew says that prize is good for over two years of free drinks and bakery items.

Does 7 Brew have a Veterans Day deal?

Yes. On November 11, veterans get 50% off any drink, all day.

What are the calories in the new November drinks?

7 Brew has not published them yet. The six drinks and the five bakery items are not in the current official nutrition guide. We will add the official numbers here and to the calorie calculator as soon as the new guide is out, instead of guessing.
