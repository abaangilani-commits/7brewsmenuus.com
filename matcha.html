Customizing your order

Matcha is a tea-based choice. Choosing a drink without coffee does not establish that it is caffeine-free.

Tell the stand the drink base, size, flavor combination and any requested adjustments. Ask about availability before assuming a flavor or topping can be substituted. Browse flavor and sweetness options.

How to Order a Matcha at 7 Brew

At the drive-thru, ask for a matcha latte in your preferred size — small (16 oz), medium (24 oz), or large (32 oz) — and specify iced, hot, or frozen chiller. The standard build uses a sweetened matcha powder with steamed half-and-half (breve style), which gives it a rich, creamy body similar to 7 Brew's signature espresso drinks like the Blondie or Smooth 7. For a lighter version, swap to oat milk or almond milk and ask for sugar-free vanilla syrup. The matcha also works as a base for custom combinations — try adding coconut or lavender syrup for a unique twist, or check the full syrup list for more ideas. See the calorie calculator to compare sizes and milk options before you order.

Frequently Asked Questions About 7 Brew Matcha

How many calories are in a 7 Brew matcha latte?
A medium (24 oz) matcha latte made with half-and-half (breve style) has approximately 380 calories. Ordering with oat milk or almond milk instead reduces the count significantly. The small (16 oz) breve version is around 250 calories.
Does 7 Brew matcha have caffeine?
Yes. A medium matcha latte contains roughly 120 mg of caffeine from the matcha green tea powder — comparable to a single espresso shot. Unlike espresso drinks, matcha delivers its caffeine more gradually, so the energy feels smoother and longer-lasting.
Can you order a 7 Brew matcha iced or frozen?
Yes. The matcha latte is available hot, iced, or as a frozen chiller at the drive-thru. The iced version is the most popular order. You can also pair matcha with 7 Energy for a matcha-energy hybrid — ask for a Strawberry Matcha Energy for one of the most-ordered combinations.
