The caffeine number every other page gets wrong

A medium iced Matcha Latte has 17 mg of caffeine. Say it plainly because the internet does not: fan pages quote 70, 100, even 140 mg, and this page used to print 120. Those numbers come from generic matcha math (so many milligrams per gram of powder) applied to a drink whose actual build nobody checked. The guide settles it. Small 11, medium 17, large 22, iced. Hot runs a little higher at 22 in a medium, and the frozen chiller is the strongest form at 34 in a medium and 45 in a large, because the chiller build carries more matcha base. Even at its strongest, matcha is the gentlest caffeinated latte on the menu. A chai beats it. An espresso drink has seven times the caffeine of the iced latte.

The three named matchas, side by side

One base, three builds, and the numbers are closer than the flavors. Medium iced: Strawberry Matcha is 300 calories and 52 g of sugar. Cereal Milk Matcha is 310 and 53, built on sugar-free toasted marshmallow and sugar-free vanilla over a pre-sweetened base. Cloud 9 Matcha is 310 and 55, lavender and vanilla, and Cloud 9 is a menu-wide flavor, not a matcha exclusive. As frozen chillers they run 400, 410 and 420. All three sit at the same 17 mg of caffeine iced. Pick on flavor. The nutrition will not rescue or ruin you either way.

Two traps to know. The sugar-free matchas still carry about 32 g of sugar in a medium, because the sugar lives in the base, not the syrups. The full story is on the sugar-free page. And matcha is not green tea: separate guide category, separate numbers (60 mg and 0 calories for a medium iced green tea, against matcha's 17 mg and 240).

How to Order a Matcha at 7 Brew

At the drive-thru, ask for a matcha latte in your preferred size — small (16 oz), medium (24 oz), or large (32 oz) — and specify iced, hot, or frozen chiller. The standard build uses a sweetened matcha powder with steamed half-and-half (breve style), which gives it a rich, creamy body similar to 7 Brew's signature espresso drinks like the Blondie or Smooth 7. For a lighter version, swap to oat milk or almond milk and ask for sugar-free vanilla syrup. The matcha also works as a base for custom combinations — try adding coconut or lavender syrup for a unique twist, or check the full syrup list for more ideas. See the calorie calculator to compare sizes and milk options before you order.

Frequently Asked Questions About 7 Brew Matcha

How many calories are in a 7 Brew matcha latte?
A medium iced Matcha Latte has 240 calories with milk, per the official guide. There is no breve matcha in the guide. The flavored builds run higher: 300 for Strawberry, 310 for Cereal Milk and Cloud 9. A medium frozen chiller is 350. Alt milks change the total somewhat, but the guide publishes the standard build, and 240 is the number to plan around. (This answer previously cited a 380-calorie breve estimate. The guide does not list one.) Reducingces the count significantly. The small (16 oz) breve version is around 250 calories.
Does 7 Brew matcha have caffeine?
Yes. A medium iced matcha latte contains 17 mg of caffeine, per the official guide. Not 120. The 120 mg figure doing the rounds is espresso math pasted onto a drink with no espresso in it. Unlike espresso drinks, matcha delivers its caffeine more gradually, so the energy feels smoother and longer-lasting.
Can you order a 7 Brew matcha iced or frozen?
Yes. The matcha latte is available hot, iced, or as a frozen chiller at the drive-thru. The iced version is the most popular order. You can also pair matcha with 7 Energy for a matcha-energy hybrid — ask for a Strawberry Matcha Energy for one of the most-ordered combinations.
Which 7 Brew matcha has the most caffeine?

A large Matcha Frozen Chiller, at 45 mg. Chillers use more matcha base than the iced latte (34 mg in a medium chiller against 17 iced). Even the strongest matcha on the menu carries about a third of the caffeine of a medium espresso drink.

What matcha flavors does 7 Brew have?

Three named builds on one base: Strawberry Matcha, Cereal Milk Matcha (sugar-free toasted marshmallow and sugar-free vanilla in a pre-sweetened base) and Cloud 9 Matcha (lavender and vanilla). Plus the plain Matcha Latte and Build Your Own. The FrankenChai matcha is a seasonal build. Each flavor has its own page here.

Is matcha the same as green tea at 7 Brew?

No. They are separate categories in the guide with separate numbers. A medium iced Green Tea has 60 mg of caffeine and 0 calories unsweetened. A medium iced Matcha Latte has 17 mg and 240 calories, because it is a milk drink built on a sweetened matcha base. People mix them up constantly, including in Google's own answers.

Is sugar-free matcha really sugar-free?

The syrups are. The drink is not. The matcha base is pre-sweetened, so a medium sugar-free Cereal Milk Matcha still carries 32 g of sugar, and the sugar-free Strawberry Matcha the same. It is the cleanest example on the menu of sugar living in the base rather than the flavor.

How many calories are in a 7 Brew matcha?

240 in a medium iced plain Matcha Latte. The named flavors run 300 to 310 in a medium iced (Strawberry 300, Cereal Milk 310, Cloud 9 310), and the frozen chillers 350 to 420. A large iced plain is 330.
