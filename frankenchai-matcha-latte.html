7 Brew FrankenChai Matcha Latte (Halloween 2026)

7 Brew's "Something's Brewing" tease turned out to be the FrankenChai Matcha Latte, revealed October 12, 2026. It is a separate drop from the five-drink "Booost Your Brew" lineup that launched October 1, not part of it. It is only around through October 31.

What is the FrankenChai

It is a mashup of two 7 Brew drinks: their Pumpkin Chai Latte and matcha. 7 Brew calls it "a monstrously smooth mashup of fall flavor." The base drinks are not new, so what is new is the combination and the cup.

The drink comes in a limited-edition Frankenstein-inspired cup, available with any medium iced or blended drink through October 31, while supplies last.

Price

Roughly $5 to $6.50 for a medium. That is an estimate from checked menu sources, not an official 7 Brew price. Confirm at your stand.

How to order it

Order the FrankenChai Matcha Latte by name through October 31. It works iced or blended in a medium, which is how you get the Frankenstein cup. If your stand has run out of the cups, the drink itself is still orderable.

Note the sugar-free angle: 7 Brew's matcha and chai both come in sugar-free versions at most stands, so ask if you want it lighter. Confirm what your stand can do.

The protein boost works here too

7 Brew's nationwide protein boost (15 grams for $2) can be added to any drink, and the FrankenChai is no exception. It is an unflavored add-on, so it does not change the pumpkin chai and matcha flavor.

Bottom line

The FrankenChai is the one truly new 7 Brew drink of the Halloween season, and the only one with its own cup. It is available through October 31, then it is gone. If you want to try it, this week is the window.
