7 Brew Deals: Jackpot Day, BOGO and Free Drinks (2026)

7 Brew's dependable offer is Jackpot Day on the 7th of every month, 7 a.m. to 7 p.m. — next on October 7, 2026. Everything else comes through the rewards program or the app. Here is what is real, what is not, and how to check today.

Every 7 Brew is independently owned, so local stands can run promotions that never get a national announcement. Asking at the window costs nothing.

Jackpot Day is the one to plan around

It falls on the 7th of every month without exception and runs 7 a.m. to 7 p.m. — the "777" behind the name. The format changes monthly and is usually announced a day or two beforehand, so the date is predictable even when the deal is not. September 2026, for example, gave a free 7 Brew Doh with two medium or large drinks.

See the next twelve Jackpot Day dates, what past months offered, and how to check this month's deal.

What is not a 7 Brew deal

A few things circulate that are worth correcting, because chasing them wastes a trip:

  • No nationwide happy hour. Some chains discount an afternoon window daily; 7 Brew does not do this nationally.
  • No coupon codes in the usual sense. Offers come through the app and the rewards program, and Jackpot Day needs no code.
  • No automatic free drink at 1,000 points any more. Since 2026 you choose when to redeem in the app's Rewards tab — points sit there until you spend them.
  • Energy cans and kids-size drinks stopped earning points on 12 August 2026, so they do not move you toward a free drink.

Getting the most out of the rewards program

Points are per drink, not per dollar: a small earns exactly what a large earns. That means the cheapest way to a free drink is ordering smalls, and the best way to spend one is on a large or a loaded custom build. Full mechanics, including how to check your balance, are on the 7 Brew Rewards page.

7 Brew deals FAQ

Does 7 Brew have a happy hour?

There is no standing nationwide happy hour. The dependable monthly promotion is Jackpot Day on the 7th, and individual stands occasionally run their own local offers.

Does 7 Brew have coupons?

Not printed coupons in the usual sense. Offers arrive through the 7 Brew app and the rewards program rather than coupon codes, and Jackpot Day needs no code at all.

How do I get a free drink at 7 Brew?

Collect 1,000 rewards points — 100 per qualifying drink, so roughly every 10th drink — then choose the free-drink reward in the app’s Rewards tab. Jackpot Day can also include a free item with a qualifying purchase.

Is there a 7 Brew student or military discount?

Nothing is published nationally. Some stands offer local discounts at their own discretion, so it is worth asking at the window.
