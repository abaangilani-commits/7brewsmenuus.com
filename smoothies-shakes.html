Milkshake vs Smoothie at 7 Brew: What's the Difference?

7 Brew's smoothies and shakes look similar on the menu but use different bases. Shakes are ice-cream-based blended frozen drink options — thick, creamy, and dessert-forward — a true ice cream based dessert in a cup. Flavors include Birthday Cake, Rocky Road, Cookies & Cream, Salted Caramel, and Orange Sherbet. Smoothies feature fruit smoothie flavors using a fruit-based blend without ice cream, making them lighter in both texture and calories. Both are a great kids caffeine-free option and come in all three cup sizes.

Shakes are the heavier option — a large Birthday Cake shake can run 800+ calories — while smoothies in the same size stay closer to 300–400 calories. If you are watching macros, the nutrition calculator breaks down exact calorie, sugar, and fat counts by size and flavor.

Best Shake and Smoothie Orders

First-timers should try the Birthday Cake shake — it is consistently the best seller in this category and delivers a genuine cake-batter flavor. For something less sweet, the Rocky Road (chocolate, marshmallow, and nut flavoring) or Salted Caramel offer a more balanced profile. The Orange Sherbet shake is the lightest of the group and tastes closest to a creamsicle.

Smoothies work well as a non-coffee, non-carbonated option for customers who skip both espresso and energy drinks. Pair a smoothie with a pastry from the drive-thru snack menu for a quick breakfast combo. Can You Add Protein? Shake Supplement Options

While 7 Brew does not currently offer a dedicated protein shake supplement add-on, you can customize your shake or smoothie with extra ingredients to boost nutrition. Some stands may carry protein powder as an off-menu addition — ask your brewista if it is available at your location. For a thick creamy blended texture with more substance, request an extra scoop of ice cream in your shake or double the fruit base in your smoothie. Every shake and smoothie is finished with a whipped cream topping and drizzle by default — skip the whip to cut 50–80 calories, or add extra for an indulgent finish.
