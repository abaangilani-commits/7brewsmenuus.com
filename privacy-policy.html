Privacy Policy

7 Brew Menu Guide USA respects your personal privacy. We collect basic server log data and standard browser cookies to monitor website performance, analyze search traffic, and deliver accurate beverage pricing information. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. Scroll down to review our complete data collection practices, cookie management options, advertising disclosures, and your rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and European GDPR below.

✓ Effective Date: September 2026 • CCPA / CPRA Compliant • GDPR Consumer Rights

1. Overview & Information We Collect

This Privacy Policy describes the policies of 7 Brew Menu Guide USA (accessible at https://7brewsmenuus.com) regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of your information when you visit our website.

We do not require user accounts, logins, or credit card submissions to access our menu catalogs, nutritional calculators, or stand locator. The types of data we receive include:

  • Log File Information: Like most standard website servers, we log internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, internet service provider (ISP), referring and exit pages, operating system, date and time stamp, and clickstream data. This data is not linked to any personally identifiable information.
  • Device & Geolocation Data: When you voluntarily click the "Use My Location" feature on our stand locator, your browser requests permission to access your geographic coordinates. These coordinates are processed exclusively in your local browser memory to calculate distance to nearby drive-thrus and are never stored on our servers.
  • Voluntary Communications: If you contact us via email to report a price correction or franchise update, we retain your email address and message contents solely to respond to your inquiry.

2. Cookies & Local Storage

7 Brew Menu Guide USA uses standard cookies to store visitor preferences, record user-specific information on which pages the visitor accesses, and personalize webpage content based on your browser type.

You can choose to disable cookies through your individual browser options. Detailed information about cookie management with specific web browsers can be found at the browsers' respective websites.

3. Third-Party Advertising & Analytics

We may partner with third-party advertising networks (such as Google AdSense) and analytics providers (such as Google Analytics) to serve advertisements and analyze web traffic.

  • Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on our site based on prior visits to this website or other sites on the internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings at https://adssettings.google.com.
  • Third-party ad servers or networks use technology in their respective advertisements and links that appear on our site. They automatically receive your IP address when this occurs.

4. California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA / CPRA) Rights

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act and California Privacy Rights Act, California consumers have specific rights regarding their personal information:

  • The right to know the categories and specific pieces of personal data collected about you.
  • The right to request the deletion of personal data collected by our website.
  • The right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal data. We do not sell personal data.
  • The right to non-discrimination for exercising any of your consumer privacy rights.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact our editorial privacy team at Contact channel not configured; see contact.html.. We respond to verified consumer requests within 45 days.

5. European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) Rights

If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA), you possess statutory data protection rights under the GDPR:

  • Right of Access: You can request copies of any personal data we hold.
  • Right to Rectification: You can request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Right to Erasure: You can request that we erase your personal data under certain legal conditions.
  • Right to Restrict Processing: You have the right to request restriction of personal data processing.
  • Right to Data Portability: You can request the transfer of your data to another organization.

6. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)

Protecting the privacy of children is especially important to us. 7 Brew Menu Guide USA is a general audience informational site and does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If a parent or guardian believes that our platform holds personal data of a child under 13, please contact us immediately, and we will promptly remove such information from our records.

7. External Links & Third-Party Sites

Our platform contains links to external websites, including the official 7 Brew corporate portal (7brew.com), mapping applications (Google Maps), and ingredient suppliers (Torani). We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or cookie management of these external sites. We encourage users to review the privacy statements of any external site they visit.
