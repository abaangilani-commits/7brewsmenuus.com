7 Brew Secret Menu: 102 Drink Combinations & Order Scripts

The 7 Brew secret menu consists of customer-invented drink combinations made by mixing standard Torani syrups into coffee, energy, or fizz bases. Popular secret drinks include the Cookie Butter Chiller (white chocolate and speculoos cookie flavor), Nightshade Energy (lavender, blue raspberry, and pomegranate), and Pink Mermaid Fizz. Because franchise baristas may not recognize informal social media nicknames, you should always order by describing the exact syrup formula. The Golden Rule for 7 Brew Secret Menu Ordering

Learning how to order secret menu drinks at 7 Brew starts with one rule: never use the nickname. These are hidden menu items — off-menu creations built from standard bases and syrup combinations — so most baristas won't recognize names like "Harry Potter" or "Barbie Drink." Instead, use our Word-for-Word Order Script with the exact barista recipe provided for every custom order hack below, and follow the 5-step drive-thru ordering formula. What does a secret menu drink cost?

The same as the drink it is built on. A medium coffee build is $5.50, a medium 7 Energy is $6.50, and a medium 7 Fizz, lemonade or tea is $3.50. The flavor combination itself is free.

Say the flavors, not just the name

Most stands know the big names. For anything else, read the flavor list from the directory below. That always works, even with a brand new Brewista.

Any combo works on any base

These are flavor combinations, not fixed drinks. A coffee combo can go on tea. An energy combo can go on a Fizz. The directory lists the base most people pick. Yours can be different.

Frequently Asked Questions: 7 Brew Secret Menu

Answers to common customer questions about secret drink recipes, extra charges, and ordering.

Does 7 Brew have an official secret menu? +

No. 7 Brew does not publish an official secret menu booklet. Secret drinks are customer and barista flavor combinations made by combining standard menu bases with 7 Brew's 30+ Torani syrups and sauces. Stand workers are happy to make any custom recipe if you list the syrups.

Do secret menu drinks cost extra at 7 Brew? +

7 Brew does not charge extra simply for ordering a secret combination. You pay the standard base drink price (for example, Breve or 7 Energy) plus standard small upcharges for extra shots ($0.75) or sweet cream cold foam ($1.00).

What is the most popular 7 Brew secret menu drink? +

The Cookie Butter Chiller is the number one viral secret drink nationwide. It combines toasted marshmallow, white chocolate, and hazelnut syrups in a blended espresso breve.

Can baristas make a secret menu drink if I only know the nickname? +

Many popular nicknames like Cookie Butter or Butterbeer are recognized at most stands. However, newer baristas might not know every TikTok creation. We always recommend reciting the base and syrups using our order scripts.

Can you order 7 Brew secret menu drinks sugar-free? +

Yes. 7 Brew stocks 12 Torani sugar-free syrups. You can request any secret recipe made sugar-free, such as a Sugar-Free Cinnamon Roll Breve or a Sugar-Free Tigers Blood 7 Energy.

Is there a 7 Brew energy secret menu?+

Yes, in the sense that most custom builds are energy drinks. The 7 Energy base takes any syrup combination, so the energy side of the secret menu is the largest — see the energy builds above, or the full 7 Energy flavour list.

Is there a 7 Fizz secret menu?+

Same idea with a soda-water base instead of the energy base. Any syrup combination that works in a 7 Energy works in a 7 Fizz; the 7 Fizz menu has the four standard flavours to build from.

Is there a 7 Brew secret menu PDF?+

Not an official one — 7 Brew publishes no secret menu, so any PDF claiming to be one is unofficial. You can download our printable 7 Brew menu PDF of the standard menu with prices instead.

What are the prices of drinks on the 7 Brew secret menu? +

A secret menu drink costs the same as the base drink it is built on. Mediums run $5.50 on a coffee base like a breve or latte, $6.50 on a 7 Energy, and $3.50 on a 7 Fizz, lemonade or tea. There is no charge for the flavor combination itself. Extra shots ($0.75) and cold foam ($1.00) carry the standard add-on prices.

What's in the Pink Mermaid at 7 Brew? +

Watermelon, strawberry and coconut syrups. It is most often ordered on a 7 Fizz base, and it works just as well on a 7 Energy. If the stand does not know the name, ask for those three flavors by name.

What does extra sweet mean at 7 Brew? +

It means extra syrup on top of the standard build. Every drink has a standard sweetness level. You can order half sweet or quarter sweet for less, or extra sweet for a stronger, sweeter drink. Sugar-free syrups can be made extra sweet too.

How do I order a secret menu drink at 7 Brew? Will the stand know the name?

Secret menu drinks are flavor combinations, not separate menu items. Most stands know the popular names, and many combos sit right in the order system. The safe way to order is name plus build: say the secret menu name, then the base and size, like a medium iced breve. If the name draws a blank, just give the flavors. Every secret menu drink is a base drink (breve, energy, fizz, lemonade, or tea) plus a known flavor combo, so the flavors alone get you the same cup. Prices follow the base drink and size.
