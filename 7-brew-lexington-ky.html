About 7 Brew in Lexington, Kentucky

Lexington has four 7 Brew drive-thru stands covering the city from multiple directions — Remington Drive in the Hamburg area, Clay Mills Road near I-75, Harrodsburg Road on the southwest side, and Richmond Road serving the eastern neighborhoods. With Kentucky hosting over 30 7 Brew locations statewide, the Lexington cluster gives Bluegrass-area coffee drinkers easy access to the chain's signature breve-style espresso drinks.

7 Brew's breve base — espresso pulled over steamed half-and-half — sets it apart from most drive-thru coffee chains. The result is a thicker, creamier drink with a natural sweetness that requires less added sugar. Browse the complete 7 Brew menu with prices to see every category, from hot and iced espresso to 7 Energy drinks, Italian sodas, and shakes.

Lexington locals looking for a first order should try the Blondie or the Cookie Butter — both are rich without being overpowering. For a non-coffee pick, the 7 Energy line delivers caffeine and B-vitamins without espresso.
