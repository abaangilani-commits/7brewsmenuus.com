About 7 Brew in San Antonio, Texas

San Antonio has four 7 Brew drive-thru stands spread across the metro — on Culebra Road, SE Military Drive, Interstate 35, and Potranco Road. Texas is one of 7 Brew's largest state markets with over 110 locations, and the San Antonio cluster covers the west, south, and northeast sides of the city. Founded in Rogers, Arkansas in 2017, 7 Brew now operates more than 777 stands across 35-plus states.

The San Antonio stands serve the full 7 Brew menu, anchored by breve-style espresso drinks — coffee pulled over half-and-half for a richer body than standard lattes. The lineup also includes 7 Energy drinks, Italian sodas, smoothies, shakes, and teas. San Antonio's hot weather makes iced breves and blended drinks popular year-round — try the Blondie on ice or a frozen Banana Bread latte.

First-time visitors should check the best drinks guide for a ranked starting point, or ask the barista to walk you through the secret menu. Every drink can be customized with sugar-free syrups and alternative milks at no extra charge. Use the calorie calculator to adjust for your diet.

Most San Antonio 7 Brew stands open between 5:30 and 6:00 AM and close at 10:00 PM, seven days a week. Compare cup sizes — 16 oz, 24 oz, and 32 oz — to decide how much caffeine your morning commute needs, and download the 7 Brew app for rewards on every purchase.
