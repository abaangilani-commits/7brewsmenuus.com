Quick Answers

7 Brew Menu Prices at a Glance

In a hurry at the drive-thru? Here are the answers people ask for most. The full menu, with every drink and price table, is right below.

How much is a 7 Brew drink?

Most drinks cost between $2.75 and $7.50. The signature drinks, like the Blondie, are $4.50 for a small, $5.25 for a medium and $6.25 for a large. Prices vary a little from stand to stand.

What is the cheapest drink at 7 Brew?

The House Blend drip coffee, at $2.75 for a small. Iced teas, lemonades and 7 Fizz sodas also start at $2.75. The Pup Cup for your dog is free.

What is the most popular drink at 7 Brew?

The Blondie. It is a breve with caramel and vanilla, and it is the most ordered drink on the menu. It comes iced, hot or as a frozen chiller.

Does 7 Brew have food?

There is no kitchen. The one food item is the Muffin Top, a baked muffin top in blueberry, chocolate chip and lemon poppyseed. 7 Brew does not publish a price for it. Reports put it around $2.50 to $3.50, and stands set their own price. Everything else on the menu is a drink.

Why doesn't 7 Brew list prices?

Because every stand sets its own prices. The official site shows the drinks and leaves the prices off. The prices here are national estimates, checked in October 2026, and the full menu is right below.

What can I get at 7 Brew for $5?

More than you'd think. Any small or medium 7 Fizz or lemonade, a small latte at $3.95, a small chai or tea at $3.95, any small classic coffee at $4.25, and even a small Blondie at $4.50.

Fluid Ounces Breakdown

7 Brew Cup Sizes: Iced vs. Hot vs. Chiller

Three sizes for almost every drink, and the iced cups run larger than the hot ones. Espresso shots step up at the large. Our 7 Brew Cup Sizes (oz) & Caffeine Guide has the exact fluid ounce comparisons and the caffeine for every size. The price table by category is just below.

🥤

Small Size

16 oz Iced / 12 oz Hot

Milk Swaps

The official milk alternatives are oat, almond and coconut milk, and 7 Brew's own support pages say dairy-free swaps come at no extra charge. One catch: the mocha mix itself contains milk, so a mocha cannot be made fully dairy-free just by swapping the milk.

Low-Sugar Orders

Order it like this: an unsweet iced tea, a plain Americano (0 calories at every size in the official guide), or any coffee with sugar-free syrup and heavy cream instead of milk. Go easy on the flavored cold foams and drizzles. They add the sugar back.

About 7 Brew Coffee

7 Brew is a drive-thru coffee menu favorite and chain founded in 2017 in Rogers, Arkansas by Ron Crume, and it now has 900+ stands across 38 states, 7 Brew has grown into one of America's fastest-expanding specialty coffee brands — serving espresso breve latte and breve-style drinks, 7 Energy blends and energy drinks fizz options, Italian soda drinks, lemonades, smoothies and shakes, plus seasonal limited time specials, all from a drive-thru-only format. Every stand is open 7 days a week, typically from 5:30 AM to 10 PM.
