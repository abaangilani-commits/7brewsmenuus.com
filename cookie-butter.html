7 Brew Cookie Butter: Ingredients, Ordering & Drink Comparisons

Explore the Cookie Butter flavor combination, how to order it, alternatives and where to check the correct calories and caffeine for your preparation.

What is in a Cookie Butter?

Toasted Marshmallow + Hazelnut + White Chocolate Breve. It is listed in 7 Brew’s 7 Originals collection. Official Cookie Butter listing.

The name does not establish that cookie spread or biscuit pieces are ingredients. Use the listed flavor combination when explaining the drink, and check ingredients separately for allergies.

What Does Cookie Butter Taste Like?

The Cookie Butter tastes exactly like its name — warm, slightly caramelized, nutty, and smooth all at once. If you have ever had Biscoff cookie butter or speculoos spread, this drink captures that same cozy, spiced sweetness in coffee form. The flavor comes from three syrups working together: toasted marshmallow brings warmth and caramelized depth, hazelnut adds a nutty backbone, and white chocolate sauce smooths everything into a cohesive finish. None of those syrups tastes like cookie butter on its own, but combined they produce something that genuinely does.

The breve base — espresso with steamed half-and-half — gives the drink a thick, dessert-like body that makes the cookie butter flavor feel even more authentic. It went viral on TikTok for a reason: it is one of those drinks that tastes indulgent enough to be a treat but balanced enough to order daily. Try it as an iced chiller for a frozen, almost Biscoff-milkshake experience, or order it with sugar-free syrups to cut the sweetness. Pair it with a Bakery Muffin Top from the 7 Brew menu for the full cookie-butter moment.

How to order it

An example order is: “A medium iced Cookie Butter, please.” Add your preferred sweetness, milk and topping choices before the order is finalized. The official menu lists hot, iced and frozen chiller preparations for the Originals. Ask the stand to confirm any substitution.

Quarter, half, regular and extra sweetness are menu options. “Half sweet” describes a flavor adjustment; it does not mean half the total calories.

Which coffee should you choose?

Choose this flavor combination if toasted marshmallow, hazelnut and white chocolate appeal to you.

Blondie emphasizes vanilla and caramel. Cookie Butter combines three dessert flavors in a breve.

Frequently Asked Questions About the Cookie Butter

What does Cookie Butter taste like at 7 Brew?
The Cookie Butter tastes like warm Biscoff cookie butter spread in coffee form — caramelized, nutty, and smooth. The flavor comes from a combination of toasted marshmallow, hazelnut, and white chocolate syrups that together recreate the speculoos cookie butter taste.
How many calories are in a 7 Brew Cookie Butter?
A medium (24 oz) Cookie Butter breve has approximately 450 calories. Choose the small (16 oz) for around 300 calories, or reduce further by swapping to oat milk or almond milk and requesting sugar-free syrups.
Is Cookie Butter a signature drink or a secret menu item?
Cookie Butter is one of 7 Brew's official signature drinks — it is on the standard menu board at every drive-thru location. It gained extra popularity after going viral on TikTok, but it has always been part of the core lineup.
